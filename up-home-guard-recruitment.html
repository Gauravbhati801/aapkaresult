Department: Uttar Pradesh Home Guard Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh Post Name: Home Guard Volunteer (Rural & Urban Companies) Status: Recruitment Notification Out

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Physical Efficiency Test (PET - 1500m race for male; 400m race for female)
  2. Stage 2: Physical Measurement Test (PMT - Height and Chest measurements)
  3. Stage 3: Educational Marks & Bonus Points Evaluation (NCC A/B/C, ITI, Driving License, Sports Certificates)
  4. Stage 4: Document Verification and Medical Fitness Test
  5. Stage 5: District-wise Final Merit List published by District Selection Board

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official UP Home Guard portal at homeguard.up.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Check the district-wise vacancy notification and recruitment schedule for your respective home district.
  3. Step 3: Register online or download the prescribed district application form.
  4. Step 4: Fill in personal, educational, and permanent residential details accurately.
  5. Step 5: Attach or upload self-attested copies of 10th certificate, domicile proof, caste certificate, and special qualification certificates.
  6. Step 6: Submit the application within the prescribed deadline for your district.
  7. Step 7: Keep your registration acknowledgment receipt ready for physical rally call-up.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th Class (High School) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • Uttar Pradesh Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra)
  • Caste Certificate (OBC, SC, ST) issued by competent UP authority
  • Character Certificate (Charitra Praman Patra) issued by Police Station / Gazetted Officer
  • Aadhaar Card or Voter ID card
  • Special Qualification Certificates (NCC Certificate, ITI Diploma, Driving License, National/State Sports Certificate)
  • Passport size photographs (minimum 6 copies)

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates must be residents of the district where they are applying.
  • Home Guard volunteers are deployed for law & order, traffic management, elections, and VIP security duties across Uttar Pradesh.
  • Running marks play a decisive role in the final merit; ensure regular practice before the rally date.
  • No candidate with a criminal record or pending FIR is eligible for appointment.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the age limit for UP Home Guard Bharti?

The age limit for UP Home Guard recruitment is 18 to 45 years.

Q2. What is the daily honorarium paid to UP Home Guards?

UP Home Guards are paid a daily duty honorarium of ₹786 per day plus dearness allowance, which works out to approximately ₹23,500 to ₹26,000 per month during active duty call-up.

Q3. Is there a written exam for UP Home Guard?

No. The selection is based on Physical Efficiency Test (Race), Physical Standards (Height & Chest), Educational qualification weightage, and bonus points for NCC, ITI, or Sports certificates.
