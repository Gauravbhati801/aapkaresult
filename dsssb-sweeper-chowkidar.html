DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 25 Posts (Advt 05/2026)

Organization: Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) Department: District & Sessions Courts, Delhi Status: Notification Out • Apply from 13 Oct
Share Alert:
Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB), Government of NCT of Delhi: DSSSB has released the official recruitment notification under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 25 vacancies of Sweeper / Safai Karamchari (Post Code 46/26) and Chowkidar (Post Code 47/26) in the District & Sessions Courts, Delhi. Candidates who have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) from any recognized board in India are eligible to apply online between 13 October 2026 (12:00 Noon) and 11 November 2026 (11:00 PM) at dsssbonline.nic.in.
Total Vacancies 25 Posts
Qualification 10th Pass (Matriculation) from recognized Board
Age Limit 18 to 27 Years (as on 01 June 2026)
Pay Scale Pay Matrix Level-1 (₹18,000 - ₹56,900)
Application Fee ₹100 (Gen/OBC/EWS) | ₹0 (SC/ST/Women)
Application Mode Online only via dsssbonline.nic.in

  • General / OBC / EWS Male: ₹100/-

Minimum Age: 18 Years  |  Maximum Age: 27 Years.

• SC / ST: 5 Years relaxation (Up to 32 Years)

• Departmental Candidates / Ex-Servicemen: As per Government of NCT of Delhi / Court Service rules.

📝 How to Fill DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Online Form 2026

  1. Step 1 (OARS Registration): Visit the official DSSSB portal dsssbonline.nic.in. If applying for the first time, complete the One-Time Registration (OARS) using your Class 10th Roll Number, Passing Year, and Aadhaar Card.
  2. Step 2 (Login & Apply): Login using your Registration credentials (DOB, 10th Roll No, 10th Passing Year, and Password). Click on Apply Online and select Advertisement No. 05/2026.
  3. Step 3 (Select Post): Choose Post Code 46/26 (Sweeper) or 47/26 (Chowkidar). Fill in your personal details, residential address, and educational qualification.
  4. Step 4 (Upload Photo & Signature): Upload your postcard-size recent photograph, signature, and left/right thumb impressions strictly per DSSSB pixel specifications.
  5. Step 5 (Fee Payment): Pay the ₹100/- application fee online via SBI e-pay (Exempted for Women/SC/ST/PwBD).
  6. Step 6 (Print Application): Preview and submit the form finally. Download and print the confirmation page for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for DSSSB Sweeper and Chowkidar recruitment 2026?

Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th class) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

Q2. What are the starting and last dates to apply for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar 2026?

The online application portal opens on 13 October 2026 (12:00 Noon) and closes on 11 November 2026 (11:00 PM).

Q3. What is the age limit for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar posts?

The candidate's age must be between 18 and 27 years as of 01 June 2026. Reserved category candidates (OBC/SC/ST/PwBD) receive relaxation as per Delhi government rules.

Q4. What is the selection process for these posts?

The selection consists of two stages: (1) An Objective Multiple Choice Written Examination of 100 marks (2 hours duration), followed by (2) an Interview of 15 marks, document verification, and medical fitness.

Q5. What is the application fee for DSSSB recruitment?

General, OBC, and EWS male candidates have to pay ₹100/-. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and all Women candidates are exempted from payment of fees (₹0/-).
