DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 25 Posts (Advt 05/2026)

Organization: Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) Department: District & Sessions Courts, Delhi Status: Notification Out • Apply from 13 Oct
Share Alert:
Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB), Govt of NCT of Delhi
DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Recruitment 2026 Online Form
District & Sessions Courts, Delhi (Advt No. 05/2026) : Short Details of Notification
Post Date / Update: 09 October 2026 | 12:00 PM 🔥 APPLY ONLINE (13 OCT TO 11 NOV)

Short Information : Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the official recruitment notification under Advertisement No. 05/2026 for 25 vacancies of Sweeper / Safai Karamchari (Post Code 46/26) and Chowkidar (Post Code 47/26) in the District & Sessions Courts, Delhi. Candidates who have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) from any recognized board in India are eligible to apply online between 13 October 2026 (12:00 Noon) and 11 November 2026 (11:00 PM) at dsssbonline.nic.in.

Important Dates
  • Notification Released : 09/10/2026
  • Application Begin : 13/10/2026 (12:00 Noon)
  • Last Date for Apply Online : 11/11/2026 (11:00 PM)
  • Pay Exam Fee Last Date : 11/11/2026 (11:00 PM)
  • Exam Date : Notify Soon
  • Admit Card Available : Before Exam
  • Interview Schedule : Post Written Evaluation
Application Fee
  • General / OBC / EWS Male : 100/-
  • SC / ST Candidates : 0/- (Exempted)
  • PwBD Candidates : 0/- (Exempted)
  • Ex-Servicemen (ESM) : 0/- (Exempted)
  • All Category Female : 0/- (Exempted)
  • Payment Mode : Pay the Examination Fee Through Online via SBI e-pay Gateway (Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI) Only.
DSSSB Advt 05/2026 : Age Limit as on 01/06/2026
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years.
  • Maximum Age : 27 Years.
  • Candidate must not have been born earlier than 02/06/1999 and not later than 01/06/2008.
  • Age Relaxation Extra as per Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) Advt 05/2026 Rules (5 Years for SC/ST, 3 Years for OBC Delhi, 10 Years for PwBD).

How to Fill DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Online Form 2026
  • Visit official DSSSB recruitment portal dsssbonline.nic.in.
  • Candidate must register under One-Time Registration System (OARS) using Class 10th Roll No, Passing Year, and Aadhaar Card.
  • Login to your profile, click on Apply Online and select Advertisement No. 05/2026.
  • Select Post Code 46/26 (Sweeper) or 47/26 (Chowkidar). Fill in personal details, address, and qualification.
  • Upload recent postcard-size photograph, digital signature, and thumb impressions as per DSSSB pixel guidelines.
  • Pay application fee of ₹100/- online via SBI e-pay (Exempted for Women, SC, ST, PwBD).
  • Check application preview thoroughly before final submit and take a printout of the confirmation page.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for DSSSB Sweeper and Chowkidar recruitment 2026?

Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th class) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

Q2. What are the starting and last dates to apply for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar 2026?

The online application portal opens on 13 October 2026 (12:00 Noon) and closes on 11 November 2026 (11:00 PM).

Q3. What is the age limit for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar posts?

The candidate's age must be between 18 and 27 years as of 01 June 2026. Reserved category candidates (OBC/SC/ST/PwBD) receive relaxation as per Delhi government rules.

Q4. What is the selection process for these posts?

The selection consists of two stages: (1) An Objective Multiple Choice Written Examination of 100 marks (2 hours duration), followed by (2) an Interview of 15 marks, document verification, and medical fitness.

Q5. What is the application fee for DSSSB recruitment?

General, OBC, and EWS male candidates have to pay ₹100/-. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and all Women candidates are exempted from payment of fees (₹0/-).
