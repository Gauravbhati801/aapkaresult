How to Fill DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar Online Form 2026
  • Visit official DSSSB recruitment portal dsssbonline.nic.in.
  • Candidate must register under One-Time Registration System (OARS) using Class 10th Roll No, Passing Year, and Aadhaar Card.
  • Login to your profile, click on Apply Online and select Advertisement No. 05/2026.
  • Select Post Code 46/26 (Sweeper) or 47/26 (Chowkidar). Fill in personal details, address, and qualification.
  • Upload recent postcard-size photograph, digital signature, and thumb impressions as per DSSSB pixel guidelines.
  • Pay application fee of ₹100/- online via SBI e-pay (Exempted for Women, SC, ST, PwBD).
  • Check application preview thoroughly before final submit and take a printout of the confirmation page.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for DSSSB Sweeper and Chowkidar recruitment 2026?

Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th class) or equivalent examination from a recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

Q2. What are the starting and last dates to apply for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar 2026?

The online application portal opens on 13 October 2026 (12:00 Noon) and closes on 11 November 2026 (11:00 PM).

Q3. What is the age limit for DSSSB Sweeper & Chowkidar posts?

The candidate's age must be between 18 and 27 years as of 01 June 2026. Reserved category candidates (OBC/SC/ST/PwBD) receive relaxation as per Delhi government rules.

Q4. What is the selection process for these posts?

The selection consists of two stages: (1) An Objective Multiple Choice Written Examination of 100 marks (2 hours duration), followed by (2) an Interview of 15 marks, document verification, and medical fitness.

Q5. What is the application fee for DSSSB recruitment?

General, OBC, and EWS male candidates have to pay ₹100/-. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and all Women candidates are exempted from payment of fees (₹0/-).

Editorial Deep-Dive: DSSSB Sweeper, Chowkidar & Peon (District Courts)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Selected staff are appointed in Delhi District Courts and Family Courts across Tis Hazari, Patiala House, Karkardooma, Rohini, Dwarka, and Saket complexes. Duties include courtroom file management, premises security, maintaining sanitation standards, assisting judicial officers, and delivering judicial summons within designated court jurisdictions.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Recruited under Pay Matrix Level-1 (Basic Pay ₹18,000 – ₹56,900). With 30% Delhi X-class HRA (₹5,400), 50% DA (₹9,000), and Transport Allowance (₹2,025), gross monthly salary is ₹34,425. Court staff also receive medical allowances and court uniform allowances. Net in-hand monthly salary credited to bank accounts is approximately ₹31,000.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

The Tier-1 Computer-Based Test consists of 100 objective questions for 100 marks. For Unreserved candidates, previous judicial recruitment cutoffs ranged between 74 and 80 marks. For OBC (Delhi), safe target is 68–74 marks, while SC/ST candidates need 62+ marks. Driving test or physical endurance evaluation may apply for specific chowkidar posts.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The test covers General Knowledge (25 Qs), English (25 Qs), Hindi (25 Qs), and Basic Mathematics (25 Qs). Maintain high speed and zero guesswork to counter the 0.25 negative marking. Hindi and basic mathematics questions are matriculation standard; focus on arithmetic fundamentals (profit-loss, average, time-work, percentages).

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Carefully verify your application details against your Class 10th marksheet. Driving license (LMV) is advantageous for messenger posts. Candidates must produce clear character certificates and undergo police background verification prior to joining judicial premises.
