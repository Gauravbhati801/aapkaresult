11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Phase 1: Online Common Entrance Examination (CEE) conducted across designated exam centers
  2. Phase 2: Recruitment Rally: Physical Fitness Test (PFT) & Physical Measurement Test (PMT)
  3. Phase 3: Adaptability Test (Psychological & Behavioral suitability assessment)
  4. Phase 4: Medical Examination at Rally Site / Military Hospital
  5. Phase 5: Final All-India Merit List and dispatch for military training

13. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

📚 General Knowledge
History (Indian & World), Geography, Terminology (Geographical, Astronomical, Legal), Sports, Awards & Honors, Armed Forces, Continents and Sub-continents, Indian Towns, States & U.Ts, Institutions & Research Stations, International Organizations, Books and Authors, Current Events.
📚 General Science
Fundamentals of Physics, Chemistry and Biology based on everyday observation and experience up to Class 10th standard (Human Body, Food & Nutrition, Diseases & Prevention, Basic Chemical Formulas, Units & Measurements).
📚 Mathematics
Arithmetic (Number Systems, HCF/LCM, Decimals, Square Roots, Percentage, Average, Ratio & Proportion, Profit & Loss), Algebra (Basic Operations, Factorization, Linear Equations), Geometry (Lines, Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Circles), Mensuration (Area, Volume of Cylinder, Cone, Sphere), Trigonometry (Simple identities).
📚 Logical Reasoning
Patterns, Number Series, Alphabet Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Non-verbal series.

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Go to the official Indian Army portal at joinindianarmy.nic.in.
  2. Step 2: Complete the captcha to enter the main website.
  3. Step 3: Under 'Agnipath' menu, click on 'User Registration' / 'Apply Online'.
  4. Step 4: Register with your Aadhaar Number or Matriculation Certificate details.
  5. Step 5: Fill in educational marks and select your trade (GD, Technical, Clerk/SKT, Tradesman).
  6. Step 6: Choose 5 exam cities for the Online Common Entrance Exam (CEE).
  7. Step 7: Pay the CEE fee of ₹250/- online and submit your application.
  8. Step 8: Download the application form and keep track of your CEE Admit Card.

  • NCC A/B/C Certificate / Sports Certificate (if claiming bonus marks)

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidate must be UNMARRIED male/female citizen of India. Marriage during the 4-year Agniveer engagement is strictly prohibited.
  • Tattoos: Permanent body tattoos are permitted only on the inner face of the forearm. Tribal candidates with traditional markings are permitted on case basis.
  • Up to 25% of each Agniveer batch will be enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Army based on merit and performance.
  • Drug screening is conducted during the physical rally; use of performance-enhancing drugs will lead to immediate police action.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the age limit for Indian Army Agniveer?

The age limit for all Agniveer categories (GD, Technical, Office Assistant/Clerk, Tradesmen) is strictly 17.5 to 21 years.

Q2. What is the Seva Nidhi Package awarded after 4 years?

Upon completion of 4 years of service, Agniveers receive an accumulated Seva Nidhi Package of approximately ₹10.04 Lakhs (inclusive of accrued interest), which is 100% exempt from income tax, along with an Agniveer Skill Certificate.

Q3. What percentage of Agniveers will be absorbed permanently in the Indian Army?

Up to 25% of Agniveers from each batch will be selected and enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Army as permanent soldiers based on centralized screening and performance.
