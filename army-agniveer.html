26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the age limit for Indian Army Agniveer?

The age limit for all Agniveer categories (GD, Technical, Office Assistant/Clerk, Tradesmen) is strictly 17.5 to 21 years.

Q2. What is the Seva Nidhi Package awarded after 4 years?

Upon completion of 4 years of service, Agniveers receive an accumulated Seva Nidhi Package of approximately ₹10.04 Lakhs (inclusive of accrued interest), which is 100% exempt from income tax, along with an Agniveer Skill Certificate.

Q3. What percentage of Agniveers will be absorbed permanently in the Indian Army?

Up to 25% of Agniveers from each batch will be selected and enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Army as permanent soldiers based on centralized screening and performance.
