UP Scholarship 2026-27 : Mandatory Guidelines & Aadhaar e-KYC
  • Aadhaar Active Mobile Linking: Candidate's Aadhaar Card must have active mobile number linked for OTP e-KYC verification.
  • Bank Account NPCI DBT Seeding: Bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar in NPCI mapper. Scholarship funds will only be disbursed through PFMS DBT.
  • Name & DOB Match: Student's Name, Father's Name, and Date of Birth in High School marksheet and Aadhaar card must match 100%.
  • Annual Family Income Limits: General / OBC / Minority: Max ₹2,00,000/- per annum | SC / ST: Max ₹2,50,000/- per annum.

How to Apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27 Online Form
  1. Visit the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in or click on the direct registration links given below.
  2. Click on Student → Registration. Choose your category (General / OBC / SC / ST / Minority) and Stage (Pre-Matric / Post-Matric / Dashmottar).
  3. Select Fresh (for new admission) or Renewal (for ongoing multi-year courses).
  4. Complete Aadhaar Authentication & DigiLocker verification via mobile OTP.
  5. Fill educational details, family income details, caste certificate numbers, and fee details carefully.
  6. Upload photograph/documents if required. Print the Draft Copy (शिक्षण संस्थान में जमा करने हेतु नहीं) for checking by your institute.
  7. After corrections, click on Lock / Final Submit to generate the Final Application Form.
  8. Submit the printed hard copy along with copies of all required certificates to your school/college within 3 working days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Who is eligible to apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27?

Permanent residents of Uttar Pradesh studying in recognized schools, colleges, or universities in Class 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, or undergraduate/postgraduate/diploma courses (BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, Polytechnic, ITI, B.Ed, etc.) with annual family income within the prescribed limit can apply.

Q2. What is the annual family income limit for UP Scholarship?

For General, OBC, and Minority category students, the annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum. For Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) students, the maximum annual income limit is ₹2,50,000/-.

Q3. Is Aadhaar NPCI DBT linking mandatory for receiving UP Scholarship?

Yes, Aadhaar-based NPCI DBT mapping in your active bank account is strictly mandatory. The scholarship amount is disbursed through PFMS via Aadhaar Payment Bridge. If your bank account is not seeded with NPCI, the funds will be rejected.

Q4. What is the difference between Fresh and Renewal scholarship application?

Fresh registration is for students entering a new course or school/college for the first time. Renewal application is for students who already received or applied for scholarship in the previous year of the same ongoing multi-year course (e.g. 2nd or 3rd year).

Q5. How can I check my UP Scholarship application status online?

Students can track their live status by logging into scholarship.up.gov.in using their Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Password under the 'Status' tab, or verify PFMS DBT payment status on pfms.nic.in.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) disbursal: Post-Matric professional courses (B.Tech, B.Ed, MBBS, Diploma) receive full non-refundable fee reimbursements ranging from ₹30,000 to ₹1,20,000 per academic year, along with annual maintenance allowances of ₹6,000 to ₹12,000 credited directly into the student’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Eligibility depends on family annual income thresholds: for General, OBC, and Minority categories, family annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,00,000; for SC and ST students, the ceiling is ₹2,50,000. Additionally, colleges must verify that the student maintains at least 75% biometric attendance throughout the academic term.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The critical factor for successful disbursement is zero mismatch between Aadhaar data and institutional admission registers. Ensure that your Aadhaar number is active, linked to your current mobile number, and officially seeded (Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI Mapping) with your active core banking savings account.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Track scholarship status regularly using the UP Scholarship portal dashboard. If the status reflects "Suspect Data", "Bank Name mismatch", or "Enrollment Number not matched with University data", submit an application with supporting marksheets to your college scholarship nodal officer during the official correction window.
