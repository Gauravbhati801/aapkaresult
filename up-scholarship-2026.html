UP Scholarship 2026-27 Online Form: Pre & Post Matric Registration, Renewal, Status & Login

Authority: Social Welfare Department (समाज कल्याण विभाग), Uttar Pradesh Scheme: UP Chhatravritti evam Shulk Pratipoorti Yojana 2026-27 Status: Online Application Active
Share Alert:
Government of Uttar Pradesh (Social Welfare, Backward Class & Minority Welfare Departments): The online application and renewal process for the Uttar Pradesh Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement Scheme 2026-2027 is currently active. Eligible candidates studying in Class 9th, 10th (Pre-Matric), Class 11th, 12th (Post-Matric Inter), and Dashmottar courses (UG, PG, B.Tech, Polytechnic, ITI, B.Ed, D.El.Ed, Nursing, Pharmacy) can submit their online registration, complete Aadhaar e-KYC, and submit required documents through the official scholarship portal scholarship.up.gov.in.

  • Payment Mode: No application fee or examination fee is payable for UP Scholarship. Registration is 100% Free.
💰 Annual Family Income Eligibility Criteria
General, OBC & Minority Candidates: The total annual income of the parents/guardian from all verifiable sources must not exceed ₹2,00,000/- (Two Lakh Rupees) per annum.
Scheduled Caste (SC) & Scheduled Tribe (ST) Candidates: The total annual income of the family must not exceed ₹2,50,000/- (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees) per annum.
Validity: Income certificate (आय प्रमाण पत्र) must be digitally verified and issued by an authorized Tehsildar of Uttar Pradesh.

⚠️ Mandatory Guidelines: Aadhaar e-KYC & Bank NPCI Seeding

Important Alert for all Fresh & Renewal Applicants:

  • Aadhaar Demographic & OTP Authentication: Your mobile number must be actively linked with your Aadhaar Card. The OTP will be sent to the Aadhaar-registered mobile number during registration and e-KYC.
  • Bank Account NPCI DBT Mapping: Scholarship funds are transferred strictly through PFMS Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB). Your bank account must be mapped with NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). Inactive or unseeded accounts will result in transaction failure.
  • Single Active Registration: A student cannot apply from multiple institutes simultaneously.
  • Non-Refundable Fee: Fill the exact annual Non-Refundable Fee as prescribed and approved for your college/course.

🚀 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27

  1. Step 1 (Registration): Visit the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in. Navigate to Student → Registration. Choose your category (General / OBC / SC / ST / Minority) and course stage (Pre-Matric, Post-Matric Inter, or Dashmottar Other).
  2. Step 2 (Aadhaar Authentication): Complete the Aadhaar Demographic e-KYC by entering your Aadhaar number and the OTP received on your mobile.
  3. Step 3 (Fill Application Form): Login using your newly generated Registration Number and Password. Enter your academic qualifications, college admission date, enrollment number, and annual fee details.
  4. Step 4 (Upload Photo & Certificates): Upload your passport photograph and verify your digital income and caste certificates.
  5. Step 5 (Scrutiny / Print for College Check): Generate the preliminary draft printout. Verify all entered marks, name spelling, father's name, and fee amount carefully with your college department.
  6. Step 6 (Final Submission & College Hard Copy): Submit the application finally. Take the final 3-page hard copy printout, attach photocopies of all required marksheets, fee receipts, caste/income certificates, and submit it to your institute within the deadline.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Who is eligible to apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27?

Permanent residents of Uttar Pradesh studying in recognized schools, colleges, or universities in Class 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, or undergraduate/postgraduate/diploma courses (BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, Polytechnic, ITI, B.Ed, etc.) with annual family income within the prescribed limit can apply.

Q2. What is the annual family income limit for UP Scholarship?

For General, OBC, and Minority category students, the annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum. For Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) students, the maximum annual income limit is ₹2,50,000/-.

Q3. Is Aadhaar NPCI DBT linking mandatory for receiving UP Scholarship?

Yes, Aadhaar-based NPCI DBT mapping in your active bank account is strictly mandatory. The scholarship amount is disbursed through PFMS via Aadhaar Payment Bridge. If your bank account is not seeded with NPCI, the funds will be rejected.

Q4. What is the difference between Fresh and Renewal scholarship application?

Fresh registration is for students entering a new course or school/college for the first time. Renewal application is for students who already received or applied for scholarship in the previous year of the same ongoing multi-year course (e.g. 2nd or 3rd year).

Q5. How can I check my UP Scholarship application status online?

Students can track their live status by logging into scholarship.up.gov.in using their Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Password under the 'Status' tab, or verify PFMS DBT payment status on pfms.nic.in.
