Editorial Deep-Dive: Indian Army Agniveer General Duty & Technical

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Agniveer soldiers form the core fighting force of the Indian Army across infantry regiments, artillery divisions, armored corps, and signal units. Recruits undergo 6 months of grueling combat and physical training followed by 3.5 years of active military operational deployment along high-altitude border posts, counter-insurgency grids, and mechanized combat zones. Outstanding performers (up to 25%) are absorbed into the regular permanent cadre.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Under the Seva Nidhi Package: Year 1 stipend is ₹30,000/month (In-hand ₹21,000, Seva Nidhi ₹9,000); Year 2: ₹33,000/month (In-hand ₹23,100); Year 3: ₹36,500/month (In-hand ₹25,550); Year 4: ₹40,000/month (In-hand ₹28,000). Upon completing 4 years, candidates receive an income-tax-free Seva Nidhi package of ~₹11.71 Lakh with accrued interest, plus ₹48 Lakh non-contributory life insurance cover.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

The Online Common Entrance Exam (CEE) requires scoring at least 35% to 40% qualifying marks, but final rally merit combines CEE marks with Physical Fitness Test (PFT) scores for GD. Scoring Group-1 in the 1.6 km run (60 marks) and 10 pull-ups (40 marks) yields a maximum 100 physical marks, which makes selection nearly certain even with moderate CEE scores.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

CEE consists of 50 questions (100 marks) in General Knowledge, General Science, and Elementary Mathematics. Focus on basic factual science (human body, diseases, chemical formulas, motion laws) and Indian history and defense awards. Daily physical training is paramount: run 5 km alternate days and practice pull-ups on an overhead horizontal bar with dead-hang precision.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Army rally physical verification is uncompromising: 1.6 km run in under 5 minutes 30 seconds for Group-1 (60 marks) or 5 mins 31 secs to 5 mins 45 secs for Group-2 (48 marks). Tattoos are permitted only on the inner face of the forearms. Carry original unmarried certificate, character certificate from village sarpanch/police, and NCC/Sports certificates to claim bonus marks.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the age limit for Indian Army Agniveer?

The age limit for all Agniveer categories (GD, Technical, Office Assistant/Clerk, Tradesmen) is strictly 17.5 to 21 years.

Q2. What is the Seva Nidhi Package awarded after 4 years?

Upon completion of 4 years of service, Agniveers receive an accumulated Seva Nidhi Package of approximately ₹10.04 Lakhs (inclusive of accrued interest), which is 100% exempt from income tax, along with an Agniveer Skill Certificate.

Q3. What percentage of Agniveers will be absorbed permanently in the Indian Army?

Up to 25% of Agniveers from each batch will be selected and enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Army as permanent soldiers based on centralized screening and performance.
