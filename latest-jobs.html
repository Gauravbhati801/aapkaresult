Step-by-Step Guide to Applying for Government Jobs Online

Applying for Central or State Government employment requires thorough preparation to avoid common application rejections. Follow these systematic stages for an error-free online submission:

  1. Thorough Notification Scrutiny: Before initiating registration, download and carefully review the official Employment Gazette notification PDF. Verify the educational qualification cut-off date, age limits (including category-specific relaxations), and required subject combinations.
  2. One-Time Registration (OTR): Major commissions such as SSC, UPSC, and UPSSSC mandate a centralized One-Time Registration (OTR). Fill your Aadhaar details, matriculation roll number, and father's name exactly as printed on your Class 10th certificate.
  3. Digital Document Optimization: Digitize and compress your recent passport-size photograph (with white or light background, taken within 90 days), signature, and category certificates (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST, PwBD) according to specified pixel dimensions and file size limits (usually 20 KB to 50 KB in JPEG format).
  4. Accurate Form Filling & Preview: Complete the post preference, examination city preference, and educational qualification fields. Utilize the "Preview" button to cross-check every single character before proceeding to the final lock.
  5. Online Fee Submission & Final Confirmation: Complete the mandatory application fee transaction via Net Banking, Debit Card, or UPI gateway. Always download and store at least three printed copies of the final submitted Application Confirmation Page and payment receipt for future Document Verification.

Frequently Asked Questions (Latest Jobs 2026)

Q1. How frequently are new government job alerts updated on AapkaResult.in?

Our research desk monitors the Employment News (Rozgar Samachar), official state gazettes, and over 120 commission portals daily. New notifications, corrigenda, and application extension notices are published in real-time within minutes of official release.

Q2. Can candidates apply for both Central and State Government jobs simultaneously?

Yes, an eligible candidate may apply for multiple central (SSC, UPSC, Railway, Banking) and state recruitment exams concurrently, provided they fulfill the required age limit, educational qualification, and domicile criteria for the respective commissions.

Q3. What should I do if the server times out during application fee payment?

If money was debited from your bank account or UPI app but the payment status reflects 'Pending' or 'Failed', wait 24 to 48 hours before paying again. Most government payment gateways reconcile bank transactions automatically. If unresolved after 48 hours, raise a grievance on the portal with your transaction reference number.

Q4. What is the difference between Level-1, Level-2, and Level-7 Pay Scales?

Under the 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC), Pay Level-1 represents Basic Pay of ₹18,000 (Group D / MTS), Pay Level-2 represents ₹19,900 (Lower Division Clerks), Pay Level-3 represents ₹21,700 (Constables / Assistants), and Pay Level-7 represents ₹44,900 (Assistant Section Officers / Inspectors). All levels receive additional DA, HRA, and allowances.
