Step-by-Step Guide to Applying for Government Jobs Online

  1. Verify Eligibility: Check age cutoff, educational qualification, and domicile requirement on the official advertisement.
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR): Create your candidate profile with Aadhaar e-KYC on portals like SSC, UPSC, or UPPSC.
  3. Fill the Application Form: Enter academic roll numbers and marks exactly matching your High School Certificate.
  4. Upload Scanned Credentials: Ensure passport photo and signature strictly meet file size requirements (20KB–50KB).
  5. Fee Payment & Submission: Make fee payments only through the official gateway and save the transaction receipt.
  6. Save Confirmation Page: Download and print the finalized application PDF for document verification.
