UPTET Exam 2027 Online Form (Advt. No. 10/2026): Primary & Upper Primary Notification Out

Conducting Body: UPESSC, Prayagraj (UP Education Service Selection Commission) Advt No.: 10/2026 Exam Levels: Paper-I (Class 1–5) & Paper-II (Class 6–8) Status: Online Form & OTR Active (24 Nov to 23 Dec 2026)
Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj
UPTET Exam 2027 Online Form (Primary & Upper Primary Level)
Advertisement No. 10/2026 • Short Details of Official Notification
Commission: UPESSC, Prayagraj (Govt. of Uttar Pradesh) 🔥 ONLINE FORM & OTR ACTIVE (24 NOV TO 23 DEC 2026)

Short Information : The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj has officially issued the short notification (Advt. No. 10/2026, dated 18 November 2026) for the Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET) 2027 for Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5) and Upper Primary Level (Classes 6 to 8). The detailed information brochure and online registration link (with mandatory One Time Registration – OTR) will go live on 24 November 2026 at upessc.up.gov.in and remain open until 23 December 2026. The offline OMR-based UPTET 2027 examination is scheduled to be conducted on 26 & 27 February 2027. Check complete schedule, eligibility criteria, application fee, Paper-I & Paper-II exam pattern, qualifying marks, and official notice PDF link below.

Important Dates
  • Short Notice Released : 18 November 2026
  • Detailed Notification & Form Start : 24 November 2026
  • Last Date for Registration & Fee : 23 December 2026
  • Application Correction Last Date : 27 December 2026
  • UPTET 2027 Exam Dates : 26 & 27 February 2027
  • Admit Card Release : Before Exam (Feb 2027)
  • Answer Key & Result : Notified After Exam
Application Fee (Previous Cycle Reference – Final Advt. 10/2026 Fee Matrix on 24 Nov)
  • General / OBC / EWS (Single Paper) : ₹1,000/-
  • SC / ST (Single Paper) : ₹500/-
  • PH / PwBD / Divyang : ₹300/-
  • Both Papers (Paper I & II) : As per Detailed Advt. 10/2026
  • Payment Mode : Online (Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI)
  • Note : Verify final single & dual paper fee on 24 Nov 2026 brochure.
UPTET 2027: Age Limit, Qualifying Marks & Certificate Validity
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years (as per NCTE / State Eligibility Rules).
  • Maximum Age : No Upper Age Limit to appear in the UPTET Teacher Eligibility Test.
  • Qualifying Marks (General / Unreserved) : Minimum 60% Marks (90 out of 150 Marks).
  • Qualifying Marks (SC / ST / OBC / PwBD / Ex-Servicemen) : Minimum 55% Marks (82 out of 150 Marks).
  • Certificate Validity : Lifetime Validity – Qualified candidates remain eligible for future UP Primary & Junior Assistant Teacher (Super TET) recruitments.
UPTET 2027 Educational Eligibility Criteria (Paper-I & Paper-II)
Exam Paper & Level Required Educational Qualification (NCTE & UPESSC Norms)
Paper-I (Primary Level)
Classes 1 to 5
  • Bachelor’s Degree in any stream with passed or appearing in final year of 2-Year D.El.Ed (BTC) / Special BTC, OR
  • 10+2 (Senior Secondary) with minimum 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 4-Year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed), OR
  • 10+2 with 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 2-Year Diploma in Education (Special Education) recognized by RCI.
  • Candidates must verify the final qualification clauses in the detailed Advt. No. 10/2026 PDF releasing on 24 November 2026.
Paper-II (Upper Primary / Junior Level)
Classes 6 to 8
  • Bachelor’s Degree in any stream with passed or appearing in final year of 2-Year D.El.Ed (BTC), OR
  • Bachelor’s Degree with minimum 50% marks (45% as per NCTE norms for reserved categories) and passed or appearing in B.Ed / Special B.Ed, OR
  • 10+2 (Intermediate) with minimum 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 4-Year B.El.Ed / B.A.Ed / B.Sc.Ed / B.A.B.Ed / B.Sc.B.Ed.

How to Fill UPTET 2027 Online Form (Mandatory OTR Process on upessc.up.gov.in)
  1. Visit Official Portal: Open the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission website at upessc.up.gov.in once the link activates on 24 November 2026.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR): Candidates must first generate their OTR Number using Aadhaar/ID proof, active mobile number, email address, and matriculation certificate details.
  3. Select Exam Level: Log in with your OTR credentials and choose Paper-I (Primary), Paper-II (Upper Primary), or Both Papers along with your Language-II choice (English/Urdu/Sanskrit).
  4. Upload Documents: Upload a clear recent passport-size photograph and signature in the prescribed pixel and KB format (use our free Photo Resizer tool below if needed).
  5. Fee Payment & Final Print: Pay the examination fee online before 23 December 2026. Review all details carefully (correction window open till 27 December 2026) and save a PDF print of your final submitted form.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – UPTET Exam 2027

Q1. When will the UPTET Exam 2027 online form start and close?

As per UPESSC’s official press notice (Advt. No. 10/2026) released on 18 November 2026, online registration and detailed notification release begin on 24 November 2026, and the last date to apply and pay fees is 23 December 2026. Corrections are permitted until 27 December 2026.

Q2. What are the official exam dates for UPTET 2027?

The UPESSC Prayagraj has officially scheduled the UPTET 2027 offline examination (Paper-I Primary and Paper-II Upper Primary) on 26 February 2027 and 27 February 2027.

Q3. Is One Time Registration (OTR) compulsory before filling out the UPTET 2027 form?

Yes, candidates must complete their One Time Registration (OTR) on upessc.up.gov.in before submitting the UPTET 2027 application form.

Q4. Is there any negative marking in UPTET 2027, and how long is the certificate valid?

There is no negative marking in either Paper-I or Paper-II. Candidates securing 90+ marks (General) or 82+ marks (OBC/SC/ST/PwBD) out of 150 receive a UPTET Eligibility Certificate with Lifetime Validity.
