11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Single Stage Computer Based Test (CBT - 100 Marks)
  2. Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET - Qualifying Weight Lifting and Running)
  3. Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) in 1:1 ratio based on normalized CBT score
  4. Stage 4: Comprehensive Medical Examination conducted at Railway Hospitals

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the centralized Railway Recruitment portal (rrbapply.gov.in) or your regional RRB portal.
  2. Step 2: Click on 'Create an Account' to register your basic details (Mobile, Email, Aadhaar).
  3. Step 3: Fill in your educational qualifications and select your ITI / NAC trade (if applicable).
  4. Step 4: Select your desired Railway Zone and fill in post priorities in order of preference.
  5. Step 5: Upload scanned photograph (white background, 35mm x 45mm, 30KB - 70KB) and signature (30KB - 70KB).
  6. Step 6: Pay the examination fee (₹500 / ₹250) online and enter your Bank Account details for the post-CBT fee refund.
  7. Step 7: Preview the complete form and submit. Save the Registration Slip with application number.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th Class (Matriculation) Certificate & Marksheet
  • NCVT / SCVT ITI Certificate or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar Card (Aadhaar authentication is mandatory in RRB examinations)
  • Bank Account details (Account Number, Account Holder Name, and IFSC Code) for fee refund
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) in Central Railway format for free sleeper rail travel pass
  • Recent passport-size color photograph with white background
  • Clear scanned signature on white paper

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates can apply to ONLY ONE Railway Zone. Multiple applications across different RRB zones will result in rejection of all applications.
  • Fee refund (₹400 for Gen/OBC; ₹250 for reserved) is credited ONLY after the candidate actually appears for the CBT examination.
  • Aadhaar-based biometric authentication is carried out at the examination center prior to entry into the testing lab.
  • Negative marking of 1/3rd applies to each incorrect answer; avoid random guessing in CBT.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is ITI compulsory for all posts in Railway RRB Group D?

As per the latest Railway Board notifications, candidates with 10th pass OR 10th + ITI / National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) are eligible. Certain technical department posts require ITI/NAC, while non-technical posts such as Pointsman accept 10th pass.

Q2. How much examination fee is refunded in RRB Group D?

For General/OBC/EWS candidates who appear in CBT-1, ₹400 out of ₹500 is refunded to their bank account (after deducting bank charges). For SC, ST, Female, and PwBD candidates, the full ₹250 fee is refunded.

Q3. Can I apply to multiple RRB zones?

No. A candidate can submit an application to only ONE Railway Zone. Submitting multiple applications to different RRBs leads to cancellation of candidature.

Q4. What is the physical running requirement in RRB Group D?

Male candidates must run 1000 meters in 4 minutes 15 seconds, and carry 35 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes. Female candidates must run 1000 meters in 5 minutes 40 seconds, and carry 20 kg weight for 100 meters in 2 minutes.
