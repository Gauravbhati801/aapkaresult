CBSE Board Date Sheet 2027 (Time Table Out) & Class 10th, 12th Result Check Online

Board: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi Category: Date Sheet 2027 / Time Table & Results Status: Officially Released on cbse.gov.in
Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi
CBSE Class 10th (Secondary) & Class 12th (Senior School) Date Sheet 2027 & Result
CBSE All India Secondary & Senior Secondary Examination 2026–2027 Schedule & Scorecard
Exam Authority: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi 📢 DATE SHEET 2027 RELEASED • EXAMS FROM 16 FEB 2027

Short Information : The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi has officially released the complete subject-wise Date Sheet / Time Table for the Annual Board Examinations 2027 for Class 10th and Class 12th on its official portal cbse.gov.in. Both Class 10 and Class 12 theory examinations will begin on 16 February 2027 (Tuesday) at 10:30 AM (IST). CBSE released the timetable roughly 100 days in advance covering more than 40,000 unique subject combinations to ensure zero timetable clashes. Candidates can view the full subject routine, practical exam dates, guidelines, and access official scorecard links below.

Important Dates & Schedule 2027
  • Date Sheet Release Date : 06 November 2026
  • Winter-Bound Practical Exams : 09 Nov to 19 Dec 2026
  • Regular Schools Practical Exams : 01 Jan to 13 Feb 2027
  • Class 10th Theory Exams : 16 Feb 2027 to 09 Mar 2027
  • Class 12th Theory Exams : 16 Feb 2027 to 09 Apr 2027
  • Daily Exam Shift Timing : 10:30 AM to 01:30 PM (IST)
  • Question Paper Reading Time : 10:15 AM to 10:30 AM
  • CBSE Hall Ticket / Roll No : January 2027
  • CBSE Board Result 2026 : Declared & Available Online

CBSE Exam Day Rules & How to Check Scorecard Online
  1. Center Entry: Candidates must reach their examination center by 09:45 AM. Center gates close strictly at 10:00 AM.
  2. Mandatory Items: Carry original CBSE Admit Card 2027 and School ID Card in proper uniform.
  3. 15-Minute Reading Time: Question papers will be distributed at 10:15 AM for reading only. Writing begins at 10:30 AM.
  4. How to Check Result: Click on Server 1 or Server 2 link below, enter your 8-digit Roll Number, 5-digit School Number, Center Number, and Admit Card ID, then click Submit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – CBSE Date Sheet & Results

Q1. When will CBSE Board Theory Exams 2027 start?

Both Class 10th and Class 12th theory examinations begin on 16 February 2027 (Tuesday) at 10:30 AM.

Q2. What are the concluding dates for Class 10 and 12 examinations?

Class 10 exams will conclude on 09 March 2027, while Class 12 exams will conclude on 09 April 2027.

Q3. When are practical exams held?

Practical exams for regular schools run from 01 January 2027 to 13 February 2027. Winter-bound schools conduct practicals from 09 November to 19 December 2026.

Q4. How can I verify my scorecards or marksheet?

You can check directly on cbseresults.nic.in or results.cbse.nic.in using your Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID, or download digitally signed certificates from DigiLocker.
