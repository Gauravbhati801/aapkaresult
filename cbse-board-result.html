CBSE Board Result 2026: Class 10th & 12th Scorecard, Roll Number Marksheet Check Online

Board: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi Mode: Online Verification Status: Live & Available Online

Exam Authority: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi 🎉 CBSE RESULT 2026 SCORECARD ONLINE

Short Information : The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi has declared the annual board examination results for Class 10th and Class 12th. Over 39 lakh candidates who appeared in the CBSE board examinations can verify their subject-wise theory marks, internal grades, and qualifying status using their Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID.

Important Schedule & Dates
  • CBSE Date Sheet Released : December 2025
  • Class 10th Board Exams : February – March 2026
  • Class 12th Board Exams : February – April 2026
  • Class 10th Result Date : Announced & Live
  • Class 12th Result Date : Announced & Live
  • DigiLocker Marksheet Available : Same Day of Result
  • Verification / Rechecking Form : Within 5 Days of Result
  • Supplementary / Compartment Exam : July 2026
Examination & Student Statistics
  • Total Candidates Enrolled : 39.5+ Lakhs Pan-India
  • Class 10th Total Students : 21.8+ Lakhs
  • Class 12th Total Students : 17.7+ Lakhs
  • Class 10th Pass Percentage : 93.60%
  • Class 12th Pass Percentage : 87.98%
  • Verification Mode : Online by Roll No & School No
Step-by-Step: How to Check Scorecard & Marksheet Online
  1. Click on CBSE Class 10th or Class 12th Server link below.
  2. Enter your 8-digit CBSE Roll Number as printed on your Hall Ticket.
  3. Enter your 5-digit School Number and Center Number.
  4. Enter your Admit Card ID (printed on your original CBSE admit card).
  5. Click Submit to view your digital marksheet and download for admissions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I check my official scorecard?

You can check directly using the official server links given in the Useful Important Links table above, or through the government DigiLocker portal.

Q2. What credentials are required to verify the result?

Keep your official examination Roll Number, Date of Birth, and School/Application Number (printed on your original admit card) ready.

Q3. Is the online marksheet valid for admissions?

Yes. The online scorecard or DigiLocker digitally signed certificate serves as valid provisional proof for admission counselling until the physical certificate is released.
