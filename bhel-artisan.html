Short Information : Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), India's premier engineering and manufacturing enterprise (Maharatna Central PSU), has officially released employment advertisement Advt. No. 01/2026 for recruitment of 660 Artisan Grade-IV positions across 9 key manufacturing plants pan-India including Tiruchirappalli (HPBP), Visakhapatnam (HPVP), Ranipet (BAP), Haridwar (HEEP), Hyderabad (HPEP), Bhopal (HEP), Jhansi (TP), and Bengaluru (EDN & SBD). ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. How many total vacancies are announced in BHEL Artisan Recruitment 2026?

There are a total of 660 posts across 7 technical trades: Fitter (278), Welder (104), Machinist (101), Electrician (90), Turner (52), Electronics Mechanic (23), and Foundryman (12).

Q2. What is the qualification required for BHEL Artisan Grade-IV?

Candidates must possess Class 10th (Matriculation) pass + National Trade Certificate (NTC / ITI) + National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in the relevant trade with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST).

Q3. What is the stipend during training and salary upon regularization?

During the 1-year training period, candidates receive a monthly stipend of ₹24,000/-. Upon successful absorption as Artisan Grade-IV, the regular pay scale is ₹29,500 – ₹65,000/- per month with full DA and allowances.

Candidates must be between 18 and 27 years of age as on 01/10/2026. Age relaxations of 3 years for OBC(NCL), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 to 15 years for PwBD apply as per government rules.

Q5. When is the BHEL Artisan CBT Exam scheduled?

The Computer-Based Examination (CBT) is tentatively scheduled for 11 December 2026 across various examination centers.

Editorial Deep-Dive: BHEL Artisan Grade-IV (WG-IV) Recruitment

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Selected candidates work across major Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) manufacturing plants located at Haridwar, Bhopal, Trichy, Hyderabad, and Ranipet. Duties involve precision metal machining, heavy electrical component fabrication, transformer core winding, high-pressure industrial boiler welding, and operating CNC machine tools under stringent industrial safety standards. Trainees undergo an intensive 1-year training program followed by absorption as regular Artisan Grade-IV with departmental promotional avenues leading to Master Technician and Junior Engineer.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

During the 1-year training period, candidates receive an all-inclusive consolidated stipend of ₹16,000 per month. Upon regular appointment into Grade-IV (Wage Group IV), recruits enter the official pay scale of ₹29,500 – ₹62,000. With current Industrial Dearness Allowance (IDA), 31% cafeteria perks, employer Provident Fund (PF) contribution, and comprehensive medical insurance coverage, the starting gross monthly salary reaches approximately ₹42,500. After statutory deductions, the starting in-hand take-home salary ranges between ₹36,500 and ₹38,200 per month.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Competition is exceptionally intense in the Fitter, Electrician, and Welder trades due to the large pool of ITI certificate holders nationwide. Historical PSU recruitment trends indicate that General (UR) category candidates need a safe target raw score of 76 to 82 marks out of 100 in the trade-specific theory section. For OBC and EWS candidates, safe cut-offs hover around 71 to 76 marks, while SC and ST aspirants should aim for 64+ marks to secure merit ranking.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The Computer-Based Examination allocates 70% weightage directly to Trade Theory and 30% to General Aptitude, Reasoning, and Basic Technical Mathematics. Candidates must thoroughly master the standard NCVT/DGT semester textbooks, engineering drawing symbols, precision measurement instruments like micrometers and vernier callipers, and workshop safety regulations. Dedicate the last 3 weeks exclusively to practicing previous years technical question papers from other heavy engineering PSUs such as SAIL, GAIL, and NTPC.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Ensure that your National Trade Certificate (NTC) or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) in the relevant trade is officially recognized by NCVT/SCVT. Candidates belonging to OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and EWS categories must ensure their category and income certificates are issued within the valid financial year as mandated in the official notification. Keep original apprentice completion logbooks and trade marksheets ready for document verification.
