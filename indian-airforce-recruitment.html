Department: Indian Air Force (IAF), Ministry of Defence, Government of India Post Name: Agniveer Vayu (Intake 01/2026 - 01/2027) - Science & Non-Science Streams Status: Application Closed (Ended 26 July 2026) • Exam Stage Completed / Phase 2 Awaited

Airforce Agniveer Vayu (Science & Other Than Science) : Short Details of Notification
Post Date / Update: 08 July 2026 | 11:00 AM 🎉 3,500+ Posts • Male & Female

Short Information : Indian Air Force (IAF) invites online applications from unmarried Indian male and female candidates for selection test as Agniveervayu under Agnipath Scheme for Intake 01/2026 and 02/2027. Selection consists of Phase-1 Online Test, Phase-2 Physical PFT & Adaptability Tests, and Phase-3 Medical Exam. Check eligibility criteria, salary package, and direct links below.

Important Dates
  • Notification Released : Intake 02/2027
  • Application Begin : July 2026
  • Last Date for Apply Online : August 2026
  • Phase-1 Online Exam Date : October / November 2026
  • Admit Card Available : 24-48 Hours Before Exam
  • Phase-2 Testing Schedule : January 2027
Application Fee
  • Examination Fee (All Candidates) : 250/- + 18% GST (approx 295/-)
  • All Categories Male & Female : 295/-
  • Payment Mode : Pay the Examination Fee Online using Debit Card, Credit Card, or Internet Banking via payment gateway.
Indian Airforce Agniveer Vayu : Age Limit
  • Minimum Age : 17.5 Years.
  • Maximum Age : 21 Years.
  • Candidate must be born between prescribed cutoff dates as announced in official intake notification.

Category / StreamTotal PostEducational Qualification Requirement
Agniveer Vayu (Science Subjects) 2,000+
  • 10+2 Intermediate with Mathematics, Physics & English with minimum 50% Marks in aggregate and 50% in English.
  • OR Three Years Diploma in Engineering (Mech / Elect / Electronics / Auto / IT) with 50% Marks.
Agniveer Vayu (Other Than Science Subjects) 1,500+
  • Passed 10+2 Intermediate in Any Stream / Discipline from Recognized Board with minimum 50% Marks in aggregate and 50% in English.
How to Fill Indian Airforce Agniveer Vayu Intake 01/2026 & 02/2027 Online Form
  • Visit official Air Force Agnipath portal agnipathvayu.cdac.in.
  • Click on Candidate Login and create new registration using Aadhaar and Email ID.
  • Fill in 10th & 12th educational marks, select stream (Science / Other Than Science / Both).
  • Upload recent color photograph (taken with black slate in front of chest), signature, and thumb impression.
  • Select 5 exam city preferences for Phase-1 Online Examination.
  • Pay online exam fee of ₹295/- and print confirmation page.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can female candidates apply for Indian Airforce Agniveer Vayu?

Yes, unmarried female candidates are fully eligible to apply for Indian Air Force Agniveer Vayu recruitment across both Science and Non-Science streams.

Q2. What is the minimum height required for IAF Agniveer Vayu?

The minimum acceptable height is 152.5 cm for male candidates and 152 cm for female candidates.

Q3. Can Arts or Commerce students apply for Airforce Agniveer?

Yes. Students from Arts, Commerce, or Humanities can apply under the 'Other Than Science Subjects' stream provided they have secured minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English in Class 12th.
