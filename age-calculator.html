How Sarkari Age Calculation & Cut-off Rules Work in 2026

In Indian competitive examinations, calculating your exact chronological age is not simply subtracting your birth year from the current year. Government commissions—including the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB), and State Police Boards (UPPRPB, BPSSC)—rely on strict arithmetic criteria defined in civil service service rules:

1. The Crucial Cut-off Date (कट-ऑफ तिथि)

A candidate's age is calculated up to a fixed official benchmark. Under DoPT regulations, exams held in the second half of the year determine eligibility as of 1st July of the recruitment year. Exams notified in the first half determine eligibility as of 1st January.

2. Gregorian Calendar Precision (Days & Months)

Our tool computes exact elapsed calendar months and residual days, taking into account leap years (29 days in February) and 30/31-day month boundaries. Being over-age or under-age by even 24 hours results in automated software rejection.

3. Reserved Category Upper-Age Concessions

Upper age relaxation is cumulative under statutory rules. For instance, an OBC candidate applying for a post with a 27-year ceiling can apply up to 30 years. SC/ST candidates are granted relaxation up to 32 years.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल)

Q1. What is the official date of birth proof accepted in government recruitments?

Across all Indian government recruitments (SSC, UPSC, Railway, State Police), the Date of Birth recorded in your Matriculation / Class 10th Board Certificate is the sole legally binding proof. Aadhaar cards, voter IDs, or hospital birth slips are not accepted if they conflict with the Class 10th certificate.

Q2. UP Police सिपाही भर्ती में आयु की गणना किस तारीख से की जाती है?

उत्तर प्रदेश पुलिस आरक्षी भर्ती नियमावली के तहत आयु की गणना भर्ती वर्ष की 1 जुलाई (01/07/2026) को आधार मानकर की जाती है। अभ्यर्थी की न्यूनतम आयु 18 वर्ष और अधिकतम आयु पुरुषों के लिए 22 वर्ष तथा महिलाओं के लिए 25 वर्ष निर्धारित है (आरक्षित वर्गों को नियमानुसार छूट सहित)।

Q3. Can an EWS candidate claim age relaxation in SSC or Railway jobs?

No. Under central government norms, Economically Weaker Section (EWS) candidates are eligible for a 10% vertical seat reservation and reduced application fees, but do not receive upper-age relaxation. The age limits for EWS are identical to the General (Unreserved) category.

Q4. How is age relaxation calculated for Ex-Servicemen (भूतपूर्व सैनिक)?

The actual military service rendered is subtracted from the candidate's chronological age as on the crucial cut-off date. If the resulting age does not exceed the prescribed maximum age limit by more than 3 years, the candidate is declared eligible.

Q5. What happens if a candidate is over-age by just 1 day?

Application forms are validated by automated database queries. If a candidate exceeds the maximum age limit by even a single day (e.g. born on 01/07 instead of 02/07), the online application system displays a validation error and blocks form submission.

Q6. क्या दूसरे राज्य के SC/ST/OBC अभ्यर्थी UP या अन्य राज्य की भर्ती में आयु छूट पा सकते हैं?

नहीं! राज्य स्तरीय भर्तियों (जैसे UP Police या BSSC) में आरक्षण और आयु छूट का लाभ केवल उसी राज्य के मूल निवासियों (Domicile Holders) को मिलता है। अन्य सभी राज्यों के अभ्यर्थी अनारक्षित (General) श्रेणी के तहत गिने जाते हैं।

Q7. How does this calculator handle leap years in age calculation?

Our tool evaluates the exact day counts of every intervening month, dynamically adjusting February to 29 days during leap years (e.g., 2024, 2028). This guarantees zero discrepancy compared to government PSC algorithms.
