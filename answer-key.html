Standard Raw Score Calculation Formula

Raw Marks = (Total Correct Answers × Positive Marks) − (Total Incorrect Answers × Negative Penalty)

Example: In an exam of 100 questions (1 mark each, 0.25 negative marking): If you attempted 80 questions with 64 correct and 16 incorrect:
Raw Score = (64 × 1) − (16 × 0.25) = 64 − 4 = 60.00 Marks.

How to Submit Online Objections Against Question Papers

  1. Log in to the official examination objection module using your Roll Number and Password / Date of Birth.
  2. Open your recorded response sheet alongside the provisional master answer key.
  3. Note down the Question ID and Option ID where you believe the official key has an error.
  4. Select the reason for challenge (e.g. Translation Error, Multiple Correct Options, No Option Correct).
  5. Upload documentary evidence (scanned pages from authentic NCERT or university standard textbooks).
  6. Pay the required fee per question through the online payment gateway and retain the transaction receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions (Answer Keys)

Q1. Are objection fees refunded if the challenge is accepted?

Yes. For major recruiting commissions like SSC and Railway RRB, if the subject expert committee verifies that your objection is justified, the challenge fee for that specific question is credited back to your original payment account.

Q2. What is a Final Answer Key?

After reviewing all candidate challenges, subject committees finalize an updated master key known as the "Final Answer Key". Result evaluation is done strictly based on this final key, and no further appeals are accepted.
