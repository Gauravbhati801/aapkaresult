11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Preliminary Written Examination (Objective MCQ - 150 Questions, 600 Marks)
  2. Stage 2: Mains Written Examination (Paper-1 General Hindi qualifying + Paper-2 General Studies)
  3. Stage 3: Typing Test / Computer Proficiency Test (applicable for clerical posts)
  4. Stage 4: Physical Measurement & Endurance Test (applicable only for physical posts)
  5. Stage 5: Counselling and Document Verification at BSSC Office, Patna

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official BSSC application website at bssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Click on '2nd Inter Level Combined Competitive Examination' and complete candidate registration.
  3. Step 3: Enter your personal details, residential address, and Bihar domicile credentials.
  4. Step 4: Enter 10th and 12th marks, roll numbers, board names, and computer/typing certificates (if applicable).
  5. Step 5: Upload your live photograph, scanned passport photograph, and Hindi & English signatures.
  6. Step 6: Pay the fee (₹540 / ₹135) through online payment gateway.
  7. Step 7: Check the preview form carefully and click Final Submit.
  8. Step 8: Download the finalized Application Form and Fee Payment Slip.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th Class (Matriculation) Certificate & Marksheet (for Date of Birth proof)
  • 10+2 (Intermediate) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • Bihar Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra)
  • Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate for BC / EBC candidates issued by Bihar Revenue Officer
  • Caste Certificate for SC / ST candidates
  • EWS Certificate issued by competent Bihar authority
  • Computer Operation & Typing Certificate (for LDC/Panchayat Sachiv posts)
  • Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving License

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • BC and EBC candidates must ensure their Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate is in the prescribed Bihar State format and valid.
  • For the Rajasva Karmchari post, no computer or typing certificate is compulsory; 10+2 pass is the sole eligibility criterion.
  • Candidates can take up to 3 textbooks (one for each section: NCERT/BSEB textbooks) into the Preliminary Examination as per BSSC open-book rules.
  • Negative marking of 1 mark applies for each incorrect answer; each question carries 4 marks.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can candidates outside Bihar apply for BSSC Inter Level Exam?

Yes, candidates from all Indian states can apply for BSSC Inter Level recruitment. However, non-Bihar candidates will be treated under General/Unreserved category and cannot claim domicile reservation benefits.

Q2. Is computer typing certificate mandatory for Rajasva Karmchari?

No. For the post of Rajasva Karmchari (3,559 vacancies), passing 10+2 Intermediate is the only compulsory requirement; computer or typing certificates are not mandatory.

Q3. What is the marking scheme in BSSC Inter Level Prelims?

The exam consists of 150 questions carrying 600 marks. Each correct answer carries 4 marks, and 1 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer.
