Editorial Deep-Dive: Bihar BSSC 2nd Inter Level Combined Exam

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Recruits staff across key administrative cadres in Bihar including Revenue Employee (Rajasva Karamchari), Panchayat Secretary (Panchayat Sachiv), Junior Field Investigator, and Lower Division Clerk (LDC). Revenue Employees handle land mutation records and crop survey verification at circle offices, while Panchayat Secretaries execute rural welfare schemes and gram panchayat audit registers.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Positions fall under Pay Matrix Level-2 (Basic ₹19,900) or Level-3 (Basic ₹21,700). Adding 50% DA, HRA (8%–16%), and Medical Allowance (₹1,000), gross starting monthly salary ranges from ₹33,000 to ₹37,500. Net monthly in-hand take-home salary is approximately ₹29,500 to ₹34,000 depending on rural vs municipal block postings.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

With over 25 lakh applicants competing for 12,199 vacancies, Prelims competition is formidable. The exam has 150 questions (600 marks, 4 marks per correct answer, -1 negative mark). Safe qualifying score targets for Mains are: UR: 420–435 marks (105–109 net correct); OBC/BC: 405–418 marks; EBC: 388–400 marks; SC: 360–375 marks; Women candidates: 370–385 marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The syllabus splits evenly into General Studies (50 questions), General Science & Mathematics (50 questions), and Mental Ability / Reasoning (50 questions). Mental Ability carries 200 marks and can be solved with 95%+ accuracy; solve 50 reasoning questions daily. For General Studies, specialize in Bihar Special history (Champaran, 1857 revolt, Kunwar Singh), Bihar geography, and rivers.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Typing qualification is mandatory for LDC posts (Hindi typing speed 30 wpm in KrutiDev or Mangal font; English 35 wpm). Revenue Employee does not require a typing certificate. OBC/EBC candidates must hold a valid Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate issued in Bihar state format within the application validity window.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can candidates outside Bihar apply for BSSC Inter Level Exam?

Yes, candidates from all Indian states can apply for BSSC Inter Level recruitment. However, non-Bihar candidates will be treated under General/Unreserved category and cannot claim domicile reservation benefits.

Q2. Is computer typing certificate mandatory for Rajasva Karmchari?

No. For the post of Rajasva Karmchari (3,559 vacancies), passing 10+2 Intermediate is the only compulsory requirement; computer or typing certificates are not mandatory.

Q3. What is the marking scheme in BSSC Inter Level Prelims?

The exam consists of 150 questions carrying 600 marks. Each correct answer carries 4 marks, and 1 mark is deducted for each incorrect answer.
