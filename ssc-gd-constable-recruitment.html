11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Computer Based Examination (CBE / CBT) in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages
  2. Stage 2: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
  3. Stage 3: Detailed Medical Examination (DME) & Review Medical Examination (RME)
  4. Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) conducted by CAPF authorities
  5. Stage 5: Final Merit List and Force Allocation based on CBT score and candidate preferences

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Go to the new official SSC portal at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: If applying on the new portal for the first time, complete One-Time Registration (OTR) with personal & educational details.
  3. Step 3: Login using your Registration Number and Password.
  4. Step 4: Under 'Live Examinations', click 'Apply' for Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces.
  5. Step 5: Select your preferred examination centers (up to 3 within the same region).
  6. Step 6: Give your order of preference for the paramilitary forces (BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, AR, SSF).
  7. Step 7: Capture live photograph via webcam/mobile camera as per SSC new guidelines and upload scanned signature (10KB - 20KB).
  8. Step 8: Pay the application fee (₹100 for Gen/OBC/EWS Male; ₹0 for SC/ST/Female) and submit.
  9. Step 9: Download and take a printout of the completed application form for future reference.

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates must choose their force preferences very carefully as force allotment once made is final and cannot be modified.
  • The new SSC portal captures live photos during the application process; ensure proper lighting without glasses, caps, or masks.
  • Merit is determined solely by the marks obtained in the Computer Based Examination (CBT). PST/PET and Medical stages are qualifying.
  • Ensure the name, father's name, and date of birth match exactly with the 10th standard certificate.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can 10th pass candidates apply for SSC GD Constable?

Yes, the minimum educational qualification for SSC GD Constable is 10th Class (Matriculation) pass from any recognized educational board.

Q2. Is there an interview in SSC GD Constable recruitment?

No, there is no interview. Selection is made through Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Physical Tests (PST/PET), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Q3. In what languages is the SSC GD CBT examination conducted?

The Computer Based Exam is conducted in Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages including Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.

Q4. What is the physical running standard for SSC GD male and female candidates?

Male candidates must complete 5 km in 24 minutes. Female candidates must complete 1.6 km in 8 minutes and 30 seconds.
