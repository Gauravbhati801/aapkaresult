1. UP Police Constable Exam Pattern & Topics

The UP Police Constable test consists of an objective OMR examination containing 150 multiple choice questions carrying 2 marks each.

Subject Area Questions Marks Syllabus Scope
General Knowledge (सामान्य ज्ञान) 38 76 History, Indian Constitution, UP Culture & Geography, Economy, Current Affairs, Science.
General Hindi (सामान्य हिन्दी) 37 74 विलोम, पर्यायवाची, तद्भव-तत्सम, संधि, समास, मुहावरे, लोकोक्तियां, प्रमुख रचनाएं.
Numerical & Mental Ability (गणित) 38 76 Number System, Simplification, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio, Time & Work, Mensuration.
Mental Aptitude / Reasoning (तर्कशक्ति) 37 74 Analogies, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Visual Memory.

Frequently Asked Questions (Exam Syllabus)

Q1. Are syllabus patterns identical every year?

While core subjects (Mathematics, Reasoning, General Knowledge) remain largely consistent, recruitment boards occasionally adjust question counts, total duration, or negative marking rules. Always consult the latest year's advertisement.

Q2. How should candidates allocate preparation time for multi-section exams?

Focus daily on scoring sections like Reasoning and Hindi/English, while allocating consistent morning slots for General Science and Current Affairs.
