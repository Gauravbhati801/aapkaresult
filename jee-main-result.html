JEE Main Result 2026: NTA Scorecard, All India Rank (AIR), Cutoff for JEE Advanced

Board: National Testing Agency (NTA), Ministry of Education, Govt. of India Mode: Online Verification Status: Live & Available Online

NTA JEE Main 2026 : B.E. / B.Tech Paper-1 & B.Arch / B.Planning Paper-2 Scorecard
Exam Authority: National Testing Agency (NTA), Ministry of Education, Govt. of India 🚀 NTA JEE MAIN OFFICIAL SCORECARD LIVE

Short Information : The National Testing Agency (NTA) has declared the official NTA Scorecard and All India Rank (AIR) list for JEE (Main) 2026. Aspirants who appeared in the Computer-Based Test can check their subject-wise normalized percentile marks (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics), category rank, and qualifying cut-off for JEE (Advanced) 2026 using their Application Number and Date of Birth.

Step-by-Step: How to Check Scorecard & Marksheet Online
  1. Click on NTA JEE Main Official Scorecard Server link below.
  2. Enter your 12-digit JEE Main Application Number.
  3. Select your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) as registered.
  4. Enter the Security Pin (Captcha) displayed on the screen.
  5. Click Submit to view and download your multi-session NTA Scorecard with All India Rank.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I check my official scorecard?

You can check directly using the official server links given in the Useful Important Links table above, or through the government DigiLocker portal.

Q2. What credentials are required to verify the result?

Keep your official examination Roll Number, Date of Birth, and School/Application Number (printed on your original admit card) ready.

Q3. Is the online marksheet valid for admissions?

Yes. The online scorecard or DigiLocker digitally signed certificate serves as valid provisional proof for admission counselling until the physical certificate is released.
