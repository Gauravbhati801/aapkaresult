How to Fill Railway RRB NTPC Graduate Online Form 2026
  • Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released Centralized Employment Notice CEN 06/2026. Candidate Can Apply Online Between 08/10/2026 to 06/11/2026.
  • Visit the official railway recruitment application portal rrbapply.gov.in.
  • Click on Apply → Create an Account. Fill in Name, DOB, Aadhaar Number, Email ID, and Mobile Number. Verify via OTP.

  • Select your desired RRB Zone and prioritize post preferences (Goods Train Manager, CCCTS, Senior Clerk, JAA).
  • Upload live webcam photograph and scanned digital signature per RRB specifications.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline (BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, BBA, etc.) from a recognized University or Institution in India.

Q2. What are the start and end dates to apply for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

The online application started on 08 October 2026 and the closing date to submit the application is 06 November 2026 (up to 23:59 Hrs).

Q3. What is the age limit for RRB NTPC Graduate posts under CEN 06/2026?

The age limit is 18 to 33 years as of 01 January 2027. Category-wise age relaxations are applicable: 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 years for PwBD candidates.

Q4. How does the RRB application fee refund policy work?

Candidates who appear in the 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1) are eligible for fee refund. General/OBC candidates pay ₹500, out of which ₹400 is refunded to their bank account. SC/ST/Female/ESM/EBC candidates pay ₹250, and the entire ₹250 is refunded after bank charges.

Q5. Is there negative marking in the RRB NTPC CBT examination?

Yes, there is negative marking of 1/3rd (0.33) marks for every incorrect answer in both CBT Stage 1 and CBT Stage 2 examinations.

Editorial Deep-Dive: Railway RRB NTPC Graduate Level (Level 5 & 6)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Station Master oversees 24/7 railway station operations, train signaling clearances, route interlocking, and passenger safety. Goods Train Manager (Guard) manages freight safety, brake power certificates, and line communication. Senior Commercial Clerk manages reservations and freight logistics. Station Masters have prestigious career advancement paths leading to Station Superintendent, Station Director, and Assistant Operations Manager (Group B Gazetted).

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Station Master is recruited under Pay Matrix Level-6 (Basic Pay ₹35,400). Adding 50% Dearness Allowance (₹17,700), House Rent Allowance (₹3,186 to ₹9,558 depending on city tier), Transport Allowance, and Running / Night Duty allowances, the starting gross salary exceeds ₹65,000 per month. In-hand take-home pay is approximately ₹56,000 to ₹61,000 per month, supplemented by free Family Railway Travel Passes, PTOs, and railway hospital medical coverage.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Station Master aspirants must pass the Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) with a minimum T-score of 42 in each test battery, with no category relaxation. For the CBT-2 written examination, target a safe raw score of 84+ out of 120 marks in high-competition railway zones like Allahabad, Chandigarh, and Patna. For Level-5 positions (Goods Guard and Senior Clerk), safe scores range between 79 and 85 marks depending on regional zone allocations.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

CBT-2 demands answering 120 questions within 90 minutes (only 45 seconds per question). Maximize scores in General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 questions) and Mathematics (35 questions) where near 100% accuracy is achievable. General Awareness (50 questions) requires consistent revision of General Science (Physics, Biology) and the last 8 months of National and International Current Affairs.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Station Master candidates must strictly fulfill the Aye-Two (A-2) medical standard: distant vision 6/9, 6/9 without glasses, and mandatory passing of tests for color vision, binocular vision, and field of vision. Laser eye corrective surgery (LASIK) is strictly disqualified for Station Master and Goods Guard posts. Check medical fitness requirements thoroughly before locking post preferences.

📈 Cut-Off Trends & Competition Level Breakdown:

The selection process for Railway RRB NTPC Graduate posts (Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Senior Commercial Clerk) involves CBT-1 (qualifying screening) followed by post-specific CBT-2 and the Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for Station Master. The CBT-2 examination demands solving 120 high-standard questions within 90 minutes with 1/3rd negative marking. Historical trends from the previous NTPC recruitment cycles reveal that popular graduate roles in prime railway zones (Northern Railway, North Eastern Railway, East Central Railway) witness intense competition, pushing the normalized cut-off beyond 70% of total marks. For Station Master aspirants, clearing the 5 test batteries of the CBAT with a minimum T-score of 42 in each battery without any category relaxation is mandatory; final merit combines 70% CBT-2 score and 30% CBAT score.

Subject-Wise Preparation Strategy & High-Weightage Topics

Tailored Study Roadmap by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk

Optimizing your preparation for the 120-question RRB NTPC CBT-2 examination requires a dedicated section-by-section plan: • General Awareness (50 Questions): With over 41% of the total question paper allocated to GA, focus heavily on Indian Polity (Constitutional amendments, emergency provisions), Modern Indian History, Physical and Economic Geography, General Science (Physics, Biology), and the last 8 months of National and International Current Affairs. • Mathematics (35 Questions): Advanced topics like Trigonometry, Heights & Distances, Coordinate Geometry, Elementary Statistics (Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation), and Data Interpretation (DI tables and pie charts) appear consistently. Speed is vital; practice mental arithmetic shortcuts. • General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 Questions): Master Complex Seating Arrangements (Circular and Linear with multiple variables), Puzzles, Syllogisms, Statement-Assumptions, and Inequalities. Target 32+ correct answers in under 26 minutes.
