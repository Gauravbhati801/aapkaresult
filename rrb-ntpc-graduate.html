Railway RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 3,548 Posts (CEN 06/2026)

Authority: Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs), Ministry of Railways Notice No: Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) No. 06/2026 Status: Online Application Active
Share Alert:
Ministry of Railways, Government of India (Railway Recruitment Boards): The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have officially issued the Centralized Employment Notice CEN 06/2026 for recruitment to 3,548 Graduate Level vacancies under Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC). Posts include Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist, and Senior Clerk cum Typist across all 21 Zonal Railways. Eligible Bachelor's degree holders can register and submit their online application from 08 October 2026 to 06 November 2026 at rrbapply.gov.in.
Total Vacancies 3,548 Posts
Qualification Graduation (Bachelor Degree in any stream)
Age Limit 18 to 33 Years (as on 01 January 2027)
Pay Level Pay Matrix Level 5 & Level 6 (7th CPC)
Application Fee ₹500 (₹400 Refundable) | ₹250 (Full Refundable)
Selection Process CBT 1 • CBT 2 • Typing Skill • DV • Medical
📅 Important Dates (CEN 06/2026)
  • Official CEN Notification Date: October 2026
  • Online Application Start Date: 08 October 2026
  • Online Application Last Date: 06 November 2026 (23:59 Hrs)
  • Last Date for Online Fee Payment: 06 November 2026
  • Online Modification / Correction Window: November 2026
  • 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1): To be Announced Soon
  • 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2): Post CBT-1 Results
  • Typing Skill Test / CBST: Post CBT-2 Shortlisting
💳 Application Fee & Bank Refund Policy
  • General / OBC / EWS Male: ₹500/- (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1)
  • SC / ST Candidates: ₹250/- (₹250 fully refunded after appearing in CBT-1)
  • All Female Candidates: ₹250/- (₹250 fully refunded after appearing in CBT-1)
  • PwBD / Ex-Servicemen / Transgender: ₹250/- (Full Refundable)
  • Minorities & Economically Backward (EBC): ₹250/- (Full Refundable)
  • Payment Modes: Online via Internet Banking, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, or UPI.
👤 Age Limit & Relaxation Criteria (As on 01 January 2027)
Minimum Age: 18 Years  |  Maximum Age: 33 Years.
Category-Wise Age Relaxations:
• OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 Years upper age relaxation (Up to 36 Years)
• SC / ST Candidates: 5 Years upper age relaxation (Up to 38 Years)
• PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities): 10 Years relaxation (PwBD OBC: 13 Yrs, PwBD SC/ST: 15 Yrs)
• Ex-Servicemen: Length of service + 3 years (plus category relaxation).

📝 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for RRB NTPC Graduate Form 2026

  1. Step 1 (Centralized Portal): Visit the official railway recruitment application portal rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Step 2 (Create Account): Click on Apply → Create an Account. Fill in your Name, Date of Birth, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Aadhaar Number, Email ID, and Mobile Number. Verify via OTP.
  3. Step 3 (Fill Application Details): Login to your account, click on Online Application, and select CEN 06/2026 (Graduate Level). Enter your educational details and graduation percentage.
  4. Step 4 (Choose RRB Zone & Post Preferences): Select your desired participating RRB Zone and prioritize your post preferences (Goods Train Manager, CCCTS, Senior Clerk, JAA).
  5. Step 5 (Upload Photo & Signature): Upload your live webcam/color passport photograph and digital signature according to RRB guidelines.
  6. Step 6 (Pay Application Fee & Bank Details): Pay the application fee and verify your bank account details carefully for the fee refund after CBT-1. Submit the form and download the confirmation slip.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline (BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, BBA, etc.) from a recognized University or Institution in India.

Q2. What are the start and end dates to apply for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

The online application started on 08 October 2026 and the closing date to submit the application is 06 November 2026 (up to 23:59 Hrs).

Q3. What is the age limit for RRB NTPC Graduate posts under CEN 06/2026?

The age limit is 18 to 33 years as of 01 January 2027. Category-wise age relaxations are applicable: 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 years for PwBD candidates.

Q4. How does the RRB application fee refund policy work?

Candidates who appear in the 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1) are eligible for fee refund. General/OBC candidates pay ₹500, out of which ₹400 is refunded to their bank account. SC/ST/Female/ESM/EBC candidates pay ₹250, and the entire ₹250 is refunded after bank charges.

Q5. Is there negative marking in the RRB NTPC CBT examination?

Yes, there is negative marking of 1/3rd (0.33) marks for every incorrect answer in both CBT Stage 1 and CBT Stage 2 examinations.
