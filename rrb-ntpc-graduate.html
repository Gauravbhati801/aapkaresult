How to Fill Railway RRB NTPC Graduate Online Form 2026
  • Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released Centralized Employment Notice CEN 06/2026. Candidate Can Apply Online Between 08/10/2026 to 06/11/2026.
  • Visit the official railway recruitment application portal rrbapply.gov.in.
  • Click on Apply → Create an Account. Fill in Name, DOB, Aadhaar Number, Email ID, and Mobile Number. Verify via OTP.
  • Login to your account, click on Online Application, select CEN 06/2026 (Graduate Level), enter qualification details.
  • Select your desired RRB Zone and prioritize post preferences (Goods Train Manager, CCCTS, Senior Clerk, JAA).
  • Upload live webcam photograph and scanned digital signature per RRB specifications.
  • Pay the application fee and verify bank account details carefully for the fee refund after appearing in CBT-1.
  • Submit the application form and take a printout of the final confirmation page for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline (BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, BBA, etc.) from a recognized University or Institution in India.

Q2. What are the start and end dates to apply for RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026?

The online application started on 08 October 2026 and the closing date to submit the application is 06 November 2026 (up to 23:59 Hrs).

Q3. What is the age limit for RRB NTPC Graduate posts under CEN 06/2026?

The age limit is 18 to 33 years as of 01 January 2027. Category-wise age relaxations are applicable: 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 years for PwBD candidates.

Q4. How does the RRB application fee refund policy work?

Candidates who appear in the 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1) are eligible for fee refund. General/OBC candidates pay ₹500, out of which ₹400 is refunded to their bank account. SC/ST/Female/ESM/EBC candidates pay ₹250, and the entire ₹250 is refunded after bank charges.

Q5. Is there negative marking in the RRB NTPC CBT examination?

Yes, there is negative marking of 1/3rd (0.33) marks for every incorrect answer in both CBT Stage 1 and CBT Stage 2 examinations.
