Physical Measurement Test (PMT) & Screening Rules in 2026

Physical Screening Tests (PST/PMT) in uniformed forces—including State Police Forces (UP Police, Delhi Police), Central Armed Police Forces (SSC GD CAPF: BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB), Railway Protection Force (RPF), and the Indian Armed Forces (Army Agniveer)—follow rigid physical measurement protocols:

1. Digital Stadiometer Height Measurement

Height is recorded bare-footed on a precision digital stadiometer. Hair must be flattened. In UP Police, General/OBC/SC male candidates must measure at least 168.0 cm, while SSC GD mandates 170.0 cm.

2. Mandatory 5 cm Active Chest Expansion (सीने का फुलाव)

For male candidates, having a broad chest (e.g. 84 cm) is not enough. You must demonstrate an active thoracic expansion of at least 5 cm upon deep inhalation (e.g. 79 cm unexpanded to 84 cm expanded).

3. WHO Body Mass Index (BMI) & Weight Standards

Weight must be proportionate to height and age based on medical standards. A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is optimal. Female candidates in UP Police must weigh a minimum of 40 kg.

⚠️ Armed Forces & Police Medical Examination Checklist (DME Guidelines)

👁️ विजन मानक (Eye Vision Standards) दूर दृष्टि (Distant Vision) बिना चश्मे के बेहतर आंख 6/6 और दूसरी आंख न्यूनतम 6/9 होनी चाहिए। कलर ब्लाइंडनेस (Color Blindness) टेस्ट में CP-III या Ishihara प्लेट्स पास करना अनिवार्य है।
🦵 नॉक-नी (Knock Knees Test) सीधे खड़े होने पर दोनों घुटने आपस में टकराने (Knock Knees) या पिंडलियों के बीच अत्यधिक गैप (Bow Legs) होने पर उम्मीदवार को अनफिट घोषित कर दिया जाता है।
🦶 फ्लैट फुट (Flat Foot Evaluation) पैर के तलवे में प्राकृतिक आर्क (Curved Arch) होना आवश्यक है। गीले पैर से फर्श पर चलने पर यदि पूरा तलवा छपता है, तो यह फ्लैट फुट की श्रेणी में आता है।
✒️ टैटू पॉलिसी (Tattoo Policy) फोरआर्म के भीतरी हिस्से पर धार्मिक चिन्ह (जैसे ॐ या नाम) का छोटा टैटू (कोहनी से कलाई के 1/4 आकार से छोटा) ही मान्य होता है। चेहरे, गर्दन या हथेली पर टैटू पूर्णतः वर्जित हैं।

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल)

Q1. UP Police Constable भर्ती में सीने का फुलाव कम से कम कितना होना चाहिए?

उत्तर प्रदेश पुलिस में सामान्य, ओबीसी और एससी पुरुष अभ्यर्थियों का सामान्य सीना न्यूनतम 79 सेमी और फुलाने पर कम से कम 84 सेमी होना चाहिए। सीने में न्यूनतम 5 सेमी का फुलाव अनिवार्य है। यदि सामान्य सीना 85 सेमी भी है लेकिन फुलाव 4 सेमी ही आया, तो अभ्यर्थी अनफिट हो जाएगा।

Q2. What is the minimum height required for female candidates in UP Police?

For General, EWS, OBC, and SC female candidates, the minimum height standard is 152.0 cm. For Scheduled Tribe (ST) female candidates, the relaxed minimum height is 147.0 cm. A minimum body weight of 40 kg is mandatory.

Q3. How is Body Mass Index (BMI) calculated for physical medicals?

Body Mass Index is calculated using the World Health Organization formula: BMI = Weight in kg ÷ (Height in meters)². A BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is considered normal. Medical boards allow a ±10% acceptable tolerance depending on age and muscular build.

Q4. What is the height requirement for SSC GD Constable CAPF recruitment?

In SSC GD Constable, General, OBC, and SC male candidates must measure at least 170 cm, and females must measure 157 cm. For ST candidates, male height is relaxed to 162.5 cm and female height to 150 cm.

Q5. Can spectacles (चश्मा) wearers qualify in Police and Paramilitary medicals?

Executive field duties (Constable, Commando, Army GD) require natural unaided vision of 6/6 in the better eye and 6/9 in the other. However, ministerial and clerical posts (e.g., Head Constable Ministerial, ASI Steno) permit spectacles with corrected vision up to 6/6 with specified refractive tolerances.

Q6. How can candidates improve their chest expansion before PST?

Practice deep diaphragmatic breathing exercises (Pranayama, Bhastrika), pull-ups, push-ups, and dumbbell pullovers daily. During the measurement, relax your shoulders before measurement and inhale deeply expanding the ribcage upwards and outwards.

Q7. How many chances are given if a candidate fails the height measurement?

Most recruitment boards (including UP Police and SSC GD) provide an on-spot appeal mechanism before the Appellate Medical Authority on the same day. The candidate is re-measured on an alternate certified digital stadiometer in the presence of the board chairman.
