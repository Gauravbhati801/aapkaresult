NLC India Executive Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 11/2026): Apply Online for 145 Posts

Organization: NLC India Limited (Navratna PSU, Ministry of Coal) Advt No.: 11/2026 Total Vacancy: 145 Posts (E-1 to E-7) Status: Online Application Active (Till 19 Dec 2026)
NLC India Limited (NLCIL) – A Navratna Govt. of India Enterprise
NLC India Executive Recruitment Online Form 2026 (145 Posts)
Advertisement No. 11/2026 • Grades E-1 to E-7 (DGM, ACM, Executive Engineer, Manager)
PSU Enterprise: NLC India Limited (Ministry of Coal) 🔥 145 EXECUTIVE POSTS • APPLY 20 NOV TO 19 DEC 2026

Short Information : NLC India Limited (NLCIL), a premier Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Coal, has officially published Advertisement No. 11/2026 for the recruitment of 145 Executive positions across Grades E-1 to E-7. Vacancies span across Thermal Power, Mines, Projects, Scientific Labs, Human Resources, Legal, and Security divisions (Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Mining, C&I, Chemical, Environmental, and Safety Engineering). Eligible candidates can register online at nlcindia.in from 20 November 2026 (10:00 AM) until 19 December 2026 (05:00 PM). Check full post-wise vacancies, IDA pay scales up to ₹2,60,000/-, age limits, fee details, and selection procedure below.

Selection Process & How to Apply Online (nlcindia.in)
  1. Selection Procedure: Selection is conducted through Personal Interview (or Computer Based Test + Personal Interview for Legal and disciplines where eligible applications exceed shortlisting ratio), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.
  2. Visit the official NLC India Limited website at www.nlcindia.in and open the Careers → Current Openings tab.
  3. Download and carefully verify eligibility, experience duration, and category seat matrix in Advt. No. 11/2026.
  4. Complete New Registration between 20 November 2026 and 19 December 2026 (05:00 PM) using valid email and mobile number.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, degree certificates, and experience proofs, then pay the online fee (₹854 / ₹354).
  6. Submit the final application before 22 December 2026 (05:00 PM) and save a printed copy for Interview / DV.
