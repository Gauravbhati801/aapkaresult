IREL Tradesman Trainee Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 131 Posts

Recruiting Body: IREL (India) Limited (Govt. of India Undertaking – Dept. of Atomic Energy) Post Name: Tradesman Trainee (Fitter, Electrician, AOCP, Instrumentation) Advt No: Advt.No.CO/HRM/36/2026

Name Of Post : IREL Tradesman Trainee (Fitter, Electrician, AOCP & Instrumentation) Recruitment 2026
Post Date / Update : 18 October 2026 | 02:45 PM 🎉 131 Posts • Monthly Stipend ₹34,200/-

Short Information : IREL (India) Limited, a premier Mini-Ratna Category-I Central Public Sector Enterprise under the administrative control of the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE), has issued employment notification Advt.No.CO/HRM/36/2026 for regular induction of 131 Tradesman Trainees across OSCOM (Odisha), Chavara (Kerala), Manavalakurichi (Tamil Nadu), RED Aluva (Kerala), REPM Vizag (AP), and RETTP Bhopal (MP). Candidates holding ITI / NAC or +2 Science qualification with 2 years of relevant experience can apply online from 19/10/2026 (10:00 AM) to 09/11/2026 (06:00 PM). Read on for complete unit-wise vacancy breakdown, age relaxations, stipend details, CBT examination pattern, and direct official application links.

Important Dates
  • Online Application Begin : 19/10/2026 (10:00 AM)
  • Last Date for Apply Online : 09/11/2026 (06:00 PM)
  • Last Date for Fee Payment : 09/11/2026
  • Crucial Date for Age & Experience : 09/11/2026
  • CBT Exam Date : To be announced later
  • Admit Card Available : Before Exam
Application Fee
  • UR / EWS / OBC (NCL) : ₹826/- (₹472 App Fee + ₹354 Processing Fee incl. 18% GST)
  • SC / ST / PwBD / ESM : ₹354/- (App Fee Exempted; Only Processing Fee)
  • Female & IREL Internal Candidates : ₹354/- (Only Processing Fee)
  • Payment Mode : Pay online via SBI e-Pay Payment Gateway (Internet Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI).
IREL Tradesman Trainee Notification 2026 : Age Limit as on 09/11/2026
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum / Upper Age Limit : 35 Years (for General / UR candidates)
  • Age Relaxation Extra : 3 Years for OBC (NCL) (up to 38 years), 5 Years for SC / ST (up to 40 years).
  • PwBD Candidates : 10 Years relaxation (PwBD + OBC = 13 Yrs, PwBD + SC/ST = 15 Yrs). Maximum age with all relaxations cannot exceed 50 years.
Stipend During Training & Regular Pay Scale Upon Absorption
  • 1st Year Training Stipend : Consolidated ₹34,200/- per month + House Rent Allowance (HRA) calculated on W-3 minimum scale of ₹22,000/- + Medical facilities for self and dependent family members as per company rules.
  • Regular Absorption : On successful completion of 1 year training, candidates will be absorbed as Tradesman in regular W-3 Grade.
  • Regular Pay Scale : ₹22,000 – ₹88,000/- with Industrial DA (IDA), HRA/Company Accommodation, Cafeteria Perks, PRPS, EPF, Gratuity & Group Insurance.
  • Approximate Annual CTC : ₹6.50 Lakhs per annum.
  • Service Agreement Bond : ₹50,000/- for UR/EWS/OBC-NCL and ₹25,000/- for SC/ST/PwBD candidates to serve the company for minimum 3 years after training.

Selection Procedure & CBT Exam Pattern (No Negative Marking)
  • Stage 1 - Written Examination (CBT) : Computer Based Test of 2 Hours duration (120 Minutes) conducted in bilingual format (Hindi & English).
  • Section-I (Subject Knowledge / Trade Test) : 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) based on respective ITI trade / +2 Science Chemistry concepts (100 Marks).
  • Section-II (General Aptitude) : 50 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) covering General Knowledge, Logical Reasoning, Numerical Ability, and General English (50 Marks).
  • Total Marks & Questions : 150 Questions for 150 Marks. Each question carries 1 mark.
  • Negative Marking : NO NEGATIVE MARKING in the CBT exam.
  • Qualifying Marks in CBT : UR & EWS candidates must score minimum 50% marks in Section-I and Section-II separately. SC / ST / OBC(NCL) / PwBD candidates must score minimum 45% marks in Section-I and Section-II separately.
  • Stage 2 - Skill Test / Trade Test : Shortlisted candidates in the ratio of 1:5 based on CBT merit will be called for Trade Test. The Trade Test is strictly Qualifying in nature.
  • Final Merit List : Prepared purely on the basis of marks obtained in the CBT examination among candidates who qualify the Trade Test, followed by Document Verification & Pre-employment Medical Examination.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. How many total vacancies are announced in IREL Tradesman Trainee Recruitment 2026?

There are a total of 131 regular vacancies: Fitter (93), Electrician (12), Instrumentation (6), and Attendant Operator Chemical Plant - AOCP (20).

Q2. What is the educational qualification and experience required?

Candidates must hold ITI / NAC in the relevant trade (Fitter, Electrician, Instrumentation, or AOCP) OR +2 Science (Intermediate with Chemistry min 50% for AOCP) plus minimum 2 years of relevant post-qualification experience or apprenticeship in a reputed industry.

Q3. What is the stipend during the training period?

Selected candidates receive a consolidated monthly stipend of ₹34,200/- plus HRA (calculated on ₹22,000 W-3 basic) and medical facility during the 1-year training. After successful completion, they are absorbed in regular W-3 grade (Pay scale: ₹22,000 – ₹88,000/- with CTC around ₹6.5 Lakhs/year).

Q4. What is the age limit and cut-off date?

The maximum age limit is 35 years as on 09/11/2026. Age relaxations of 3 years for OBC(NCL), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 to 15 years for PwBD candidates are applicable as per Central Government rules.

Q5. Is there negative marking in the IREL Tradesman CBT exam?

No, there is NO negative marking in the 150-mark Computer Based Test (100 Trade Questions + 50 Aptitude Questions).
