AapkaResult : How to Fill UPSSSC Lower Subordinate (10+2 Level) Online Form 2026
  • UPSSSC Combined Lower Subordinate (10+2 Level) Recruitment online applications are accepted between 10/11/2026 to 01/12/2026.
  • Visit the official commission portal upsssc.gov.in.
  • Click on Applicant Authentication through OTR / PET. Enter your PET Registration Number, Date of Birth, Gender, and Mobile OTP.
  • Your basic personal data, photo, and signature will auto-populate from your PET profile.
  • Enter your 10+2 (Intermediate) academic details (Board Name, Passing Year, Roll Number, Marks/Percentage).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the qualification required for UPSSSC Combined Lower Subordinate 2026?

Candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognized Board in India and must possess a valid UPSSSC PET scorecard.

Q2. What is the age limit for UPSSSC Lower Subordinate recruitment?

The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01/07/2026. Age relaxation of 5 years is applicable for SC, ST, and OBC candidates belonging to Uttar Pradesh.

Q3. What is the application fee for UPSSSC Advt 26-Exam/2026?

The application processing fee is ₹25/- for all categories (General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, PH).

Q4. What are the key posts included in this recruitment?

Key posts include Vanijya Kar Sangrah Amin (Commercial Tax Collection Amin - 235), Bhoomi Arjan Amin (Land Acquisition Amin - 187), Bhandar Pal evam Lipik (Storekeeper & Clerk - 118), Sahakari Krishi Parikshak (50), Tabulator (14), Warden (05), Farm Clerk, Salesman, and other subordinate cadres (Total: 1,784 Posts).

Q5. When will the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Exam be conducted?

The UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Exam date will be announced as per schedule. The exam city slip and admit card will be released before the examination.
