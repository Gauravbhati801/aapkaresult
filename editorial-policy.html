Editorial Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | AapkaResult.in Standards

At AapkaResult.in, our editorial mission is to provide authentic, timely, and easy-to-understand educational updates and recruitment alerts to candidates across India. We strictly adhere to principles of accuracy, transparency, and independence.

1. Primary Source Verification

Our editorial team prioritizes official government gazettes, state recruitment boards, Central Commission notifications (such as UPSC, SSC, RRBs, NTA), and verified government web portals. We do not publish speculative reports without direct official confirmation.

  • Every job alert is cross-checked with the official Employment News or authorized board PDF.
  • Official application URLs and notification PDF links are always linked for candidate verification.
  • Updates with tentative schedules are clearly labelled as "Provisional / Expected".

2. Human Review & Content Originality

All published articles, eligibility criteria breakdowns, syllabus analyses, and exam pattern guides undergo thorough human editorial review. While technology assists in formatting and data compilation, every detail is verified by our experienced content editors before going live.

  • No machine-generated content is published without expert verification.
  • Content is simplified into structured tables, checklists, and step-by-step guides for maximum readability.
  • Clickbait titles and misleading headlines are strictly prohibited.

3. Correction & Update Transparency

If an error, typo, or recruitment date change occurs, our editorial team immediately updates the article with an updated timestamp. Readers can flag any discrepancy by writing directly to our grievance desk at support@aapkaresult.in.

Feedback & Queries: If you have any inquiries regarding our policies or wish to report a correction, please contact our editorial desk at support@aapkaresult.in or visit our Contact Us page.
