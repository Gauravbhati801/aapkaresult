Editorial & Fact-Checking Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Editorial Standards of AapkaResult.in

Our Commitment to Indian Aspirants: At AapkaResult.in, our highest editorial priority is accuracy, impartiality, and speed. We recognize that an incorrect application date, misstated eligibility criterion, or misleading examination notice can cost a student months of preparation and thousands of rupees in lost opportunities. Every article, alert, and syllabus breakdown published on our portal is researched, written, and verified exclusively by human education analysts.

1. Primary Source Verification Mandate

The foundation of our editorial process is strict reliance on primary official government sources. Our research desk does not rely on hearsay, social media rumors, or secondary news aggregators. Before any recruitment alert or result update is drafted, our analysts verify the information against:

  • Official Government Web Portals: Websites bearing authorized top-level domains ending strictly in .gov.in, .nic.in, or .ac.in (such as ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, rrbcdg.gov.in, uppbpb.gov.in, and nta.ac.in).
  • Central & State Gazettes: Official Gazette of India notifications, state legislative gazettes, and the weekly print and digital editions of Employment News (Rozgar Samachar) published by the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting.
  • Original Advertisements in PDF Format: We extract dates, vacancy counts, qualification thresholds, and fee structures directly from digitally signed official notification PDFs released by the respective recruitment boards.

2. Rigorous 4-Stage Human Editorial Workflow

To ensure zero discrepancies, every article published on AapkaResult.in passes through four mandatory stages of human scrutiny:

  1. Stage 1 — Source Ingestion: The researcher verifies that the recruitment notification is live on the conducting board's official server and saves an archival copy of the original PDF advertisement.
  2. Stage 2 — Multi-Point Parameter Extraction: The researcher manually extracts and validates essential candidate parameters:
    • Crucial eligibility cut-off dates for age and educational qualifications.
    • Category-wise reservation quotas (UR, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD, ESM).
    • Exact fee payment deadlines and permissible payment gateways.
    • Physical measurement (PMT) and physical efficiency (PET) benchmarks.
    • Detailed negative marking formulas and multi-shift normalization rules.
  3. Stage 3 — Editorial Simplification: Legalistic gazette language is synthesized into clear, structured tables, bullet points, and step-by-step instructional guides that students from all linguistic backgrounds can effortlessly understand.
  4. Stage 4 — Pre-Publish Senior Verification: A senior editor cross-examines the drafted post against the primary PDF before clearing it for publication.

3. Corrigendum, Extension & Amendment Tracking

Recruitment boards frequently release corrigendums (amendments) extending application deadlines, altering category-wise vacancies, or modifying examination centers. Our editorial desk maintains continuous surveillance of commission notice boards.

Whenever an official corrigendum is issued, our corresponding article is updated immediately. We append a highlighted revision notice specifying what changed, when it changed, and providing a direct link to the official corrigendum PDF so candidates remain fully informed.

4. Zero Clickbait & Strict Anti-Speculation Guarantee

We believe that sensationalism has no place in educational journalism. We strictly uphold the following non-negotiable guidelines:

  • No Speculative Dates: We never declare examination dates or result timings without an official press note or public notice from the conducting authority.
  • Clear Labeling of Tentative Information: If an examination calendar lists tentative schedules, they are explicitly designated as "Tentative / Subject to Official Confirmation".
  • Direct, Unaltered Destination Links: When directing students to apply online or download admit cards, we link directly to the official government login portal—never through deceptive ad-interstitials, paid shorteners, or confusing redirection chains.

5. Transparency, Independence & Commercial Non-Influence

AapkaResult.in is an independent digital news publisher. We maintain complete editorial independence:

  • We have no formal affiliation, partnership, or sponsorship agreements with any government commission, ministry, or coaching institute.
  • Advertisements displayed on our portal (such as Google AdSense units) are strictly segregated from editorial content. Commercial considerations have zero influence on our reporting, job recommendations, or exam reviews.
  • We never charge candidates fees for accessing alerts, question papers, syllabus PDFs, or practice mock tests.

6. Reader Feedback & Grievance Redressal Protocol

Despite our stringent verification standards, typographical oversights or rapid board-level changes can occasionally occur. We welcome our readers' vigilance and maintain an active, transparent corrections process:

  • Correction Request Channel: Readers and educators who notice a discrepancy can write directly to our chief editor at gauravbhati@aapkaresult.in with the subject line "Correction Request - [Exam Name]".
  • Turnaround Commitment: Our desk reviews reported discrepancies against official gazette records within four hours of receipt. If an error is verified, the post is updated immediately with an explicit editorial note acknowledging the update.
  • Grievance Office: You may also reach out via our dedicated Contact Us page.
