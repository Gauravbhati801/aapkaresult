BHU Undergraduate (UG) & Postgraduate (PG) Admission Counseling 2026

Board / University: Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi (Central University) Mode: Online Registration & Counseling Status: Admission Portal Active
Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi (Central University)
BHU Undergraduate (UG) & Postgraduate (PG) Admission Counseling 2026
CAP-UG / CAP-PG Combined Allotment Program via CUET Scorecards
Authority: Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi (Central University) 🎓 BHU ADMISSION & CAP COUNSELING PORTAL

Short Information : Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi invites online counseling registrations through its Combined Allotment Program (CAP-UG / CAP-PG) for admission into B.A. (Hons), B.Sc. (Hons), B.Com (Hons), B.Tech, LL.B, and Postgraduate programs. Admissions are strictly processed on the basis of NTA CUET UG / PG normalized scores and subject eligibility mapping.

Important Admission Dates
  • CUET UG / PG Entrance Exam : May – June 2026
  • BHU CAP Registration Window : July – August 2026
  • Preference Filling & Correction : August 2026
  • Round-1 to Round-4 Seat Allotment : August – September 2026
  • Spot Counseling Round : September 2026
Eligibility & Fee Structure
  • UG Course Requirement : 10+2 Pass + Valid CUET UG 2026 Score
  • PG Course Requirement : Bachelor Degree + Valid CUET PG Score
  • CAP Registration Fee (Gen/OBC) : ₹500/- (Approx.)
  • CAP Registration Fee (SC/ST/PwBD) : ₹250/- (Approx.)
  • Mandatory Document : CUET Application No & 10+2 Marksheet
Step-by-Step: Admission Registration & Counseling Procedure
  1. Go to the official Banaras Hindu University admission portal (bhuonline.in / bhucuet.samarth.edu.in).
  2. Log in using your NTA CUET Application Number and Date of Birth.
  3. Fill in your academic marks, reservation certificates (EWS/OBC-NCL/SC/ST), and upload documents.
  4. Lock your course and campus preferences (FMC, MMV, RGSC, Affiliated Colleges).
  5. Check allotment rounds regularly and pay the admission commitment fee within the deadline to confirm your seat.

AapkaResult.in Editorial Analysis: BHU CAP Counseling Cutoff Trends & Seat Preference Strategy

1. Understanding BHU Main Campus vs. Affiliated College Cutoffs: Banaras Hindu University allocates Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) seats across the Main Campus (FMC), Mahila Mahavidyalaya (MMV), and four affiliated colleges in Varanasi (DAV PG College, Arya Mahila PG College, Vasanta College for Women, and VKM). Historical Combined Allotment Program (CAP) rounds demonstrate that Main Campus BA (Hons) Social Sciences, B.Com (Hons), and B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture/Mathematics typically close within the top 8% to 12% normalized CUET percentile bracket in Round-1, whereas affiliated colleges extend 35 to 60 marks lower across Rounds 2 through 4 and the Mop-Up Spot Round.

2. How to Order Honours Subject & Paid-Seat Preferences: During BHU CAP registration on bhucuet.samarth.edu.in, candidates frequently make the mistake of selecting only 3 or 4 top subject combinations. Because BHU calculates merit strictly on specific domain + language combinations defined in the Information Bulletin, applicants should fill at least 15 to 25 preference rows in descending order of academic priority. Furthermore, ticking the optional "Paid Seat" preference alongside Regular Fee seats provides a crucial secondary safety net if your score falls 5 to 12 marks short of the regular cutoff.

3. Document Verification & Upgrade Rule Checklist: Once a seat is offered in any regular round, candidates must pay the admission commitment fee within the 24-to-48-hour payment window to stay active in the counseling loop. Choosing the "Float / Upgrade" option retains your currently allotted seat while automatically considering you for higher preferences in subsequent rounds, whereas selecting "Freeze" permanently locks your allotment and generates the final admission letter for physical reporting at the respective faculty dean's office.
