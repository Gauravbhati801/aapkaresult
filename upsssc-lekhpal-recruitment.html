Editorial Deep-Dive: UPSSSC Rajasva Lekhpal Examination

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Lekhpals are frontline village revenue officials under the Board of Revenue, Uttar Pradesh. They maintain land ownership records (Khatauni, Khasra), survey agricultural boundaries, conduct crop yield inspections (Girdawari), assess flood and drought agricultural damages for state compensation, and assist Tehsildars in anti-encroachment operations.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Recruited under 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level-3 (Basic Pay ₹21,700 – ₹69,100). Adding 50% Dearness Allowance (₹10,850), rural/suburban HRA (₹1,500 to ₹3,200), and stationary/bicycle allowances, gross monthly salary is approximately ₹35,500 to ₹37,500. Net in-hand take-home salary is approximately ₹31,500 to ₹33,800/month after NPS deductions.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Shortlisting from UPSSSC PET requires a high percentile (typically 96.5+ percentile, raw score ~65+ marks). In the 100-mark Lekhpal Mains examination, qualifying cut-offs are exceptionally competitive: UR: 77.5–81.25 marks; OBC: 76.5–80.5 marks; EWS: 76.5–80.5 marks; SC: 72.5–76 marks; ST: 66–70 marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The 100-question paper is evenly divided into General Hindi (25 Qs), Mathematics (25 Qs), General Knowledge (25 Qs), and Rural Development & Administration / Gramya Samaj (25 Qs). Master Rural Development programs, UP land measurement units (Bigha, Biswa, Acre, Hectare), and agricultural schemes to score 22+ marks in Gramya Samaj.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Verify that your Intermediate (10+2) marksheet was issued before the recruitment notification cutoff date. UP Domicile and category reservation certificates must be issued on Uttar Pradesh state formats with valid revenue portal bar codes. CCC computer certificate requirement should be verified against the official service rules.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is UPSSSC PET score mandatory for Lekhpal recruitment?

Yes, appearing in UPSSSC PET and having a valid scorecard with positive (above zero) marks is compulsory to apply for Rajasva Lekhpal recruitment.

Q2. Is CCC Computer Certificate compulsory for Lekhpal?

No. According to the amended Uttar Pradesh Rajasva Lekhpal Service Rules, CCC certificate is not mandatory. 10+2 Intermediate qualification is the sole required educational criterion.

Q3. What is the negative marking in the UPSSSC Lekhpal exam?

There is a negative marking of 1/4th (0.25 marks) for each wrong answer in the Lekhpal mains examination.

Q4. What are the four subjects included in the Lekhpal exam?

The exam consists of 100 questions divided equally into General Hindi (25 marks), Mathematics (25 marks), General Knowledge (25 marks), and Village Society & Development (Gram Samaj evam Vikas - 25 marks).
