UPSSSC Rajasva Lekhpal Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 7,994 Posts

Department: Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC), Lucknow Post Name: Rajasva Lekhpal (Revenue Accountant / Village Land Revenue Officer) Status: Recruitment Portal Active

8 & 9. Educational Qualification & Post-Wise Eligibility

General Requirement: Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) Examination from a recognized Board of Secondary Education in India. Candidates must possess a valid UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) scorecard.

Post Name / Cadre Total Vacancies Minimum Prescribed Educational Qualification
Rajasva Lekhpal 7,994 Posts 10+2 Intermediate Passed from a recognized board + Valid UPSSSC PET Scorecard.

10. Physical Standards (PST / PET)

Non-Physical Desk Role: Not Applicable. Rajasva Lekhpal is a civil revenue administrative post; there are no physical running or height/chest requirements.

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Shortlisting of applicants based on valid UPSSSC PET Normalized Score
  2. Stage 2: Rajasva Lekhpal Mains Written Examination (100 Questions, 100 Marks)
  3. Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) at UPSSSC Office, Lucknow
  4. Stage 4: Final District Allocation and Appointment Letter

12. Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

Exam Mode: Offline OMR Based Written Examination • Duration: 120 Minutes (2 Hours) • Total Questions: 100 Questions • Total Marks: 100 Marks (1 Mark per question) • Negative Marking: 1/4th (0.25) Marks deducted for each wrong answer
Subject / Examination Section Total Questions Maximum Marks

13. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

📚 General Hindi
Sandhi, Vilom, Paryayvachi, Vakyansh ke liye Ek Shabd, Samas, Ling, Vachan, Karak, Kaal, Vartani, Truti se Sambandhit, Muhavare evam Lokoktiyan, Apathit Gadyansh par Aadharit Prashna.
📚 Mathematics
Arithmetic & Statistics (Number System, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Statistics, Classification of Facts, Frequency, Histogram, Central Tendency: Mean, Median, Mode), Algebra (LCM & HCF, Simultaneous Equations, Quadratic Equations, Factors, Area Theorem), Geometry (Triangle & Pythagoras Theorem, Rectangle, Square, Trapezium, Perimeter & Area of Circles).
📚 General Knowledge
General Science, National & International Current Events, History of India, Indian National Movement, Indian Polity & Economy, World Geography & Population, UP Special General Knowledge (Culture, Agriculture, Industry, Trade, Living & Social Traditions).
📚 Village Society & Development (Gram Samaj)
Village Administration, Revenue Administration (Duties of Lekhpal, Khasra, Khatauni, Land Measurement units Bigha/Biswa/Hectare), Rural Development Programmes, Rural Social Development, Rural Employment Schemes (MGNREGA, PMAY), Panchayati Raj System, Central & State Government Welfare Schemes.

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official UPSSSC portal at upsssc.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Under Applicant Segment, click on 'Candidate Registration' for Rajasva Lekhpal Recruitment.
  3. Step 3: Authenticate using your UPSSSC PET Registration Number (via OTP or personal details).
  4. Step 4: All personal, academic, and category details from PET will populate automatically.
  5. Step 5: Verify Intermediate details, enter address, and check document previews.
  6. Step 6: Pay the initial registration fee (₹25/-) via online SBI payment gateway.
  7. Step 7: Download and save the final Application Confirmation Receipt for future reference.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • UPSSSC PET Scorecard with valid registration number
  • 10th (High School) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • UP Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra)
  • Caste Certificate (OBC non-creamy layer, SC, ST) issued by competent UP authority
  • EWS Certificate valid for current financial year (if claiming EWS quota)
  • Scanned Photograph and Signature (automatically fetched from PET profile or updated)

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates can apply only if they have appeared in UPSSSC PET and received a valid scorecard with positive normalized marks.
  • CCC Computer Certificate is NOT mandatory for Rajasva Lekhpal recruitment as per the revised revenue service rules.
  • Ensure caste and domicile certificates are issued before the cutoff date prescribed in the advertisement.
  • Shortlisting for the Mains Examination is strictly based on normalized PET scores.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is UPSSSC PET score mandatory for Lekhpal recruitment?

Yes, appearing in UPSSSC PET and having a valid scorecard with positive (above zero) marks is compulsory to apply for Rajasva Lekhpal recruitment.

Q2. Is CCC Computer Certificate compulsory for Lekhpal?

No. According to the amended Uttar Pradesh Rajasva Lekhpal Service Rules, CCC certificate is not mandatory. 10+2 Intermediate qualification is the sole required educational criterion.

Q3. What is the negative marking in the UPSSSC Lekhpal exam?

There is a negative marking of 1/4th (0.25 marks) for each wrong answer in the Lekhpal mains examination.

Q4. What are the four subjects included in the Lekhpal exam?

The exam consists of 100 questions divided equally into General Hindi (25 marks), Mathematics (25 marks), General Knowledge (25 marks), and Village Society & Development (Gram Samaj evam Vikas - 25 marks).
