Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At AapkaResult.in, accessible from https://aapkaresult.in, the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal data collected, used, and protected by AapkaResult.in.

If you have additional questions or require further information regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance desk at workwebsite299@gmail.com.

1. User Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to all of its terms and conditions.

2. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. The personal information you are requested to provide, and the purposes for which it is requested, are made transparent at the time of interaction:

  • Voluntary Correspondence: If you contact us directly via our Contact Us form or email, we receive your name, email address, inquiry topic, and message content.
  • Technical Non-Personal Data: Standard server log files collect your browser user-agent, operating system, IP address, referral URLs, and pages visited. None of this data is linked to personally identifiable identity.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We use the collected information for standard operational purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining our educational news and job alert portal.
  • Enhancing website layout, mobile responsiveness, and reader experience.
  • Responding promptly to user inquiries, correction requests, and support tickets.
  • Preventing technical glitches, security breaches, and fraudulent bot activities.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Google AdSense

Google is a third-party advertising vendor on AapkaResult.in. Google uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visit to www.aapkaresult.in and other websites across the internet.

  • Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve personalized ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its network partners to serve relevant ads based on browsing habits.
  • Users may choose to opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Log Files & Web Beacons

AapkaResult.in follows standard log file procedures. The information logged includes IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to personally identifiable information and are strictly used to analyze demographic traffic trends and maintain server stability.

6. Third-Party Websites & Recruitment Portals

AapkaResult.in contains external hyperlinks to official government recruitment portals (such as ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, and rrbapply.gov.in). Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. We strongly recommend reviewing the respective Privacy Policies of these official third-party web portals.

7. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Request that a business not sell their personal data. (Note: AapkaResult.in does NOT sell personal data).

8. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Every European user is entitled to data rights including: the right to access personal data copies, the right to rectification of inaccurate data, the right to erasure, the right to restrict or object to processing, and the right to data portability.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children's online safety is an essential priority. AapkaResult.in does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details on our portal, please contact us immediately to have it purged from our databases.
