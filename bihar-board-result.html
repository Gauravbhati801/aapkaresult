Bihar Board Result 2026: BSEB 10th Matric & 12th Inter Marksheet Check Online

Board: Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), Patna Mode: Online Verification Status: Live & Available Online
Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), Patna
Bihar Board Class 10th (Matric) & Class 12th (Intermediate) Result 2026
BSEB Annual Board Examination 2026 : Details of Result & Merit List
Exam Authority: Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), Patna 🎉 BSEB BIHAR BOARD RESULT DECLARED ONLINE

Short Information : The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), Patna has officially announced the Annual Secondary (Matric) and Higher Secondary (Intermediate) examination results. Over 30 lakh students from Arts, Science, and Commerce streams can check their marks, passing division (1st, 2nd, 3rd), and toppers list by entering their Roll Code and Roll Number.

Important Schedule & Dates
  • BSEB Time Table Released : December 2025
  • Class 12th (Inter) Exam Dates : 01 to 12 February 2026
  • Class 10th (Matric) Exam Dates : 15 to 23 February 2026
  • BSEB 12th Result Declared : March 2026
  • BSEB 10th Result Declared : March 2026
  • Scrutiny Application Window : Within 10 Days of Result
  • Compartment / Special Exam : April / May 2026
Examination & Student Statistics
  • Total Candidates Enrolled : 31.4+ Lakh Students
  • Matric (10th) Candidates : 16.6+ Lakhs
  • Inter (12th) Candidates : 13.0+ Lakhs (Science/Arts/Com)
  • 12th Overall Pass Percentage : 87.21%
  • 10th Overall Pass Percentage : 82.91%
  • Check Mode : Online by Roll Code & Roll Number
Step-by-Step: How to Check Scorecard & Marksheet Online
  1. Click on BSEB Matric 10th or Intermediate 12th direct link below.
  2. Enter your 5-digit School Roll Code.
  3. Enter your official Roll Number as per admit card.
  4. Enter the Captcha code calculation and press Search Result.
  5. Download the subject-wise provisional marksheet for college admissions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I check my official scorecard?

You can check directly using the official server links given in the Useful Important Links table above, or through the government DigiLocker portal.

Q2. What credentials are required to verify the result?

Keep your official examination Roll Number, Date of Birth, and School/Application Number (printed on your original admit card) ready.

Q3. Is the online marksheet valid for admissions?

Yes. The online scorecard or DigiLocker digitally signed certificate serves as valid provisional proof for admission counselling until the physical certificate is released.
