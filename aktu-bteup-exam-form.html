Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) and Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP), Lucknow release odd and even semester examination schedules, carry-over forms, scrutiny/re-evaluation portals, and AKTU One View digital marksheets online. Students enrolled in affiliated engineering, management, pharmacy, and polytechnic colleges can verify examination details and access official ERP portals below.

Important Admission Dates
  • Odd Semester Exam Form : November – December 2026
  • Odd Semester Theory Exams : December 2026 – January 2027
  • Even Semester Exam Form : April – May 2026
  • Scrutiny & Re-evaluation Form : Within 15 Days of Result
  • AKTU One View Result Status : Declared Phase-Wise Online
Eligibility & Fee Structure
  • Courses Covered (AKTU) : B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Arch, MBA, MCA
  • Courses Covered (BTEUP) : 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma & D.Pharm
  • Mandatory Attendance : Minimum 75% Biometric Attendance
  • Student Login Required : Roll No / Enrollment No & DOB
  • Official ERP Portal : erp.aktu.ac.in / bteup.ac.in
Step-by-Step: Admission Registration & Counseling Procedure
  1. Visit AKTU ERP (erp.aktu.ac.in) or BTEUP Student Portal (bteup.ac.in).
  2. Log in using your University Roll Number / Enrollment Number and Password.
  3. Navigate to Examination Form Dashboard and verify your regular and carry-over subjects.
  4. Pay the prescribed semester exam fee or scrutiny fee online before the deadline.
  5. Download your verified Exam Admit Card and check AKTU One View marksheet after evaluation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

You can register directly via the official portal (aktu.ac.in) provided in the Useful Important Links table above.

AapkaResult.in Editorial Analysis: ABC ID Mapping, Carry-Over (COP) Rules & ERP Attendance Verification

1. Mandatory Academic Bank of Credits (ABC ID) Synchronization: Under NEP-2020 compliance enforced by both Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) on erp.aktu.ac.in and the Board of Technical Education UP (BTEUP) on bteup.ac.in, no student can submit an Odd or Even Semester examination form without a verified 12-digit ABC ID generated via DigiLocker. If your Aadhaar name spelling or date of birth differs even slightly from your university ERP profile, the automated API handshake fails—students must resolve Aadhaar demographics first before the exam form portal locks.

2. Carry-Over Paper (COP) & Grace Mark Eligibility Rules: Engineering, Pharmacy, MBA, and Polytechnic students filling back-paper (Carry-Over) forms must carefully cross-check subject codes against the revised CBCS / AICTE syllabus scheme. Remember that odd-semester back papers are ordinarily conducted during odd-semester cycles and even-semester back papers during even cycles, except for final-year special carry-over examinations.

3. Preventing Detention & Admit Card Hold-Ups: Even after successful ERP exam form submission and online fee payment, an institute registrar can withhold hall ticket generation if internal sessional marks, practical attendance (minimum 75% criterion), or college tuition clearance is pending. Always verify that your form status reads "Verified & Forwarded by Institute" on the dashboard at least 7 days prior to exam commencement.
