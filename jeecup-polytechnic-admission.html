UP Polytechnic JEECUP 2026 Diploma Admission & Counseling

Board / University: Joint Entrance Examination Council (Polytechnic), Uttar Pradesh Mode: Online Registration & Counseling Status: Admission Portal Active
Joint Entrance Examination Council (Polytechnic), Uttar Pradesh
UP Polytechnic JEECUP 2026 Diploma Admission & Counseling
Uttar Pradesh State Diploma Engineering, Pharmacy & Lateral Entry Entrance 2026
Authority: Joint Entrance Examination Council (Polytechnic), Uttar Pradesh 🎓 UP POLYTECHNIC ADMISSION & COUNSELING PORTAL

Short Information : The Joint Entrance Examination Council, Uttar Pradesh (JEECUP) conducts the state-level Polytechnic Entrance Examination for admission into Government, Aided, and Private Polytechnic institutes across Uttar Pradesh. Eligible 10th, 12th, and ITI pass candidates can check Group A to K eligibility, counseling rounds, fee structure, and download admit card or rank card via the official portal below.

Important Admission Dates
  • Application Window : January – May 2026
  • JEECUP Entrance Exam Date : June 2026 (CBT Mode)
  • Result & Rank Card Release : June 2026
  • Counseling & Choice Filling : July – August 2026
  • Document Verification & Fee : At Reporting Centers
Eligibility & Fee Structure
  • Group A (Diploma Engineering) : 10th Pass with min. 35% marks
  • Group E (Diploma Pharmacy) : 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Bio/Maths
  • Group K (Lateral Entry 2nd Year) : 10+2 Science / 10th + ITI
  • Application Fee (Gen/OBC) : ₹300/- per group
  • Application Fee (SC/ST) : ₹200/- per group
Step-by-Step: Admission Registration & Counseling Procedure
  1. Visit the official JEECUP portal at jeecup.admissions.nic.in.
  2. Register using your active mobile number, email ID, and Aadhaar details.
  3. Select your preferred exam group (Group A, E, or K) and fill educational qualifications.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and pay the examination fee online.
  5. Appear for CBT exam, download your Rank Card, and participate in online choice locking for UP Polytechnic colleges.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I register online for this admission process?

You can register directly via the official portal (jeecup.admissions.nic.in) provided in the Useful Important Links table above.

Q2. What documents are mandatory for admission counseling?

Keep your Class 10th & 12th marksheets, Transfer Certificate (TC), recent passport-size photo, Aadhaar Card, and valid category certificate (if applicable) ready.
