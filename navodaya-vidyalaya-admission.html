Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) Admission 2026–27

Board / University: Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), Ministry of Education, Govt. of India Mode: Online Registration & Counseling Status: Admission Portal Active
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), Ministry of Education, Govt. of India
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) Admission 2026–27
Class 6 & Class 9 / 11 Lateral Entry Residential School Admission Portal
Authority: Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), Ministry of Education, Govt. of India 🏫 JNVST CLASS 6 & 9 ADMISSION PORTAL LIVE

Short Information : Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) conducts the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) annually for free quality residential education from Class 6 to Class 12 across India. Students studying in Class 5 or Class 8 in recognized schools within the same district are eligible to apply online without any application fee. Check selection criteria, exam pattern, reservation rules, and official portal links below.

Important Admission Dates
  • Class 6 JNVST Registration : July – September 2026
  • Class 9 Lateral Entry Form : September – November 2026
  • Class 6 Phase-1 Exam Date : January 2027
  • Class 9 Lateral Entry Exam : February 2027
  • Merit List & Waitlist Result : March – May 2027
Eligibility & Fee Structure
  • Application Fee (All Categories) : ₹0/- (100% Free Registration)
  • Class 6 Eligibility : Studying in Class 5 in same district
  • Class 9 Eligibility : Studying in Class 8 in same district
  • Rural Seat Reservation : 75% Seats reserved for Rural Students
  • Girls Reservation : Minimum 1/3rd Seats reserved for Girls
Step-by-Step: Admission Registration & Counseling Procedure
  1. Visit the official Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti website at navodaya.gov.in.
  2. Click on "Click here to apply for Class VI / Class IX JNVST Admission".
  3. Download the Headmaster-verified certificate format, fill it, and get it signed by your current school Principal.
  4. Upload the signed certificate, candidate photo, and parent signature online.
  5. Submit the form completely free of cost and save the registration number for downloading the JNVST Admit Card.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I register online for this admission process?

You can register directly via the official portal (navodaya.gov.in) provided in the Useful Important Links table above.

Q2. What documents are mandatory for admission counseling?

Keep your Class 10th & 12th marksheets, Transfer Certificate (TC), recent passport-size photo, Aadhaar Card, and valid category certificate (if applicable) ready.
