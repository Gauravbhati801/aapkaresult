How to Check Exam Results and Download Scorecards

  1. Locate your respective examination in the results directory table above and click "Check Result" or "Official Status".
  2. On the official commission portal, click on the examination link.
  3. Enter your Roll Number, Registration Number, and Date of Birth as printed on your Hall Ticket.
  4. Review your subject-wise marks, qualifying status, and normalized score.
  5. Download and print at least two copies of the scorecard for your upcoming Document Verification or Counseling stages.

Frequently Asked Questions (Exam Results)

Q1. What should I do if the official result portal is not opening?

Due to high visitor traffic upon declaration, government servers occasionally experience congestion. Please wait 10 to 15 minutes, clear your browser cache, or check using alternate mirror links provided on AapkaResult.in.

Q2. How long are entrance exam scorecards valid?

Entrance exam scorecards like CUET UG, JEE Main, and NEET UG are valid for the immediate academic admission session, whereas eligibility certificates like CTET and UP-TET possess lifetime validity.
