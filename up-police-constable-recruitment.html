Editorial Deep-Dive: UP Police Constable Civil & PAC Recruitment

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Civil Police Constables handle police station beat patrols, register initial complaint reports, manage law & order, VIP convoy escort duties, and crime scene cordon. PAC (Provincial Armed Constabulary) personnel are deployed across the state for riot control, large-scale festival security, assembly elections, and disaster relief operations. Recruits undergo 6 months of rigorous residential basic training at designated Police Training Colleges (PTC).

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Recruited under Pay Matrix Level-3 (Basic Pay ₹21,700). Adding 50% Dearness Allowance (₹10,850), HRA (₹1,200 to ₹3,600), Uniform Maintenance Allowance, and Ration Allowance, gross monthly earnings reach approximately ₹38,200. UP Police personnel also receive an extra 13th-month bonus salary compensation annually. The monthly in-hand net starting salary ranges between ₹34,200 and ₹36,500 after NPS pension deductions.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Across historical 300-mark examinations (150 questions, each carrying 2 marks with 0.5 negative marking), competitive qualifying benchmarks have stabilized: Unreserved (UR): 215–225 marks; OBC: 206–216 marks; EWS: 200–210 marks; SC: 182–192 marks; Female candidates: 196–206 marks. To safeguard your merit ranking despite normalization, set your preparation target at 225+ marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

Samanya Hindi carries 37 questions (74 marks) and offers the highest scoring return on investment; aim for at least 32+ correct answers. Mental Aptitude & Reasoning carries 37 questions (74 marks) where diagrammatic reasoning, blood relations, and coding-decoding can be solved rapidly. Begin physical endurance training early to complete the mandatory 4.8 km run in under 25 minutes (2.4 km in 14 minutes for females).

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Physical Standards are non-negotiable: Male candidates must meet the minimum height requirement of 168 cm (160 cm for ST) and unexpanded chest of 79 cm with mandatory 5 cm expansion. Female candidates must measure at least 152 cm with a minimum body weight of 40 kg. All caste and domicile certificates must adhere strictly to UP government prescribed formats with active verification stamps.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can candidates from other states apply for UP Police Constable recruitment?

Yes, Indian citizens from any state or union territory can apply for UP Police Constable recruitment. However, non-UP residents will be treated under the Unreserved (General) category and will not be eligible for caste or age relaxations.

Q2. Is there negative marking in the UP Police Constable written exam?

Yes. There is a negative marking of 0.50 marks for each incorrect answer in the OMR-based written examination. Each question carries 2 marks.

Q3. What is the educational qualification required for UP Police Constable?

The candidate must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from any recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

Q4. What is the physical running requirement for male and female candidates?

Male candidates must complete a 4.8 km run in 25 minutes. Female candidates must complete a 2.4 km run in 14 minutes. The running test is purely qualifying in nature.

Q5. What is the monthly in-hand salary of a UP Police Constable?

A UP Police Constable is recruited under Pay Level-3 with a basic pay of ₹21,700. Including Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Ration Money, and allowances, the gross monthly salary is approximately ₹30,000 to ₹34,000.
