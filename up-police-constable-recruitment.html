26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can candidates from other states apply for UP Police Constable recruitment?

Yes, Indian citizens from any state or union territory can apply for UP Police Constable recruitment. However, non-UP residents will be treated under the Unreserved (General) category and will not be eligible for caste or age relaxations.

Q2. Is there negative marking in the UP Police Constable written exam?

Yes. There is a negative marking of 0.50 marks for each incorrect answer in the OMR-based written examination. Each question carries 2 marks.

Q3. What is the educational qualification required for UP Police Constable?

The candidate must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from any recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

Q4. What is the physical running requirement for male and female candidates?

Male candidates must complete a 4.8 km run in 25 minutes. Female candidates must complete a 2.4 km run in 14 minutes. The running test is purely qualifying in nature.

Q5. What is the monthly in-hand salary of a UP Police Constable?

A UP Police Constable is recruited under Pay Level-3 with a basic pay of ₹21,700. Including Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Ration Money, and allowances, the gross monthly salary is approximately ₹30,000 to ₹34,000.
