IBPS RRB Officer & Office Assistant Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 9,000+ Posts

Department: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), Mumbai Post Name: Office Assistant (Multipurpose / Clerk) & Officer Scale-I (Assistant Manager / PO), Scale-II & III Status: Application Closed (Ended 21 September 2026) • Prelims Exam Nov/Dec 2026 & Allotment Live
Share Alert:
Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), Mumbai: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts the Common Recruitment Process (CRP RRBs) for recruitment of Office Assistants (Multipurpose) and Officers (Scale-I, II, and III) in 43 Regional Rural Banks across India. 8 & 9. Educational Qualification & Post-Wise Eligibility

General Requirement: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or equivalent. Candidate must have proficiency in the local language of the State/UT where the participating Regional Rural Bank is located. Working knowledge of Computer is desirable.

Post Name / Cadre Total Vacancies Minimum Prescribed Educational Qualification

Officer Scale-I (Assistant Manager / PO) 3,500+ Posts Bachelor's Degree in any discipline + Local Language proficiency.
Officer Scale-II (Specialist / General Banking) 500+ Posts Bachelor's Degree with 50% marks + 2 years banking experience.

10. Physical Standards (PST / PET)

Non-Physical Desk Role: Not Applicable. Banking positions are administrative financial roles with no physical running or height/chest tests.

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Preliminary Online Examination (Reasoning & Quantitative Aptitude / Numerical Ability)
  2. Stage 2: Mains Online Examination (Reasoning, Computer Knowledge, General Awareness, English/Hindi, Quant)
  3. Stage 3: Common Interview (only for Officer Scale-I, II, and III; NO INTERVIEW for Office Assistant)
  4. Stage 4: Provisional State-Wise Allotment to participating Regional Rural Banks

12. Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

Exam Mode: Online Computer Based Test (Prelims & Mains) • Duration: Prelims: 45 Minutes | Mains: 120 Minutes • Total Questions: Prelims: 80 Questions | Mains: 200 Questions • Total Marks: Prelims: 80 Marks | Mains: 200 Marks • Negative Marking: 1/4th (0.25) Marks deducted for each incorrect answer
Subject / Examination Section Total Questions Maximum Marks
Prelims: Reasoning (40 Qs | 40 M) + Numerical Ability / Quant (40 Qs | 40 M) 80 80
Mains: Reasoning (40 Qs | 50 M), Computer Knowledge (40 Qs | 20 M), General Awareness (40 Qs | 40 M), English/Hindi (40 Qs | 40 M), Quant (40 Qs | 50 M) 200 200

13. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

📚 Reasoning (Prelims & Mains)
Puzzles and Seating Arrangement (Circular, Linear, Floor, Box), Syllogism, Inequality, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Coding-Decoding, Order & Ranking, Alpha-Numeric-Symbol Series, Input-Output, Logical/Critical Reasoning.
📚 Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
Simplification and Approximation, Number Series (Missing & Wrong), Quadratic Equations, Data Interpretation (Bar Graph, Line Graph, Tabular, Pie Chart, Caselet), Arithmetic Topics (Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Average, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Mensuration).
📚 General & Banking Awareness
Current Affairs (National & International), Banking & Financial Awareness, RBI Policy Rates, Types of Accounts, Priority Sector Lending (PSL), Regional Rural Banks History & Regulations, Government Welfare Schemes, Headquarters of Banks, Currencies and Capitals.
📚 English / Hindi Language & Computer Knowledge
Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Detection, Para Jumbles, Fillers, Hindi/English Grammar and Vocabulary. Computer: Hardware, Software, Networking, Internet, MS Office (Word, Excel, PPT), Computer Abbreviations & Shortcuts.

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in.
  2. Step 2: Click on 'CRP RRBs' and select the recruitment link for Office Assistant or Officer Scale-I.
  3. Step 3: Click 'Click here for New Registration' and enter your basic details.
  4. Step 4: Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  5. Step 5: Fill in your educational qualifications, select state/circle, and choose RRB preferences.
  6. Step 6: Pay the application fee (₹850 for Gen/OBC/EWS; ₹175 for SC/ST/PwD) through online payment gateway.
  7. Step 7: Submit the application and download the application confirmation printout.

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates can apply for BOTH Office Assistant (Multipurpose) and Officer Scale-I separately by paying the fee for each post.
  • Candidates must choose the State where they are proficient in the local language.
  • There is NO interview for Office Assistant (Clerk); final merit is based 100% on marks in the Mains examination.
  • For Officer Scale-I (PO), the ratio of weightage for Mains Exam and Interview is 80:20.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can a candidate apply for both Office Assistant and Officer Scale-I in IBPS RRB?

Yes, a candidate can apply for both Office Assistant (Multipurpose) and Officer Scale-I (PO). However, separate applications and application fees are required for each post.

Q2. Is there an interview for IBPS RRB Clerk (Office Assistant)?

No. There is no interview for the Office Assistant post. Final selection and allotment are based strictly on marks scored in the Mains examination.

Q3. Is knowledge of the local language mandatory for IBPS RRBs?

Yes. Candidates must have proficiency (reading, writing, and speaking) in the local language of the state/UT in which the participating Regional Rural Bank operates.
