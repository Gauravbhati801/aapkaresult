Editorial Deep-Dive: UP Anganwadi Karyakatri & Sahayika Bharti

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Recruited under the Department of Women and Child Development (Bal Vikas Seva Evam Pushtahar), Government of Uttar Pradesh. Anganwadi Workers (Karyakatri) manage rural child development centers, distribute nutritious supplementary food (Pushtahar) to pregnant women and infants, conduct preschool non-formal education for children aged 3–6, and maintain health vaccination tracking cards.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Anganwadi Workers and Helpers receive a monthly consolidated honorarium funded jointly by Central and State Governments. Anganwadi Workers receive approximately ₹7,500 to ₹9,000 per month including performance-linked incentive bonuses (PLI). Helpers (Sahayika) receive approximately ₹4,000 to ₹5,000 per month with annual welfare allowances.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Selection is made 100% on the basis of academic merit without any written exam. A composite merit score is calculated from Class 10th (High School) and Class 12th (Intermediate) percentage marks. Preference is given to widows, divorcees, and BPL families residing permanently within the exact same Gram Sabha or urban ward.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

Because there is no written test, success depends entirely on applying for the exact ward or gram sabha of permanent residence and submitting accurate educational percentage data. Candidates must confirm their name is listed in the rural Parivar Register or urban ward electoral roll before submitting the online form.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Mandatory eligibility requires the candidate to be a female resident of the concerned Gram Sabha/Ward. The income certificate for BPL category priority must be issued by the concerned Tehsil authority with an annual income below the designated rural/urban poverty limit. Keep caste and resident certificates updated.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can male candidates apply for UP Anganwadi Bharti?

No. Anganwadi Worker and Helper positions are exclusively reserved for eligible female candidates.

Q2. Is there any exam or interview for UP Anganwadi recruitment?

No. Selection is 100% direct and merit-based, calculated by quality points based on 10th and 12th board percentages, with statutory priority for widows and BPL families.

Q3. Can a candidate from another village apply for an Anganwadi post?

No. The candidate must be a permanent resident of the specific Gram Sabha (for rural areas) or Ward (for urban areas) where the vacancy is advertised.
