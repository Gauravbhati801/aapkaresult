26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can outside Delhi OBC candidates claim OBC reservation in DSSSB?

No. According to Delhi Government policy, OBC reservations are granted ONLY to candidates holding OBC certificates issued by the Govt of NCT of Delhi. OBC candidates from other states are treated as General/UR.

Q2. What is the examination scheme for DSSSB MTS?

The exam is a One-Tier Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, divided across 5 sections (General Awareness, Reasoning, Maths, Hindi, and English) with 40 questions each.

Q3. Is there any typing test for the MTS post?

No. There is no typing or skill test for the Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) post. Typing tests apply only to Junior Assistant and Stenographer positions.
