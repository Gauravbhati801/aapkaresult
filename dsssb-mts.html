Editorial Deep-Dive: DSSSB Delhi Multi Tasking Staff (MTS)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

MTS personnel are deployed across key departments of the Government of NCT of Delhi, including Delhi Secretariat, Directorate of Education, Social Welfare Department, and Municipal Corporation. Duties include dairy dispatch of files, digitizing office documents, assisting supervisory staff, operating office equipment, and assisting public grievance delivery counters with fixed 5-day week office schedules.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Recruited under 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level-1 (Basic Pay ₹18,000). Because Delhi is classified as a Tier-1 (X Class) metropolitan city, House Rent Allowance is paid at the maximum 30% rate (₹5,400). Adding 50% DA (₹9,000) and Transport Allowance (₹1,350 + DA = ₹2,025), gross monthly salary reaches ₹34,425. Starting net in-hand salary is approximately ₹30,800 to ₹32,000/month.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

DSSSB Tier-1 exam consists of 200 questions for 200 marks with 0.25 negative marking. Given Delhi postings and attractive living conditions, competition is very high. Previous DSSSB MTS qualifying cut-offs for UR stood at 142–150 marks; OBC (Delhi only): 128–136 marks; EWS: 135–142 marks; SC: 124–132 marks; ST: 110–118 marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The 200-question test is divided into 5 equal sections of 40 marks each: General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Arithmetical & Numerical Ability, Test of Hindi Language & Comprehension, and Test of English Language & Comprehension. Hindi and English sections are rapid scoring zones; practice reading comprehension and grammar to secure 70+ marks combined.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Crucial OBC eligibility rule: Only OBC certificates issued by the Revenue Department of the Government of NCT of Delhi are accepted for OBC reservation in DSSSB. OBC candidates from other states are treated strictly as Unreserved (General) category candidates. EWS and SC/ST certificates from all recognized authorities nationwide are valid.

Target 32+ marks.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can outside Delhi OBC candidates claim OBC reservation in DSSSB?

No. According to Delhi Government policy, OBC reservations are granted ONLY to candidates holding OBC certificates issued by the Govt of NCT of Delhi. OBC candidates from other states are treated as General/UR.

Q2. What is the examination scheme for DSSSB MTS?

The exam is a One-Tier Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, divided across 5 sections (General Awareness, Reasoning, Maths, Hindi, and English) with 40 questions each.

Q3. Is there any typing test for the MTS post?

No. There is no typing or skill test for the Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) post. Typing tests apply only to Junior Assistant and Stenographer positions.
