DSSSB Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 714 Posts

Department: Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB), Government of NCT of Delhi Post Name: Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS), Junior Assistant & Stenographer Status: Application Portal Active

8 & 9. Educational Qualification & Post-Wise Eligibility

General Requirement: Candidates must have passed 10th Standard (Matriculation) from a recognized Board/University for MTS. For Junior Assistant posts: 12th pass with typing speed of 35 wpm in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on computer.

10. Physical Standards (PST / PET)

Non-Physical Desk Role: Not Applicable. DSSSB MTS is a general civil administrative office support post with no physical endurance or height/chest tests.

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Tier-I One-Tier Computer Based Examination (CBT - 200 Questions, 200 Marks)
  2. Stage 2: Skill Test / Typing Test (applicable only for Junior Assistant and Stenographer posts)
  3. Stage 3: e-Dossier submission and Document Verification of shortlisted candidates
  4. Stage 4: Final Department Allocation and Appointment

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the DSSSB online application portal at dsssbonline.nic.in.
  2. Step 2: If a new user, register by clicking 'Click for New Registration' with Class 10th Roll Number, Passing Year, and Date of Birth.
  3. Step 3: Login using your 10th Roll Number, DOB, and password.
  4. Step 4: Click on 'Apply' against Advertisement Post Code for Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS).
  5. Step 5: Fill in your educational details, category, and department preferences.
  6. Step 6: Upload postcard-size photograph (5x7 inches), standard passport photo, signature, and left/right thumb impressions.
  7. Step 7: Pay the application fee (₹100 for Gen/OBC/EWS Male; ₹0 for SC/ST/Female) via SBI e-Pay.
  8. Step 8: Submit the application and print the confirmation page for your records.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th Class (Matriculation) Certificate & Marksheet

  • Aadhaar Card or other Photo Identity Card

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Only OBC certificates issued by the Government of NCT of Delhi are accepted for OBC reservation. OBC candidates from other states are treated as Unreserved/General.
  • DSSSB does not allow any changes to the online application after final submission; verify all details carefully.
  • Postcard size photograph upload is compulsory as per DSSSB instructions.
  • Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies to all objective questions.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can outside Delhi OBC candidates claim OBC reservation in DSSSB?

No. According to Delhi Government policy, OBC reservations are granted ONLY to candidates holding OBC certificates issued by the Govt of NCT of Delhi. OBC candidates from other states are treated as General/UR.

Q2. What is the examination scheme for DSSSB MTS?

The exam is a One-Tier Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, divided across 5 sections (General Awareness, Reasoning, Maths, Hindi, and English) with 40 questions each.

Q3. Is there any typing test for the MTS post?

No. There is no typing or skill test for the Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) post. Typing tests apply only to Junior Assistant and Stenographer positions.
