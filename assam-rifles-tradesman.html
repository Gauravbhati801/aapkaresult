Assam Rifles Technical & Tradesman Recruitment Rally 2026: Apply Online for 354 Posts

Recruiting Board: Directorate General Assam Rifles, Shillong Advt Date: 03 November 2026 Total Vacancy: 354 Posts Status: Online Application Live (15 Nov – 14 Dec 2026)
Directorate General Assam Rifles, Shillong (Ministry of Home Affairs)
Assam Rifles Technical & Tradesman Recruitment Rally 2026
Warrant Officer, Havildar & Rifleman (Group B & C) Vacancies • Rally from 18 Jan 2027
Authority: Office of DG Assam Rifles, Shillong 🔥 354 POSTS ONLINE FORM LIVE NOW

Short Information : The Directorate General Assam Rifles, Shillong has released the official notification for the Assam Rifles Technical and Tradesman Recruitment Rally 2026. Online applications are invited from eligible Male Indian Citizens to fill 354 Group-B and Group-C vacancies across 17 diverse technical and tradesman categories including Warrant Officer (Cipher), Personal Assistant, Religious Teacher, Lineman Field, Plumber, Surveyor, Pharmacist, and Rifleman trades. The recruitment rally is scheduled to commence on 18 January 2027 onwards at Jorhat and Diphu (Assam). Interested candidates can read the complete official notification, trade-wise eligibility, physical standards (PST/PET), and apply online through the official portal before 14 December 2026.

Important Dates
  • Official Notification Date : 03 November 2026
  • Online Application Start Date : 15 November 2026
  • Last Date to Apply Online : 14 December 2026 (11:59 PM)
  • Last Date for Fee Payment : 14 December 2026

  • Rally Venues : Jorhat & Diphu, Assam
  • Admit Card / Call Letter : January 2027 (1st Week)
Application Fee & Mode
  • Group B Posts (Religious Teacher, etc.) : ₹200/-
  • Group C Posts (Tradesman / Technical) : ₹100/-
  • SC / ST / ESM / Female Candidates : ₹0/- (Exempted)
  • Payment Mode : Online via SBI Current A/C Mechanism
  • Account Details : HQ DGAR Recruitment Branch, SBI A/C No. 37088046712
Assam Rifles Rally 2026: Age Limit (As on 01 July 2026)
  • General Trades Age Limit: 18 to 23 Years (Candidate must not have been born earlier than 01 August 2003 and later than 01 August 2008).
  • Warrant Officer (Cipher / PA / Draughtsman): 18 to 25 Years.
  • Religious Teacher: 18 to 30 Years.
  • Surveyor ITC & Pharmacist: 20 to 28 Years / 20 to 25 Years.
  • Age Relaxation: OBC candidates get +3 Years relaxation, SC/ST candidates get +5 Years relaxation as per Central Government of India rules.

Assam Rifles Recruitment 2026: Stage-Wise Selection Process
  1. Stage 1: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET) – Height, chest, and 5 Km running test (qualifying in nature).
  2. Stage 2: Written Examination – 100 Marks objective question paper covering English, General Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, and General Knowledge / Trade Technical Awareness. Qualifying marks: 35% for Gen/EWS, 33% for SC/ST/OBC.
  3. Stage 3: Trade / Skill Test – Practical demonstration of technical trade expertise (qualifying in nature, no marks awarded towards final merit).
  4. Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) – Verification of original certificates, educational qualifications, caste/category, and domicile.
  5. Stage 5: Detailed Medical Examination (DME) & Review Medical (RME) – Medical fitness assessment by Assam Rifles medical board.
  6. Stage 6: Final Merit List – Prepared strictly on the basis of marks obtained in the Written Examination subject to clearing all qualifying stages.
Step-by-Step: How to Apply Online for Assam Rifles Tradesman 2026
  1. Visit the official Assam Rifles portal at assamrifles.gov.in or click on the direct Apply Online link below.
  2. Select your State of Domicile and choose the Trade/Post you are applying for.
  3. Fill in your basic personal details, permanent and correspondence address, caste category, and mobile/email address.
  4. Upload clear scanned copies of your passport-size photograph, signature, and educational/trade certificate.
  5. Pay the application fee (₹100 for Gr C / ₹200 for Gr B) online through the designated SBI payment gateway. SC/ST/ESM/Female candidates are exempt.
  6. Verify all entered details carefully before final submission. After submission, print the acknowledgment receipt and application form for the rally center.
