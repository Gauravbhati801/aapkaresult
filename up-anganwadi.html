UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 – Apply for 53,289 Worker & Helper Posts

Department: Women and Child Development Department (WCD), Government of Uttar Pradesh Post Name: Anganwadi Karyakarti (Worker), Mini Anganwadi Worker & Anganwadi Sahayika (Helper) Status: District-Wise Merit Selection Active
Share Alert: WhatsApp Telegram
Women and Child Development Department (WCD), Government of Uttar Pradesh: Women and Child Development Department (WCD), Government of Uttar Pradesh has invited online applications for direct recruitment to 53,289 vacancies of Anganwadi Worker (Karyakarti) and Helper (Sahayika) across all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. Female candidates with 10th or 12th pass qualifications residing in the respective Gram Sabha or Ward can apply online with zero application fee.

Age Limit 18 Years - 35 Years
Salary / Pay Level Honorarium System (Monthly Allowance)

8 & 9. Educational Qualification & Post-Wise Eligibility

General Requirement: Anganwadi Worker (Karyakarti) & Mini Worker: Minimum 10+2 (Intermediate) passed from recognized board. Anganwadi Helper (Sahayika): Minimum 10th (High School) passed from recognized board. Candidate must be a permanent resident of the respective Gram Sabha (Rural) or Ward (Urban).

10. Physical Standards (PST / PET)

Non-Physical Desk Role: Not Applicable. Anganwadi positions are community childcare, nutrition, and pre-school education roles with no physical endurance or height tests.

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Preparation of District-Wise Merit Points based on educational percentage (10th, 12th, and Graduation marks divided by 10)
  2. Stage 2: Application of statutory priority reservation (Widows, Divorced/Destitute women, BPL cardholders receive legal priority)
  3. Stage 3: Verification of documents and domicile by District Selection Committee headed by District Magistrate (DM)
  4. Stage 4: Publication of Final Merit List and issue of appointment orders

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official UP Anganwadi portal at upanganwadibharti.in.
  2. Step 2: Select your District, Project (Block), Sector, and Gram Sabha / Ward.
  3. Step 3: Register using your active mobile number and Aadhaar details.
  4. Step 4: Enter marks of High School (10th) and Intermediate (12th) accurately.
  5. Step 5: Upload passport-size photograph, signature, domicile certificate, and income certificate.
  6. Step 6: Review the details and submit the application online with zero fee.
  7. Step 7: Print the application form for document verification.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th (High School) Marksheet & Certificate
  • 12th (Intermediate) Marksheet & Certificate
  • Graduation Marksheet (if applicable)
  • Residence / Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra) certifying residence in the specific Gram Sabha / Ward
  • Family Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra) issued by Tehsildar
  • Caste Certificate (Jati Praman Patra) for OBC, SC, ST candidates
  • BPL Ration Card or Widow/Divorcee legal certificate (if claiming priority)
  • Aadhaar Card of the applicant

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Only female candidates residing in the respective Gram Sabha or Urban Ward where the vacancy exists are eligible to apply.
  • Annual family income must not exceed the prescribed ceiling (₹46,080 for rural; ₹56,460 for urban) as per UP Government norms.
  • Widows, legally divorced women, and destitute women residing in the same village get first priority in selection.
  • There is NO application fee for any category.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can male candidates apply for UP Anganwadi Bharti?

No. Anganwadi Worker and Helper positions are exclusively reserved for eligible female candidates.

Q2. Is there any exam or interview for UP Anganwadi recruitment?

No. Selection is 100% direct and merit-based, calculated by quality points based on 10th and 12th board percentages, with statutory priority for widows and BPL families.

Q3. Can a candidate from another village apply for an Anganwadi post?

No. The candidate must be a permanent resident of the specific Gram Sabha (for rural areas) or Ward (for urban areas) where the vacancy is advertised.
