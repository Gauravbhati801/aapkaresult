Department: Women and Child Development Department (WCD), Government of Uttar Pradesh Post Name: Anganwadi Karyakarti (Worker), Mini Anganwadi Worker & Anganwadi Sahayika (Helper) Status: District-Wise Rolling Merit Selection Active

District-Wise Rolling Recruitment (53,289 Posts) : Short Details of Notification
Post Date / Update: 05 March 2026 | 11:00 AM 🎉 53,289 Posts • Female Only (No Fee)

Short Information : Bal Vikas Seva Evam Pushtahar Vibhag, Uttar Pradesh has announced rolling online recruitment for 53,289 vacancies of Anganwadi Karyakatri (Workers) and Anganwadi Sahayika (Helpers) across all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. Selection is purely merit-based on intermediate marks for female residents of the respective village/ward. Check eligibility and apply online below.

  • Minimum Age : 18 Years.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can male candidates apply for UP Anganwadi Bharti?

No. Anganwadi Worker and Helper positions are exclusively reserved for eligible female candidates.

Q2. Is there any exam or interview for UP Anganwadi recruitment?

No. Selection is 100% direct and merit-based, calculated by quality points based on 10th and 12th board percentages, with statutory priority for widows and BPL families.

Q3. Can a candidate from another village apply for an Anganwadi post?

No. The candidate must be a permanent resident of the specific Gram Sabha (for rural areas) or Ward (for urban areas) where the vacancy is advertised.
