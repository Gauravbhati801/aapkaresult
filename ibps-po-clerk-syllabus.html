IBPS Bank PO & Clerk Prelims + Mains Complete Syllabus & Exam Pattern 2026

Board / Commission: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), Mumbai Mode: Official Portal Guide Status: Live & Verified 2026
Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), Mumbai
IBPS Bank PO & Clerk Prelims + Mains Complete Syllabus & Exam Pattern 2026
CRP PO/MT & CRP CSA (Clerical Cadre) Selection Blueprint & Topic Weightage
Exam Authority: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), Mumbai 📘 IBPS PO & CLERK SYLLABUS 2026 COMPLETE GUIDE

Short Information : The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts annual CRP recruitments for Probationary Officers (PO/MT) and Customer Service Associates (Clerk) across 11 Public Sector Banks in India. Understanding the sectional timing, negative marking (0.25 marks), descriptive essay/letter writing (for PO), and high-weightage Reasoning, Quant & Banking Awareness topics is essential for clearing sectional and overall cutoffs.

Prelims Pattern (100 Marks | 60 Min)
  • English Language : 30 Questions | 30 Marks (20 Min)
  • Numerical Ability / Quant : 35 Questions | 35 Marks (20 Min)
  • Reasoning Ability : 35 Questions | 35 Marks (20 Min)
  • Negative Marking : 0.25 Marks (1/4th Penalty)
  • Prelims Nature : Qualifying (Shortlisting for Mains)
Mains Exam Structure (PO & Clerk)
  • Reasoning & Computer Aptitude : 45 Qs / 60 Marks (PO) | 50 Qs / 60 Marks (Clerk)
  • General / Economy / Banking Awareness : 40 Qs / 40 Marks (PO) | 50 Qs / 50 Marks (Clerk)
  • English Language : 35 Qs / 40 Marks (PO) | 40 Qs / 40 Marks (Clerk)
  • Data Analysis & Interpretation : 35 Qs / 60 Marks (PO) | 50 Qs / 50 Marks (Clerk)
  • Descriptive Test (PO Only) : 2 Qs (Letter + Essay) / 25 Marks (30 Min)
Subject-Wise High-Weightage Syllabus Topics
  1. Reasoning Ability: Seating Arrangement, Puzzles (Floor, Box, Month), Syllogism, Inequality, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Input-Output, and Critical Reasoning.
  2. Quantitative Aptitude / DI: Simplification & Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, Pie/Bar/Line/Caselet DI, Percentage, Ratio, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, and Time & Work.
  3. English Language: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, and Vocabulary/Connectors.
  4. Banking & Financial Awareness: RBI Monetary Policy, Repo/Reverse Repo, Basel Norms, NPAs, Union Budget, UPI/NPCI Initiatives, and Last 6 Months Current Affairs.
  5. Final Selection: Clerk selection is 100% based on Mains marks (LPT mandatory); PO selection combines Mains (80% weightage) + Interview (20% weightage).

AapkaResult.in Editorial Analysis: IBPS Sectional Timing Strategy, Prelims vs. Mains Weightage & Descriptive Scoring

1. Dual Cutoff Rule (Sectional + Overall) & 20-Minute Sectional Locks: Unlike SSC exams where candidates can switch freely between subjects for 60 minutes, IBPS PO and IBPS Clerk Prelims enforce a strict 20-minute non-transferable timer per section (English: 20 mins, Quantitative Aptitude: 20 mins, Reasoning Ability: 20 mins) AND require you to clear both the individual Sectional Cutoff and the Overall State/All-India Cutoff. In Prelims, speed arithmetic (Simplification, Quadratic Equations, Number Series) and 3-to-4 seating/floor puzzles decide 70% of your score.

2. Final Selection Weightage Rule (PO 80:20 vs. Clerk 100% Mains): Candidates must remember that Prelims marks are purely qualifying in both exams:
• For IBPS Clerk (CSA): There is NO interview; 100% of final bank allotment merit is prepared directly from your 200-mark Mains CBT score.
• For IBPS PO / MT: Final merit combines your Mains score (225 marks incl. 25-mark English Descriptive Letter & Essay) and Personal Interview (100 marks) in an exact 80:20 weightage ratio.

3. The Game-Changer in Mains—General/Financial Awareness (GA): While Data Analysis & High-Level Reasoning in PO Mains often have low sectional cutoffs (8 to 14 marks) due to lengthy caselets, General/Economy/Banking Awareness (last 5 months' RBI circulars, Monetary Policy, Static Banking Terms, Union Budget) offers 35 to 40 rapid marks in under 20 minutes with zero calculation fatigue.
