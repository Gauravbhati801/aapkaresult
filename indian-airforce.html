Indian Airforce Agniveer Vayu Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 3,500+ Posts

Department: Indian Air Force (IAF), Ministry of Defence, Government of India Post Name: Agniveer Vayu (Intake 01/2026 - 01/2027) - Science & Non-Science Streams Status: Intake Notification Active

Indian Air Force (IAF), Ministry of Defence, Government of India: Indian Air Force (IAF) invites online applications from eligible unmarried Indian male and female candidates to join the Indian Air Force as Agniveer Vayu under the Agnipath Scheme. 10+2 Intermediate qualified candidates in Science or Non-Science streams can apply online through the official agnipathvayu.cdac.in portal.

8 & 9. Educational Qualification & Post-Wise Eligibility

General Requirement: Science Subjects: 10+2 Intermediate with Mathematics, Physics, and English with minimum 50% aggregate marks and 50% in English OR 3-Year Engineering Diploma with 50% aggregate. Other Than Science Subjects: 10+2 Intermediate in any stream with minimum 50% aggregate marks and 50% in English.

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Phase 1: Online Computer Based Examination (Phase-I Test)
  2. Phase 2: Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Test-I & Adaptability Test-II
  3. Phase 3: Mandatory Medical Examination at Air Force Medical Centers
  4. Phase 4: Provisional Select List (PSL) & Final Enrollment List

13. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

📚 English (Common for Both Streams)
Comprehension, Grammar (Subject-Verb Agreement, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Tenses), Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms, One Word), Sentence Transformation, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect.
📚 Mathematics (Science Stream)
Sets, Relations, Functions, Trigonometry, Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Matrices, Calculus, Vectors, Probability, Coordinate Geometry.
📚 Physics (Science Stream)
Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy & Power, Gravitation, Thermodynamics, Optics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Modern Physics.
📚 RAGA (Reasoning & General Awareness - Non-Science)
Numerical Series, Mathematical Operations, Analogy, Odd One Out, Coding-Decoding, Non-verbal reasoning, GK, General Science, Civics, Geography, Current Affairs, Basic Computer.

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Open the official IAF Agnipath Vayu recruitment portal at agnipathvayu.cdac.in.
  2. Step 2: Click on 'Candidate Login' and select 'Register' to create your account.
  3. Step 3: Enter your 10th & 10+2 marks and choose whether applying for Science, Other than Science, or Both.
  4. Step 4: Upload your passport photograph (holding black slate with name and date in chalk), signature, and thumb impression.
  5. Step 5: Select 5 preferred Phase-I exam cities.
  6. Step 6: Pay the registration fee online (₹250 + GST) and submit.
  7. Step 7: Download and print the finalized Application Form.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • Class 10th / Matriculation Passing Certificate (for Date of Birth proof)
  • 10+2 (Intermediate) Marksheet and Passing Certificate or Engineering Diploma Certificate
  • Passport size color photograph (taken with candidate holding black slate with name and date of photograph)
  • Scanned copy of signature and left thumb impression
  • Aadhaar Card of the applicant

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Only UNMARRIED male and female candidates are eligible for enrollment.
  • Photograph must show the candidate holding a black slate in front of their chest with their name and date of photo taken clearly written in white chalk.
  • Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies to all objective questions.
  • Up to 25% of Agniveer Vayu will be enrolled in the regular cadre of the Indian Air Force.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can female candidates apply for Indian Airforce Agniveer Vayu?

Yes, unmarried female candidates are fully eligible to apply for Indian Air Force Agniveer Vayu recruitment across both Science and Non-Science streams.

Q2. What is the minimum height required for IAF Agniveer Vayu?

The minimum acceptable height is 152.5 cm for male candidates and 152 cm for female candidates.

Q3. Can Arts or Commerce students apply for Airforce Agniveer?

Yes. Students from Arts, Commerce, or Humanities can apply under the 'Other Than Science Subjects' stream provided they have secured minimum 50% marks in aggregate and 50% marks in English in Class 12th.
