Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor & PhD Eligibility Test
Exam Authority: National Testing Agency (NTA) & University Grants Commission (UGC) 🔑 NTA UGC NET RESPONSE SHEET & KEY PORTAL

Short Information : The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts UGC NET twice a year across 83 subjects for awarding Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor eligibility, and PhD admission. Appeared candidates can access their recorded response sheets, provisional answer keys, question IDs, and subject-wise cutoffs via the official NTA portal.

Answer Key & Objection Schedule
  • Exam Cycle : June & December Cycles (CBT Mode)
  • Provisional Key & Response Sheet : Available via Application No
  • Challenge Fee : ₹200/- per Question (Non-Refundable)
  • Final Key Basis : Reviewed by Subject Expert Panel
  • Result Format : Marks & Percentile (JRF / Asst Prof / PhD)
NTA UGC NET Marking Scheme
  • Paper-1 (Teaching & Research) : 50 Questions / 100 Marks
  • Paper-2 (Subject Specific) : 100 Questions / 200 Marks
  • Total Exam Duration : 3 Hours (180 Minutes, No Break)
  • Correct Answer : +2 Marks per Question
  • Negative Marking : NO Negative Marking (0 Deduction)
How to Check NTA UGC NET Recorded Response & Answer Key
  1. Visit ugcnet.nta.ac.in and click on "Display of Provisional Answer Key(s) & Question Paper with Recorded Responses".
  2. Sign in using your NTA Application Number and Date of Birth / Password.
  3. Download both your Question Paper PDF (with Question IDs & Chosen Option IDs) and the Provisional Answer Key table.
  4. Compare your Chosen Option ID against the Correct Option ID listed against each Question ID.
  5. If any discrepancy is found, upload supporting academic textbook proof and submit your challenge before the closing time.

AapkaResult.in Editorial Analysis: UGC NET 3-Category Qualification Rule (JRF, Assistant Professor & Ph.D. Admission)

1. How the Revised 3-Tier UGC NET Result System Works: Under the UGC National Education Policy (NEP-2020) notification implemented by NTA on ugcnet.nta.ac.in, candidates are evaluated across three distinct eligibility tiers from a single 300-mark (Paper-1: 100 marks + Paper-2: 200 marks, zero negative marking) score:
• Category-1 (JRF + Assistant Professor + Ph.D.): Top ~1% of qualifying candidates receive the Junior Research Fellowship stipend plus lifelong Assistant Professor eligibility.
• Category-2 (Assistant Professor + Ph.D. Admission): Top ~6% across both papers combined qualify for Assistant Professor recruitment and Ph.D. entrance without JRF.
• Category-3 (Ph.D. Admission Only): Candidates falling below the 6% Assistant Professor cutoff but inside the next merit band receive a score valid for 1 Year for Ph.D. admissions in central and state universities (70% NET score weightage + 30% university interview).

2. Mandatory Minimum Aggregate Requirement & Dropped Question Policy: Before subject-wise slot cutoffs are applied, candidates must first score at least 40% aggregate (120/300 marks) in Paper-1 and Paper-2 combined for General/EWS, or 35% aggregate (105/300 marks) for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and PwBD. If a challenged question is officially dropped by NTA's subject expert committee in the Final Answer Key, +2 bonus marks are awarded to all candidates who appeared in that shift.
