Technician Grade-I Signal & Technician Grade-III CEN Provisional & Final Answer Key
Exam Authority: Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), Ministry of Railways, Govt. of India 🔑 RRB TECHNICIAN RESPONSE SHEET & KEY ACTIVE

Short Information : The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Computer Based Test (CBT) response sheet and provisional answer key for Technician Grade-I (Signal) and Technician Grade-III recruitment. Candidates who appeared in the online CBT exam can log in with their Registration Number and Date of Birth to verify marked responses, calculate probable score, and raise objections against incorrect questions.

Important Schedule & Objection Rules
  • CBT Examination Window : Conducted Pan-India in Shifts
  • Provisional Answer Key Release : Available via Candidate Login
  • Objection Raising Fee : ₹50/- per Question (+ Bank Charges)
  • Fee Refund Policy : Refunded if Objection Found Valid
  • Final Answer Key & Result : Published After Objection Review
Marking Scheme & Score Calculation
  • Total Questions (Gr-III) : 100 MCQs (90 Minutes)
  • Total Questions (Gr-I Signal) : 100 MCQs (90 Minutes)
  • Correct Answer Marks : +1 Mark
  • Negative Marking : -1/3rd (0.33 Marks Deducted)
  • Minimum Qualifying Marks : UR/EWS: 40%, OBC/SC: 30%, ST: 25%
Step-by-Step: How to Download RRB Technician Response Sheet & Calculate Score
  1. Click on the official RRB regional portal link (rrbcdg.gov.in or your applied RRB zone) below.
  2. Select the link for "CEN Technician CBT Answer Key, Response Sheet & Objection Tracker".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), then verify the security captcha.
  4. Open the "Candidate Response" tab to view your question paper along with official correct options and your chosen answer.
  5. Use our Negative Marking Calculator tool to calculate your exact net raw score before normalization.
