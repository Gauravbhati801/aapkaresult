Editorial Deep-Dive: Indian Navy Agniveer MR (Chef, Steward, Hygienist)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Agniveer MR sailors serve aboard frontline Indian Navy warships, aircraft carriers, submarines, and shore establishments. Chefs prepare nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian food for ship crews as per naval menu standards. Stewards manage officers mess service, wine handling, and ration accounting. Hygienists maintain sanitation standards in living quarters, mess decks, and ship medical bays.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Structured under the Agniveer Seva Nidhi scale starting at ₹30,000 gross in Year 1 to ₹40,000 in Year 4, with 30% credited monthly to the Seva Nidhi package. In addition, when deployed on active sea duty, personnel receive Ship Boarding and High Seas allowances. In-hand pay is ₹21,000 to ₹28,000/month, culminating in an ₹11.71 Lakh exit corpus.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

The Computer-Based Examination (INET) carries 50 questions (50 marks) covering Science, Mathematics, and General Awareness. State-wise merit cut-offs vary: Northern states like UP, Bihar, and Rajasthan traditionally observe cut-offs around 34–38 marks out of 50, whereas coastal and southern states observe cut-offs between 24 and 30 marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The test duration is only 30 minutes for 50 questions. Build immediate recognition speed for basic science concepts (elements, motion, work, light) and national symbols, geography, and defense awards. Mathematics questions are direct formula-based arithmetic; practice standard Class 10th NCERT problems.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Stage-2 Physical Fitness Assessment requires completing a 1.6 km run in 6.5 minutes for males (8 minutes for females), 20 squats (Uthak Baithak), and 12 push-ups. Minimum height requirement is 157 cm for males and 152 cm for females. Eye vision must be 6/6, 6/9 without spectacles for Chef and Steward trades.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can 10th pass candidates apply for Indian Navy MR?

Yes. 10th Class (Matriculation) pass from any recognized educational board is the required qualification for Indian Navy Agniveer MR.

Q2. What are the 3 branches in Indian Navy Agniveer MR?

The three branches under Agniveer MR are Chef (preparation of vegetarian and non-vegetarian food), Steward (serving food in officers' mess, accounting, housekeeping), and Hygienist (cleaning of washrooms, spaces, and maintaining general hygiene on naval ships and establishments).

Q3. Is state domicile certificate compulsory for Navy recruitment?

Yes, candidates must possess a valid Domicile Certificate of the state they represent. Selection cutoffs are determined state-wise for Stage-1.
