CTET Result 2026: CBSE Scorecard Check, Cutoff Marks & DigiLocker Eligibility Certificate

Board: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi Mode: Online Verification Status: Live & Available Online
Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi
Central Teacher Eligibility Test – CTET 2026 Examination Result
National Eligibility Benchmark for Primary & Upper Primary School Teachers
Exam Authority: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi 👨‍🏫 CBSE CTET MARKS STATEMENT ONLINE

Short Information : The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Delhi has declared the official results for the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) 2026. Candidates who appeared for Paper-I (Classes I-V) and Paper-II (Classes VI-VIII) across India can verify their subject-wise marks, qualifying status (60% for General, 55% for Reserved Categories), and download their lifetime-valid DigiLocker digital certificate.

Important Schedule & Dates
  • CTET Examination Date : December 2025 / January 2026
  • Official Answer Key & OMR Sheet : February 2026
  • CTET Result Declared Online : Announced & Live
  • DigiLocker Marksheet Available : 15 Days Post Result
  • Certificate Validity : Lifetime (Valid for all KVS, NVS, DSSSB, State Jobs)
Examination & Student Statistics
  • Total Candidates Registered : 26.8+ Lakh Aspirants
  • Paper 1 (Primary) Appeared : 9.5+ Lakh Candidates
  • Paper 2 (Upper Primary) Appeared : 17.3+ Lakh Candidates
  • General Category Qualifying Cutoff : 60% (90 Marks out of 150)
  • OBC / SC / ST Qualifying Cutoff : 55% (82 Marks out of 150)
  • Verification Mode : Online by Roll Number
Step-by-Step: How to Check Scorecard & Marksheet Online
  1. Click on the CBSE CTET Result link given in the links table below.
  2. Enter your official Roll Number as printed on your CTET Admit Card.
  3. Click on Submit to view your Paper 1 and Paper 2 marks statement.
  4. Verify that you scored 90+ marks (General) or 82+ marks (SC/ST/OBC).
  5. Download your marksheet and install DigiLocker app to get your digitally signed lifetime certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Where can I check my official scorecard?

You can check directly using the official server links given in the Useful Important Links table above, or through the government DigiLocker portal.

Q2. What credentials are required to verify the result?

Keep your official examination Roll Number, Date of Birth, and School/Application Number (printed on your original admit card) ready.

Q3. Is the online marksheet valid for admissions?

Yes. The online scorecard or DigiLocker digitally signed certificate serves as valid provisional proof for admission counselling until the physical certificate is released.
