26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is there any exam or interview in India Post GDS recruitment?

No. There is neither a written examination nor an interview. Selection is made automatically by computer software strictly on the basis of 10th standard board percentage.

Q2. Is a computer certificate mandatory for India Post GDS?

Candidates should possess a basic computer training certificate of minimum 60 days from a recognized training center. However, if computer was a subject in Class 10th or 12th, a separate certificate is not mandatory.

Q3. Can a candidate from Uttar Pradesh apply for Bihar or Rajasthan Postal Circle?

Yes, candidates can apply for any circle provided they have studied the official local language of that circle up to 10th standard.

Q4. What is the difference between BPM and ABPM posts?

A Branch Postmaster (BPM) heads the Gramin Dak Seva branch office and manages postal operations, accounts, and IPPB services. An Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) assists the BPM and handles mail delivery, conveyance, and stamp sales.
