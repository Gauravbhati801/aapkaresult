17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official India Post GDS engagement portal at indiapostgdsonline.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Click on 'Registration' and enter Mobile Number, Email ID, Applicant's Name, Father's Name, and Date of Birth.
  3. Step 3: Pay the application fee (₹100 for Gen/OBC/EWS Male; ₹0 for SC/ST/Female/PwD).
  4. Step 4: Login using your Registration Number and choose your Postal Circle.
  5. Step 5: Enter 10th Class marks subject-wise exactly as stated on your original marksheet.
  6. Step 6: Upload scanned photograph (20KB - 50KB) and signature (10KB - 20KB).
  7. Step 7: Select your post preferences (choose as many division post offices as desired in order of priority).
  8. Step 8: Submit the application and download the confirmation printout.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • Original 10th Class (Secondary School) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
  • Computer Training Certificate of minimum 60 days duration (from recognized computer institute or studied computer in school/college)
  • Proof of Date of Birth (10th certificate or birth certificate)
  • Caste / Community Certificate (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS) in prescribed format
  • Disability Certificate (if applying under PwD category)
  • Recent passport size photograph
  • Valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID)

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Do NOT enter higher secondary or graduation marks; only 10th standard marks are considered.
  • Ensure the local language of the postal circle you are applying for was studied by you up to 10th class.
  • Candidates should give maximum post preferences to increase chances of selection in subsequent merit lists.
  • Shortlisted candidates who fail to attend document verification within 15 days of merit list release forfeit their candidature.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is there any exam or interview in India Post GDS recruitment?

No. There is neither a written examination nor an interview. Selection is made automatically by computer software strictly on the basis of 10th standard board percentage.

Q2. Is a computer certificate mandatory for India Post GDS?

Candidates should possess a basic computer training certificate of minimum 60 days from a recognized training center. However, if computer was a subject in Class 10th or 12th, a separate certificate is not mandatory.

Q3. Can a candidate from Uttar Pradesh apply for Bihar or Rajasthan Postal Circle?

Yes, candidates can apply for any circle provided they have studied the official local language of that circle up to 10th standard.

Q4. What is the difference between BPM and ABPM posts?

A Branch Postmaster (BPM) heads the Gramin Dak Seva branch office and manages postal operations, accounts, and IPPB services. An Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) assists the BPM and handles mail delivery, conveyance, and stamp sales.
