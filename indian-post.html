Editorial Deep-Dive: India Post GDS (Branch Postmaster & Dak Sevak)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

GDS personnel are the backbone of rural postal connectivity across Indian villages. Branch Postmasters (BPM) manage village branch post office operations, IPPB digital banking, Speed Post booking, and rural savings account transactions. Assistant Branch Postmasters (ABPM) and Dak Sevaks handle door-to-door mail delivery, parcel dispatch, and government cash disbursals under Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) schemes.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Compensated under Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA). BPM receives a minimum TRCA slab of ₹12,000 – ₹29,380 for 4 hours of daily duty. ABPM and Dak Sevak receive ₹10,000 – ₹24,470 for 4 hours of duty. In addition, GDS personnel receive Dearness Allowance (currently 50%), composite office maintenance allowances, and stationary allowances, yielding an in-hand remuneration of ₹16,500 to ₹21,000/month for 4 to 5 hours of duty.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Selection is 100% merit-based calculated exclusively on Class 10th Board marks with zero written exam. In populous postal circles like Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh, 10th-grade cutoffs routinely exceed 97% to 99% in unreserved and OBC categories. Candidates with 90% to 95% marks have realistic prospects primarily in remote rural clusters, hill circles, or reserved categories.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

Because there is no written examination, selection success depends completely on error-free application submission and strategic division selection. Aspirants must carefully examine previous circle-wise merit lists and select divisions where previous cut-offs were comparatively moderate. Ensure local language proficiency up to Class 10th for the chosen postal circle.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

The most frequent cause of rejection is discrepancies in Class 10th marksheets: candidate name, father’s name, and date of birth must match character-by-character with Aadhaar card details. Candidates must also obtain a basic computer training course certificate of at least 60 days duration from a recognized computer training institute before document verification.

Opting for remote, interior rural branch post offices rather than prime subdivisions adjacent to district headquarters significantly improves selection probability for candidates with 90%–95% aggregate marks. • Local Language Proficiency Verification: Ensure that the local language of the postal circle applied for was studied as a compulsory or elective subject up to Class 10th. 26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is there any exam or interview in India Post GDS recruitment?

No. There is neither a written examination nor an interview. Selection is made automatically by computer software strictly on the basis of 10th standard board percentage.

Q2. Is a computer certificate mandatory for India Post GDS?

Candidates should possess a basic computer training certificate of minimum 60 days from a recognized training center. However, if computer was a subject in Class 10th or 12th, a separate certificate is not mandatory.

Q3. Can a candidate from Uttar Pradesh apply for Bihar or Rajasthan Postal Circle?

Yes, candidates can apply for any circle provided they have studied the official local language of that circle up to 10th standard.

Q4. What is the difference between BPM and ABPM posts?

A Branch Postmaster (BPM) heads the Gramin Dak Seva branch office and manages postal operations, accounts, and IPPB services. An Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) assists the BPM and handles mail delivery, conveyance, and stamp sales.
