Editorial Deep-Dive: SSC GD Constable (CAPFs, SSF, Assam Rifles)

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

Personnel serve on frontline national defense borders and internal counter-insurgency grids across BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, AR, and SSF. Responsibilities range from international border patrolling (Indo-Pak, Indo-Bangla, Indo-China borders) to industrial airport security (CISF), VIP protection, and counter-terrorist operations. Recruits undergo 44 weeks of intense tactical and weapons training at centralized force training centers.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Recruited under Pay Matrix Level-3 (Basic Pay ₹21,700 – ₹69,100). With 50% DA, HRA, Transport Allowance, Ration Money Allowance (₹3,965/month), and Risk & Hardship Allowances (ranging from ₹6,000 to ₹17,300 depending on border or high-altitude posting), gross monthly earnings range between ₹39,000 and ₹54,000. In-hand take-home salary is approximately ₹35,000 to ₹48,000 per month.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Cut-offs vary significantly by state domicile, force preference, and border/militancy-affected districts. For high-demand forces like CISF and SSF in Northern states (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Haryana), safe target scores in the 160-mark CBT are 138+ marks for UR and 134+ marks for OBC. For general border posts in BSF and CRPF, target scores range between 124 and 132 marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

The CBT has 80 questions (160 marks) with only 60 minutes duration. Time management is crucial: allocate 12 minutes to Hindi/English (20 Qs), 10 minutes to General Knowledge (20 Qs), 18 minutes to Reasoning (20 Qs), and 20 minutes to Elementary Mathematics (20 Qs). Practice mental calculations and arithmetic shortcuts to avoid losing time on multi-step percentage and compound interest problems.

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Physical Efficiency Test (PET) requires running 5 km in 24 minutes for males and 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes for females. Medical examination checks for knock knees, flat foot, varicose veins, squint eyes, and minimum visual acuity of 6/6 and 6/9 without glasses. Ensure your state domicile certificate is authentic, as attempting to claim cutoff benefits from other states leads to permanent debarment.

Subject-Wise Preparation Strategy & High-Weightage Topics

Tailored Study Roadmap by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk

Succeeding in the 60-minute SSC GD Computer-Based Examination demands lightning speed and disciplined question-skipping: • General Intelligence & Reasoning (20 Questions / 40 Marks): Practice Syllogisms, Non-Verbal Paper Folding, Mirror Images, Figure Analogy, and Seating Arrangements daily. Complete this entire section within 14 minutes with a target score of 38+ marks. • Elementary Mathematics (20 Questions / 40 Marks): Arithmetic dominates the paper. Master Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Time and Work, Speed-Time-Distance, Averages, Profit and Loss, and Mensuration 2D/3D formulas. Master Vartani Shuddhi, Vakya Sanshodhan, Vilom Shabd, Muhavare, and Rekhankit Shabd substitution. Aim for 36+ marks in under 10 minutes. • General Knowledge & General Awareness (20 Questions / 40 Marks): Focus on Classical & Folk Dances of India, Musical Instruments and Maestros, Fundamental Rights & DPSP, Five-Year Plans, Sports Tournaments, and National Parks. Spend no more than 7 minutes on this section.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can 10th pass candidates apply for SSC GD Constable?

Yes, the minimum educational qualification for SSC GD Constable is 10th Class (Matriculation) pass from any recognized educational board.

Q2. Is there an interview in SSC GD Constable recruitment?

No, there is no interview. Selection is made through Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Physical Tests (PST/PET), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Q3. In what languages is the SSC GD CBT examination conducted?

The Computer Based Exam is conducted in Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages including Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.

Q4. What is the physical running standard for SSC GD male and female candidates?

Male candidates must complete 5 km in 24 minutes. Female candidates must complete 1.6 km in 8 minutes and 30 seconds.
