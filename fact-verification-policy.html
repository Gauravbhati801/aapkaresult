Fact Verification Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | AapkaResult.in Standards

AapkaResult.in follows rigorous fact-checking protocols to ensure that all information concerning government vacancies, exam dates, syllabus details, and merit lists is verified and accurate.

1. Source Hierarchy Protocol

When evaluating information, our editorial team follows a strict verification hierarchy:

  • Tier 1 (Highest Authority): Official Gazette notifications, official commission websites (.gov.in, .nic.in), and signed recruitment advertisements.
  • Tier 2: Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases and official state government information departments.
  • Tier 3: Reputed national daily newspapers (The Hindu, Indian Express, Dainik Jagran) for initial press releases.

2. Data Point Cross-Verification

Every recruitment post must have its core data points independently verified:

  • Application Start and Last Dates verified against the portal registration schedule.
  • Category-wise application fees confirmed from the official financial instructions.
  • Age limit cutoffs and category relaxations verified with state/central reservation rules.
  • Physical measurement standards (Height, Chest, Running) confirmed from official annexures.

3. Handling Rumours and Unverified Leaks

We actively combat fake news and social media rumours regarding exam cancellations or leaked answer keys. If an unverified notice circulates on social media, we verify directly with the board before publishing, or explicitly tag it as "Unverified / Clarification Awaited".

Feedback & Queries: If you have any inquiries regarding our policies or wish to report a correction, please contact our editorial desk at support@aapkaresult.in or visit our Contact Us page.
