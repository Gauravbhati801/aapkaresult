ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Online Form 2026 – Apply for 400 Trade, Diploma & Graduate Posts

Recruiting Body: Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR (SDSC SHAR), ISRO Post Name: Trade, Technician Diploma, Graduate & Vocational Apprentice Advt No: SDSC SHAR/RMT/02/2026
Share Alert:
Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR (SDSC SHAR), Sriharikota • ISRO
ISRO SDSC SHAR Various Trade, Diploma & Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026
Advt. No. SDSC SHAR/RMT/02/2026 : Short Details of Official Notification
Name Of Post : ISRO SDSC SHAR Various Trade Apprentice Engagement 2026
Post Date / Update : 06 November 2026 | Active 🎉 400 Vacancies • Direct Merit Selection (No Exam)

Short Information : Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR, Sriharikota (Tirupati Dist., Andhra Pradesh) has officially announced the engagement of 400 Apprentices for the 2026-27 batch under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Positions are open across Engineering Graduate Apprentices (50 Posts), Non-Engineering Graduate Apprentices (60 Posts), Technician Diploma Apprentices (35 Posts), Diploma in DCCP (60 Posts), Trade Apprentices (180 Posts), and Vocational Apprentices (15 Posts). Eligible candidates with ITI, Diploma, B.E./B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, or B.A. can submit online applications from 06/11/2026 to 26/11/2026 (05:00 PM). There is NO application fee for any category. Read below for trade-wise vacancies, stipend details, eligibility, and direct apply online links.

  • All Category Female Candidates : ₹0/- (Exempted)
  • No Application Fee : This recruitment is completely free to apply for all candidates under Apprentices Act rules.
ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Notification 2026 : Age Limit as on 26/11/2026
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum Age Limit : As per Apprenticeship Rules (Standard Government of India guidelines)
  • Age Relaxation Extra : 3 Years for OBC (NCL), 5 Years for SC / ST, and 10 Years for PwBD candidates as per Central Government Apprenticeship norms.
  • Passing Year Criterion : Degree / Diploma candidates must have passed qualifying examination in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026. Candidates who completed before 2022 or already have 1+ year training/experience are not eligible.
Monthly Stipend & 1-Year Training Period at Sriharikota
  • Graduate Apprentices (Engineering & Non-Engineering) : ₹12,300/- per month consolidated stipend.
  • Technician Apprentices (Diploma in Engg & DCCP) : ₹10,900/- per month consolidated stipend.
  • Trade Apprentices (ITI NTC Holders) : ₹9,600/- per month consolidated stipend.
  • Vocational Apprentices : ₹9,600/- per month consolidated stipend.
  • Training Duration & Benefits : 12 Months (1 Year) at Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR, Sriharikota. Candidates receive subsidized medical facilities, canteen amenities, and space launch centre exposure.

ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Selection Process 2026
  • Stage 1 : Merit-Based Shortlisting – There is NO written test. Candidates are shortlisted purely on the basis of aggregate percentage of marks scored in the qualifying degree, diploma, or ITI examination.
  • Stage 2 : Document Verification (DV) – Shortlisted candidates must present original marksheets, degree/diploma/ITI certificates, proof of date of birth, caste/community certificates, and valid photo ID at SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota.
  • Stage 3 : Medical Fitness Verification – Final engagement is subject to meeting ISRO's prescribed medical fitness standards.
  • Aadhaar-Linked Bank Account (DBT) : Selected apprentices must ensure their bank account is seeded with Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer of monthly stipend.
How to Fill ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Online Application Form 2026
  • Applications for ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice 2026 must be submitted online between 06/11/2026 to 26/11/2026 (05:00 PM).
  • Step 1 – Portal Enrollment :
    • Graduates and Diploma candidates must register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS 2.0) portal: nats.education.gov.in.
    • Trade (ITI) candidates must register on the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) portal: apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
  • Step 2 – SDSC SHAR Online Application : Visit the official portal www.shar.gov.in (or through the UMANG App / Web portal) and navigate to Careers > Apprentice Recruitment 2026.
  • Fill in all required educational details, trade selection, marks percentages, and permanent address carefully.
  • Upload clear scanned copies of recent passport size photograph, signature, 10th marksheet, ITI/Diploma/Degree certificate, and Community Certificate (if applicable).
  • Review all details before clicking the final submit button. Remember: there is ₹0 application fee.
  • Download and print the generated online application confirmation slip for document verification.

The training will be conducted at Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC) SHAR, Sriharikota, Tirupati District, Andhra Pradesh—India's primary spaceport for satellite launch missions.

Editorial Deep-Dive: ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Training & Career Prospects

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Space & Technical Careers Desk • 2026 Standards

🛰️ 1. Work Culture & Facilities at Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC SHAR)

Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC) SHAR, situated on the barrier island of Sriharikota in Andhra Pradesh, is the spaceport of the nation responsible for solid propellant processing, rocket vehicle integration, testing, telemetry, tracking, and launching ISRO missions including PSLV, GSLV, and LVM3. Apprentices receive hands-on training under seasoned ISRO scientists and technical officers in high-tech laboratories, launch pad complexes, and cryogenic storage facilities. Working in clean-room environments and learning space-grade quality control provides unmatched technical credibility on any candidate's resume.

💼 2. Value of ISRO National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC)

Upon successful completion of the 1-year apprenticeship, candidates appear for the All India Trade Test (AITT) conducted by NCVT to receive the prestigious National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC). In regular recruitment drives announced by ISRO centres (such as VSSC, URSC, LPSC, SAC, and SDSC SHAR for Technician-B, Technical Assistant, and Scientific Assistant positions), candidates holding an apprenticeship certificate from a premier space/defense facility enjoy a massive competitive advantage during skill tests and trade examinations.

📈 3. Merit Cut-Off Expectations & Trade Competition

Because there is no written test and no application fee, competition is fierce nationwide. For Engineering Graduate Apprentices (Mechanical & Electronics), historical shortlist cutoffs usually hover above 80% aggregate marks (or 8.2+ CGPA) for General/UR categories, and 74% to 78% for OBC/EWS. For ITI Trade Apprentices (Electrician and Fitter), candidates with 85%+ marks in ITI NTC stand the highest chance of selection in the first merit list.

⚠️ 4. Crucial Guidelines for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) & NATS/NAPS Registration

Candidates must ensure that their Aadhaar number is active and properly mapped to their active savings bank account via NPCI Aadhaar Seeding. Government stipend transfers are credited exclusively via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Candidates must complete their profile registration on the NATS 2.0 or NAPS portal before submitting their final form on the SDSC SHAR website.
