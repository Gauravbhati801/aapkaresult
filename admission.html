Candidate Roadmap: College Admissions & Government Scholarship Portals

Navigating higher education admissions and securing government fee reimbursement requires systematic documentation. Follow these steps:

  1. Entrance Examination Registration & Information Bulletin: Review the official Information Bulletin for required domain subjects (e.g., CUET UG subject mapping or NEET eligibility criteria). Register early to secure your preferred examination city.
  2. Counseling Registration & Choice Locking: Following score declaration, register on the centralized counseling portal (such as DU CSAS, UP JEECUP, or AKTU). Fill and lock your course and college preferences carefully before the published deadline.
  3. Physical Document Verification & Seat Acceptance: When allocated a seat, pay the seat acceptance fee and report to the designated nodal center with original educational certificates, transfer certificates (TC), migration certificates, and reservation credentials.
  4. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Bank Seeding: To claim government fee reimbursement and maintenance stipends (such as UP Scholarship), your bank account must be Aadhaar-seeded with active NPCI mapping. Standard savings accounts without DBT seeding will fail to receive scholarship funds.
  5. Institutional Verification & Attendance Tracking: Submit your online scholarship application copy along with fee receipts to your college scholarship committee. Ensure your academic biometric attendance remains above the mandatory 75% threshold throughout the session.

Frequently Asked Questions (Admissions & Scholarships)

Q1. Why does the UP Scholarship portal display 'Aadhaar NPCI Mapping Pending'?

This status indicates that your bank account is not officially mapped with the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). To resolve this, visit your home branch and submit an Aadhaar Seeding / DBT Mandate form.

Q2. What is CUET UG and which universities accept its scorecard?

CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test) is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission into undergraduate programs across all 45+ Central Universities (including DU, BHU, JNU, Jamia) and dozens of participating State, Deemed, and Private universities.

Q3. What is the validity period of an Income Certificate for scholarship applications?

In Uttar Pradesh and most other states, an official Tehsildar-issued Income Certificate remains valid for exactly 3 years from its date of issuance. Ensure the certificate is active and reflects your family's annual income below the designated ceiling.

Q4. Can a student apply for two different scholarships in the same academic year?

Under government audit rules, a student cannot receive financial maintenance stipends from more than one government-funded scholarship scheme simultaneously. You may apply for private merit scholarships alongside state fee reimbursement if permitted by state regulations.
