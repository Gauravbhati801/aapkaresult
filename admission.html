How to Apply for Uttar Pradesh Scholarship Online

  1. Student Registration: Visit the official portal scholarship.up.gov.in and choose your category (General, OBC, SC, ST, Minority) and course stage (Pre-Matric, Post-Matric Inter, or Post-Matric Other Than Inter).
  2. Aadhaar Demographic & OTP Authentication: Complete your e-KYC using your active Aadhaar linked mobile number.
  3. Fill Academic & Bank Details: Enter your annual course fee, bank account number (Aadhaar NPCI-linked), income certificate, and caste certificate numbers.
  4. Upload Documents: Upload your passport photograph and verified enrollment number given by your college or institute.
  5. Print Final Copy: Submit the online application, take a hard copy printout, attach required photocopies, and submit it to your college within the prescribed deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions (Admissions & Scholarships)

Q1. Is bank account Aadhaar NPCI mapping mandatory for government scholarships?

Yes. The Government of India transfers scholarship and fellowship funds via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Your bank account must be actively seeded with Aadhaar on the NPCI mapper.

Q2. What is CUET UG and who can appear for it?

Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) is a nationwide computer-based test conducted by NTA for admission to undergraduate courses across all Central, State, and participating Deemed Universities in India.
