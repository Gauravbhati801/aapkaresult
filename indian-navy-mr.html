Indian Navy Agniveer MR Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 300+ Posts

Department: Indian Navy, Ministry of Defence, Government of India Post Name: Agniveer MR (Matric Recruit - Chef, Steward & Hygienist) Status: Recruitment Portal Active

11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Shortlisting based on Computer Based Online Examination (INET - Indian Navy Entrance Test)
  2. Stage 2: Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Written Examination (Physical Mode), and Initial Medical
  3. Stage 3: Final Medical Examination at INS Chilka prior to commencement of training

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the official Join Indian Navy portal at joinindiannavy.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Click on 'Register' with your Aadhaar virtual ID or mobile number.
  3. Step 3: Login to your dashboard and click 'Current Opportunities' for Agniveer (MR).
  4. Step 4: Enter 10th marksheet details and select post preferences (Chef, Steward, Hygienist).
  5. Step 5: Upload clear passport photo with blue background, signature, and state domicile certificate.
  6. Step 6: Pay the application fee (₹550 + GST) online.
  7. Step 7: Submit the application and download the confirmation page.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • 10th Class (Matriculation) Certificate & Marksheet
  • State Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra) is mandatory for Indian Navy applicants
  • Valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card)
  • Recent passport-size photograph with plain blue background
  • Scanned signature of the applicant

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Only UNMARRIED male and female candidates are eligible for appointment.
  • Candidates must produce original 10th marksheet and domicile certificate at the Stage-2 testing center.
  • Possession of a valid State Domicile Certificate is compulsory; without it, candidature will be cancelled.
  • Up to 25% of Agniveer MR will be selected for permanent service in the Indian Navy.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can 10th pass candidates apply for Indian Navy MR?

Yes. 10th Class (Matriculation) pass from any recognized educational board is the required qualification for Indian Navy Agniveer MR.

Q2. What are the 3 branches in Indian Navy Agniveer MR?

The three branches under Agniveer MR are Chef (preparation of vegetarian and non-vegetarian food), Steward (serving food in officers' mess, accounting, housekeeping), and Hygienist (cleaning of washrooms, spaces, and maintaining general hygiene on naval ships and establishments).

Q3. Is state domicile certificate compulsory for Navy recruitment?

Yes, candidates must possess a valid Domicile Certificate of the state they represent. Selection cutoffs are determined state-wise for Stage-1.
