❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the educational qualification for RRB Station Master Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline (BA, BSc, BCom, BTech, BBA, BCA, etc.) from a recognized University or Institution. Candidates awaiting final results are not eligible to apply.

Q2. What are the opening and closing dates for online applications under CEN 08/2026?

The online application started on 27 October 2026 and the closing date to submit the application is 25 November 2026 (23:59 Hrs). Application fee payment ends on 27 November 2026, and the modification window remains active from 28 November to 07 December 2026.

Q3. What is the age limit and relaxation for Station Master post?

The prescribed age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01 January 2027. Category-wise upper age relaxation is available: 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) up to 36 years, and 5 years for SC/ST candidates up to 38 years.

Q4. What is the selection process and final merit formula?

The selection process comprises a Single-Stage Computer-Based Test (100 Questions, 120 Minutes, 1/3 negative marking), followed by a Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT / Psycho Test). Final merit is compiled by giving 70% weightage to CBT marks and 30% weightage to CBAT marks, followed by Document Verification and A-2 Medical Examination.

Q5. Can candidates wearing spectacles (glasses) apply for Station Master?

No. Station Master requires the strict A-2 Medical Fitness Standard. Distance vision must be 6/9, 6/9 without glasses, and near vision must be Sn 0.6, 0.6 without glasses. Candidates who wear spectacles or have undergone LASIK / refractive surgery are disqualified under A-2 rules.

Q6. How does the application fee refund work?

General, OBC, and EWS candidates pay ₹500, out of which ₹400 is refunded to their bank account upon appearing in the CBT exam. SC, ST, Female, Transgender, Ex-SM, and EBC candidates pay ₹250, and the entire ₹250 is refunded after appearing in the CBT exam.

Editorial Deep-Dive: Railway RRB Station Master (CEN 08/2026) Strategy & Career Guide

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Operations & Mentorship Desk • 2026 Standards

👮 1. Job Role, Operational Authority & Shift Timings

A Station Master is the operational head of a railway station, directly responsible for the safe and punctual movement of passenger and goods trains, route relay interlocking (RRI/panel operation), emergency block clearances, and passenger safety. Station Masters operate on rotating 8-hour roster shifts (Day, Evening, and Night). Because of their pivotal role in railway operating safety, Station Masters undergo rigorous 6-month initial training at Zonal Railway Training Institutes (ZRTI) before field posting.

💰 2. Pay Matrix Level 6 Salary, Night Duty & Station Allowances

Station Master is appointed at Pay Level-6 of the 7th Central Pay Commission with an initial Basic Pay of ₹35,400 per month. Under current central pay norms, allowances include 50% Dearness Allowance (DA = ₹17,700), House Rent Allowance (HRA = 9% to 27% based on city category), Night Duty Allowance (NDA), National Holiday Allowance (NHA), and Transport Allowance. The starting gross salary ranges between ₹62,000 and ₹68,000 per month, yielding an approximate net in-hand salary of ₹55,000 to ₹60,000 per month, alongside free All-India Railway Medical coverage and Annual Family Privilege Passes.

🧠 3. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT / Psycho Test) Mastery

Unlike standard clerk or guard posts, Station Master requires qualifying the Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT). The CBAT evaluates 5 core cognitive batteries: Intelligence Test, Selective Attention Test, Spatial Scanning (Shortest Route), Information Ordering, and Personality/Perceptual Speed Test. Candidates must score a minimum qualifying T-Score of 42 marks in each individual battery — failure in even a single battery disqualifies the candidate, regardless of high CBT written marks. Since CBAT carries a substantial 30% weight in the final merit formula, daily computer-based psycho practice is indispensable.

📈 4. Promotional Career Hierarchy in Operating Cadre

Station Masters enjoy one of the fastest and most prestigious departmental career tracks in Indian Railways. The promotional hierarchy advances from Station Master (Level 6) → Station Superintendent (SS, Level 7) → Station Director / Chief Controller (Level 8) → Assistant Operations Manager (AOM, Group 'B' Gazetted Officer) via Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) → Divisional Operations Manager (DOM, Senior Scale) → Senior Divisional Operations Manager (Sr. DOM, Level 12 Group 'A').
