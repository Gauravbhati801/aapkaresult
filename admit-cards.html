Candidate Advisory: Examination Hall Entry Protocol & Troubleshooting

Downloading your hall ticket on time and understanding examination center protocols ensures a seamless test experience. Follow these essential instructions:

  1. Downloading & Printing Verification: Download your admit card from the verified commission link using your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password. Print the document on clean white A4 paper using a high-resolution color or laser printer to ensure the barcode and photograph are sharp and scan properly.
  2. Mandatory Photo Identity Verification: Carry at least one original, government-issued photo identity card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card, or Driving License). The candidate's name, parent's name, and date of birth on the photo ID must match the admit card character-by-character.
  3. Reporting Schedule & Gate Closing Strictness: Plan your travel itinerary to reach the designated examination venue at least 60 to 90 minutes before the scheduled reporting time. Note that gate closing timings are strictly enforced, and entry is barred after the designated cut-off minute without exception.
  4. Biometric Thumbprint & Iris Authentication: At modern Computer-Based Test (CBT) centers, candidates must undergo digital facial scanning and biometric thumbprint matching before workstation allocation. Avoid applying henna, ink, or chemicals to your fingers before the test day.
  5. Prohibited Items in the Examination Hall: Electronic devices (mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth earphones, calculators), metal ornaments, wallets, belts, and written paper chits are strictly forbidden inside the testing hall. Only transparent ballpoint pens and a clear water bottle are permitted.

Frequently Asked Questions (Admit Cards & Hall Tickets)

Q1. What is the difference between an Exam City Slip and an Admit Card?

An Exam City Intimation Slip is an advance travel information slip released 7 to 10 days before the exam to inform candidates of the city where their exam is scheduled. The official Admit Card (Hall Ticket) is issued 3 to 4 days before the exam date and specifies the exact venue address, shift timing, and gate closing time.

Q2. Is Aadhaar-based biometric verification mandatory at the exam center?

Yes, recruitment commissions such as SSC, Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB), and UPPRPB have made Aadhaar biometric thumbprint verification mandatory at test center entry gates. Ensure your Aadhaar biometric locking is disabled prior to the exam day.

Q3. What should I do if my photograph or signature is missing on the printed admit card?

If your photograph or signature is blurred or missing, immediately contact the regional helpdesk of the examining commission with your registration details. Additionally, bring two recent passport-size photographs, original photo ID proof, and a printed copy of your online confirmation page directly to the exam center superintendent.

Q4. What happens if I arrive at the examination center after the gate closing time?

Under strict commission regulations, entry gates are sealed precisely at the designated Gate Closing Time (usually 30 to 45 minutes before exam commencement). Late arrivals are strictly barred from entering under any circumstances.
