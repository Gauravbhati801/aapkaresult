Mandatory Guidelines for Candidates on Examination Day

Permitted Items at Exam Center
  • Original Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License).
  • Clear printed copy of the Admit Card (Color or sharp Black & White).
  • Two recent passport-sized color photographs matching the application photo.
  • Transparent blue or black ballpoint pen (where permitted by board rules).
Strictly Prohibited Items
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, and earphones.
  • Calculators, digital pens, or electronic memory gadgets.
  • Books, loose sheets of paper, or unauthorized geometry cases.
  • Heavy metallic jewelry or thick-soled boots violating dress code norms.

How to Download Your Admit Card Online

  1. Click on the designated download action link for your respective exam in the table above.
  2. On the official commission login page, enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth / Password.

Frequently Asked Questions (Admit Cards)

Q1. What is the difference between Exam City Slip and Admit Card?

An Exam City Intimation Slip is issued 7 to 10 days before the exam to assist outstation candidates in booking train and hotel travel. It only states the city and test date. The actual Admit Card (with venue address and roll number) is released 3 to 4 days prior to the examination.

Q2. Is Aadhaar-based biometric verification mandatory at the exam center?

Yes. Major recruitment bodies like SSC, UPSC, and RRB conduct mandatory Aadhaar-based biometric authentication at entrance gates. Bring your original physical Aadhaar card.
