Step-by-Step: How to Check UP Board Result 2026 Online
  1. Click on either Server 1 (Class 10th) or Server 2 (Class 12th) link given in the direct links table below.
  2. Enter your 10-digit UP Board Roll Number as printed on your original board examination Admit Card.
  3. For Class 12th students, enter your 5-digit School Code printed on your admit card.
  4. Enter the security Captcha Code shown on the screen and click on Submit / View Result button.
  5. Your provisional subject-wise scorecard will appear on screen showing theory marks, practical marks, and qualifying grade.
  6. Download or take a printout of the marksheet for future reference and higher admission counselling.

Rechecking, Scrutiny & Compartment Examination

Students who believe their evaluated marks do not reflect their true performance can apply for UPMSP Scrutiny (Revaluation) within 25 days from the date of result declaration.

  • Scrutiny application fee is ₹500/- per subject (practical and theory separate).
  • Candidates must deposit the fee via Treasury Challan in the State Bank of India (SBI) in favor of the Secretary, Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad, UP.
  • Students who fail in one or two subjects can appear in the UP Board Compartment / Improvement Exam scheduled for July 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the minimum passing marks required in UP Board 10th & 12th Exams?

A student must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each individual subject (both theory and practical separately) and 33% in aggregate to be declared PASS.

Q2. What credentials are needed to check UP Board Result 2026 online?

Class 10th students only need their 10-digit Roll Number. Class 12th students need their Roll Number and their 5-digit School Code as mentioned on their admit card.

Q3. Where can I get my original hardcopy UP Board marksheet?

Original marksheets and passing certificates signed by the board secretary are dispatched to respective schools and junior colleges within 2 to 3 weeks of online result publication.

Q4. What should I do if the official UPMSP website is slow or crashing?

Due to heavy concurrent traffic of over 50 lakh students, servers may load slowly. Avoid refreshing continuously. Wait 5 minutes or use alternative Server 2 or the DigiLocker government mobile app.
