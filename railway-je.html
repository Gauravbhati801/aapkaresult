11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: 1st Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1 - Screening Test)
  2. Stage 2: 2nd Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2 - Core Technical & General Subjects)
  3. Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) in 1:1 ratio based on CBT-2 normalized marks
  4. Stage 4: Rigorous Medical Examination conducted at Railway Hospitals

12. Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

Exam Mode: Two-Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1 & CBT-2) • Duration: CBT-1: 90 Minutes | CBT-2: 120 Minutes • Total Questions: CBT-1: 100 Questions | CBT-2: 150 Questions • Total Marks: CBT-1: 100 Marks | CBT-2: 150 Marks • Negative Marking: 1/3rd Mark deducted for every wrong answer

13. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

📚 CBT-1 Non-Technical Syllabus
Mathematics (BODMAS, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics), General Intelligence & Reasoning (Analogies, Coding, Syllogisms, Series, Venn Diagrams), General Awareness (Current Affairs, Science & Tech, Sports, Polity), General Science (10th standard Physics, Chemistry, Biology).

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Visit the centralized recruitment portal at rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Create an account using your Aadhaar number, active mobile, and personal email.
  3. Step 3: Log in and select the Junior Engineer (CEN) recruitment.
  4. Step 4: Enter Diploma / Engineering Degree details and select your engineering discipline exam group.
  5. Step 5: Select your preferred Railway RRB Zone and order of post preferences.
  6. Step 6: Upload passport photo and signature as per prescribed specifications.
  7. Step 7: Pay the fee (₹500 / ₹250) and provide bank account details for refund.
  8. Step 8: Submit the application and download the application confirmation PDF.

18. Required Documents for Online Registration & Verification

  • Engineering Diploma or B.E. / B.Tech Degree Certificate & Semester Marksheets
  • 10th Class (Matriculation) Certificate (for DOB verification)
  • Aadhaar Card (Mandatory for biometric verification)
  • Bank Account details (Account Number, IFSC) for fee refund
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL) in prescribed Central Railway format
  • Recent passport-size color photograph and scanned signature

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • A candidate can apply to only ONE Railway Zone. Applying to multiple RRBs results in permanent disqualification.
  • The choice of exam group for CBT-2 must correspond strictly to your engineering discipline.
  • Biometric Aadhaar authentication is conducted at the exam centers.
  • Marks scored in CBT-1 are purely screening; the final merit list is prepared strictly based on CBT-2 normalized marks.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can final semester engineering students apply for RRB JE?

No. Candidates must possess the prescribed educational qualification (Diploma or Degree) on or before the closing date of application submission. Candidates awaiting final results are not eligible.

Q2. Is there any interview for the RRB JE post?

No. There is no interview for Junior Engineer posts. Selection is determined entirely by CBT-2 marks followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Q3. Can B.E. / B.Tech graduates apply for RRB Junior Engineer?

Yes. Candidates holding a B.E. / B.Tech degree in relevant engineering disciplines are fully eligible to apply alongside Diploma holders.
