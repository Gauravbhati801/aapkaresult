11. Selection Process & Stages

  1. Stage 1: Tier-I Computer Based Examination (Objective MCQ - Qualifying in Nature)
  2. Stage 2: Tier-II Computer Based Examination (Paper-I Compulsory for all posts + DEST Data Entry Speed Test)
  3. Stage 3: Paper-II for Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) candidates
  4. Stage 4: Post Preference Submission & Document Verification by Indenting Departments
  5. Stage 5: Final All-India Merit List based on Tier-II Paper-I marks

12. Examination Pattern & Marking Scheme

Exam Mode: Two-Tier Computer Based Test (CBT) • Duration: Tier-1: 60 Minutes | Tier-2: 2 Hours 15 Minutes • Total Questions: Tier-1: 100 Questions | Tier-2 Paper-1: 130 Questions + 20 Computer Qs + DEST • Total Marks: Tier-1: 200 Marks | Tier-2 Paper-1: 390 Marks (+ 60 Qualifying Computer Marks) • Negative Marking: Tier-1: 0.50 Marks | Tier-2: 1 Mark per incorrect answer

Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Structure, Synonyms, Antonyms, Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Narration, Shuffling of Sentence Parts, Cloze Passage, Reading Comprehension.

History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy & Scientific Research, Current Affairs. Computer: Basics of CPU, Memory, Windows OS, MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, Internet, Web Browsing, Email, Networking & Cyber Security.

17. How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1: Open the official Staff Selection Commission website at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Step 2: Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if applying on the new portal for the first time.
  3. Step 3: Log in using your Registration Number and Password.
  4. Step 4: Click 'Apply' under Combined Graduate Level Examination.
  5. Step 5: Fill in your university, degree details, graduation percentage/CGPA, and select exam cities.
  6. Step 6: Capture a live photograph through the webcam/mobile camera and upload your signature (10KB - 20KB).
  7. Step 7: Pay the application fee (₹100 for Gen/OBC/EWS Male; ₹0 for SC/ST/Female) and submit.
  8. Step 8: Download and retain the printout of the application form.

19. Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Tier-1 examination is now purely qualifying in nature. Final merit is determined strictly on the basis of marks scored in Tier-II Paper-I (390 marks).
  • Qualifying both the Computer Knowledge Module and DEST (Data Entry Speed Test - 2000 key depressions in 15 minutes) is mandatory for final appointment.
  • Candidates must inspect the photo preview to ensure the live capture is sharp and properly illuminated without headgear or spectacles.
  • Post preferences are submitted online after Tier-II results are declared.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is Tier-1 marks counted in the final merit of SSC CGL?

No. Under the revised exam scheme, Tier-1 marks are purely qualifying in nature. The final merit list is prepared strictly based on aggregate marks scored in Tier-II Paper-I (maximum 390 marks).

Q2. Is Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) compulsory for all candidates in SSC CGL?

Yes. Module-II of Section-III in Tier-II (Data Entry Speed Test) is mandatory for all posts. Candidates must achieve a typing speed of 2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes.

Q3. Can appearing final year graduate students apply for SSC CGL?

Yes, candidates appearing in their final year can apply provided they acquire the essential educational qualification (degree completion) on or before the crucial cutoff date specified in the official notification.
