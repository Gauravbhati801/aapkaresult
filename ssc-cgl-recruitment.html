Editorial Deep-Dive: SSC Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination

Verified Research by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk • 2026 Examination & Recruitment Standards

👮 1. Job Profile, Daily Duties & Career Growth Hierarchy

The premier national civil recruitment for Group B and C gazetted and non-gazetted posts: Assistant Section Officer (ASO) in Central Secretariat (CSS) and Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Inspector of Central GST & Excise, Preventive Officer, Enforcement Officer in Enforcement Directorate (ED), and Sub-Inspector in CBI. Offers prestigious administrative careers with policy influence and fast promotions.

💰 2. In-Hand Salary, Grade Pay & Statutory Allowances Breakdown

Posts range across Pay Level-4 (Basic ₹25,500) to Level-8 (Basic ₹47,600). For 4600 Grade Pay posts (Level-7, Basic ₹44,900) in X-Class metropolitan cities (Delhi, Mumbai), gross monthly salary exceeds ₹85,000 with 50% DA, 30% HRA, and Transport Allowance. In-hand monthly salary credited is approximately ₹74,000 to ₹77,000.

📊 3. Previous Cut-Off Trends & Competition Benchmarks

Tier-1 is qualifying (200 marks). Due to intense nationwide competition, qualifying cut-offs for UR hover around 148–154 marks, OBC: 145–151 marks, and EWS: 142–148 marks. In Tier-2 (390 marks in Section I & II), candidates aiming for top 4600 GP posts (ASO, GST Inspector) must target a safe score of 325+ marks.

🎯 4. High-Weightage Preparation Strategy & Syllabus Traps

Tier-2 Section-1 combines Mathematical Abilities (30 Qs, 90 marks) and Reasoning (30 Qs, 90 marks) where candidates must aim for 170+ out of 180 marks. English Language & Comprehension (45 Qs, 135 marks) demands advanced vocabulary, reading speed, and error spotting. Do not neglect Computer Proficiency (20 Qs) and DEST typing test (27 wpm).

📋 5. Document Verification, Crucial Dates & Candidature Advisory

Document Verification is conducted directly by the indenting user departments after final result declaration. Crucial date for OBC-NCL, EWS, and PwBD certificates is strictly the closing date of the online application window. Ensure your degree graduation certificate or provisional degree was conferred prior to the cutoff date.

📈 Cut-Off Trends & Competition Level Breakdown:

Under the revised SSC CGL examination scheme, Tier-1 (200 marks) is strictly qualifying in nature, but scoring safely above the 150+ raw mark benchmark is essential to clear competitive screening. Final selection and departmental cadre allotment are governed exclusively by performance in Tier-2 Section-I and Section-II (comprising 130 questions for 390 total marks). The negative marking penalty in Tier-2 is severe: 1 mark deducted for every incorrect response (one-third penalty). Aspirants aiming for premier Grade Pay 4600/4200 posts (Assistant Section Officer in CSS or MEA, Inspector of Central GST & Excise, Preventive Officer, and Enforcement Officer) must target a composite score of 330+ marks out of 390. Furthermore, failing the Computer Proficiency Module or DEST typing test (27 WPM with under 5% errors) immediately disqualifies your candidature regardless of high Tier-2 marks.

Subject-Wise Preparation Strategy & High-Weightage Topics

Tailored Study Roadmap by AapkaResult.in Mentorship Desk

Mastering SSC CGL Tier-2 requires disciplined execution across the four main evaluated modules: • Mathematical Abilities (30 Questions / 90 Marks): Focus on Arithmetic and Advanced Mathematics in equal measure. Prioritize Geometry theorems, Trigonometric identities, Mensuration 3D, Coordinate Geometry, Number Systems, and newly integrated chapters like Probability and Basic Statistics. Target 28+ correct answers. • Reasoning & General Intelligence (30 Questions / 90 Marks): Critical reasoning questions (Statement-Inference, Cause-Effect, Course of Action) now feature heavily alongside traditional non-verbal, coding, and matrix puzzles. Target 28+ correct answers. • English Language & Comprehension (45 Questions / 135 Marks): The largest scoring component. Read editorial articles daily to build rapid comprehension for Reading Passages and Cloze Tests. Master Active/Passive voice, Direct/Indirect speech, and advanced Vocabulary (Idioms, Phrasal Verbs). Target 41+ correct. • General Awareness (25 Questions / 75 Marks): Focus on NCERT-based Indian Polity, Modern History, Macroeconomics, and recent government schemes. Computer Module: Solve 20 PYQ quizzes on MS Office 365, networking, and keyboard shortcuts.

26. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is Tier-1 marks counted in the final merit of SSC CGL?

No. Under the revised exam scheme, Tier-1 marks are purely qualifying in nature. The final merit list is prepared strictly based on aggregate marks scored in Tier-II Paper-I (maximum 390 marks).

Q2. Is Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) compulsory for all candidates in SSC CGL?

Yes. Module-II of Section-III in Tier-II (Data Entry Speed Test) is mandatory for all posts. Candidates must achieve a typing speed of 2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes.

Q3. Can appearing final year graduate students apply for SSC CGL?

Yes, candidates appearing in their final year can apply provided they acquire the essential educational qualification (degree completion) on or before the crucial cutoff date specified in the official notification.
